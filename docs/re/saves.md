# Saves

What the original's save holds, and when it saves and loads (read 2026-11-03 for #1956, a part of #1095; **confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise).
The game does not load the original's saves (PLAN.md §1.1, ADR-08): this is what its own saves must give back.
The saves' window is in docs/re/menus.md, "The saves' window"; the driver's view of it and the keys are in docs/re/window.md ("The menus that the driver clicks", the keys of `cEngine_receiveEvent`); the exported heroes `save/hero*.pax` are not saves (docs/02 §9, docs/re/menus.md).
Implemented in `opensacred-game` (`saves`, `saves_window`, `save_picture`, `npcs`, `scripts`, `seed`, `points`) and `opensacred-rules` (`quests`).

## The file

`cEngine::save` (`0x619BF0`) writes one file, `./SAVE/GAME%.2d.PAK` by the save's slot (the strings also name `GAMEF%d.PAK` and `SAVE02.PAK`, not read), into `.\Save`, which it makes where it is missing.
It builds the whole file in memory (a `cFile_ram` of 8 MiB) and writes it at once; a save that cannot have the memory logs `---SAVING FAILED` and writes nothing.
While it saves, the game's timer stands (`0x6413E0`), and a loading screen shows `PAK\SAVEGAME.BMP` with a line for each part as it is written.

Before it saves, it compares a few of the hero's values (three numbers at its `+0x3B4`, `+0x3EE` and `+0x3FE`, its attributes' bases and bonuses, its skills' levels) with a copy kept in a write-protected page, and where they differ it marks the hero a cheater and takes those values away (`Cheater detected`); the game has no such check (its console and its commands change the hero openly).

The file begins with a header of 256 bytes, then a table of its parts, 64 entries of 12 bytes (the part's id, its offset and its size; the header's `+4` holds 64), then the parts (corrected 2026-11-04 for #1990 and #1991, **confirmed** by code and seen in the original's files):

- **the header**: the letters `AMS`, the version 27 (`---SAVING (%c%c%c) v%d #%d`), the hero's type (`+0x14`, 1 the Seraphim) and a colour of it (`+0x18`, `0x43C250`), the campaign (`+0x24`: 2 Ancaria, 3 the Underworld, `0x182EE48`), whether the game is a network one (`+0x2C`, `0x182EBEC`), the difficulty (`0x182EE44`), the hero's level reached on each of the five difficulties, at `+0x44` the whole seconds of the statistics' survival counter `+0x570C` (`0x424650` at `0x61A5E1`, which the load gives back times 1,000, `0x424670` at `0x61BB3D`; docs/re/questbook.md, "The statistics' book"; read 2026-11-25 for #3749, **confirmed** by code and **seen**), the game's calendar (its day, hour and minute), the computer's date and time (`+0x5C` to `+0x78`, a `SYSTEMTIME` as words of four bytes), `0xBAADF00D` at `+0x7C`, and the sums of the bytes after it;
- **a descriptor** (`0x95`, 256 bytes of UTF-16): the line `Date[dd/mm/yyyy] Time[hh.mm]` of the game's calendar and a line feed, which the saves' list shows where the header holds no computer's date;
- the save's **name** (`0x83`, 64 bytes), which the saves' window, the quick save (`QUICKSAVE`) and the autosave (its number's text) give;
- **the engine** (`0x80`): its flags word `+0x54` (docs/re/drawing.md, "The view's point"), three words, `+0x5C` (not read) and the right button's art and its kind (`+0x5A`, `+0x58`; docs/re/arts.md), and `+0x13C`, the object that the player's clicks move, the hero or the horse it rides (docs/re/horses.md, "The control and the gait") (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code);
- **the mouse** (`0x98`): written only while an item is on the cursor (`cMouse +0x68`, `0x655550`), 32 bytes that hold its record of 20, two words and four longs (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code); a save made with an item on the cursor puts it back on the cursor as it loads, in a new run of the game too, the backpack without it (**seen** 2026-11-18, #2822);
- **the triggers** (`0x81`): the world's sectors (`0x63B580`, the same function loads them: `cWorld::load() sectors: #world[%d] #saved[%d]`, the sectors of the world and those it keeps a state of; each sector's record not read);
- **the calendar** (`0x8B`): the day and the time;
- **the blood** (`0x94`, `0x403EA0`), so named by its line on the loading screen: the renderer's (`cGranny`, `0x401660`) 32 slots of vertex colours, each a count and up to 4,097 colours (`0xA23FC0`, `0x80100` bytes), and the mask of the slots in use (`0xAA42C0`), which the creatures' update fades toward white (`0x402490` from `0x5375C0`) and the effects' end, the rise and the Vampiress's change of form free (`0x402470`) (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code; **hypothesis**: the tints that the creatures' effects give their models, not the blood's stains on the ground);
- **the regions** (`0x99`, `0x61D450`, `0x61DE30`): the areas' table whole, its 256 entries of 24 bytes (docs/re/inventory.md, docs/re/sound.md), the mark of an area pacified (`+2`) and of a region run (`+8`, docs/re/spawning.md) among them;
- **the sides** (`0x9A`, `0x422820`, `0x423960`): the sides' table, 256 bytes that tell whether each of 16 sides is hostile to each (`sdk_SCRAMBLED_MATRIX`, which the engine's start copies from the built-in one, `0x4227C0`, and which only the horse theft's effect changes, for its 25 s, `0x423430`; docs/re/horses.md, "The horse window"), and the 16 players' team words, 64 bytes (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code; the explored land, its hypothesis before, is in the world's sectors' part `0x81`, each sector's bitmap at its `+0x180`, docs/re/maps.md);
- **the items** (`0x8E`): every object of the object manager (`0x602300`; `cObjectManager::save() Ref[%3d] Fam[%2d] wp[%d,%d,%d]`, each with its family and its place in the world), the creatures among them (`cCreature::save`), so the world's creatures, alive and dead, and the items lying on the ground;
- **the inventory** (`0x8D`): the hero's items (`0x5DEBC0` with the hero; `ItemDataMgr::saveHero`, each slot's item and the hero's place `wpos` and portals);
- **the particles** (`0x96`): the particle systems that live, the spells' and the arts' objects among them (`0x7CC3B0`, read 2026-11-22 for #3170, **confirmed** by code: each system's slot, type and particles' count, then its class's own record, which writes its own fields and then the base's, its place, its points, its particles and its flags; the load makes each anew in its slot and reads its record back; docs/re/effects.md, "In `opensacred-game`");
- **the views** (`0x82`): the engine's world views, not its players, as this note read before: the index of the current one (`+0x48`), then for each of the 16 slots at `+0x08` a byte whether a view is there and that view's own record (`cWorldView0`'s virtual `+0x1C`, `0x632BB0`, through `0x6288F0`): its flags `+0x10`, its current point `+0x14` and `+0x18`, the point asked `+0x1C` and `+0x20`, its offset `+0x24` and `+0x28`, `+0x2C`, and its zoom `+0xB60` (docs/re/drawing.md, "The view's point"; read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code);
- **the weather** (`0x92`, `0x41BBA0`);
- **the quests** (`0x91`): the scripts' state (`sdk_SAVELOAD_SERIAL`: the quests, their flags, the variables, the timers);
- **the picture** (`0x93`): the frame shown in the saves' window ("The picture", below), listed 0x6000 bytes long in the table but written 0x8000 long and followed by `0xDEADC0DE`;
- **the stats** (`0x9B`, `0x424E10`): the player's statistics object (`0x425290`, at `0xAA5598`; docs/re/questbook.md, "The statistics' book"): its 768 records by type, the ten types of the most kills, the 16 latest kills, the levels' records and their count, the sectors visited, the kills, the resurrections, the hours, minutes and milliseconds played (`+0x56F8` to `+0x5700`), the wins of each of six difficulties (`+0x5704`, a byte each, `0x424CF0`), a word `+0x570A` (not read), the runes of the class that the quests gave (`+0x5710`, `0x424690`; docs/re/netscript.md), and the hero's quest bits of each of five difficulties (`+0x5724`, 160 bits each, `HeroQBit`, `0x424DA0`); not `+0x570C`, the survival counter, whose whole seconds the header keeps instead (above), nor the marks of the last two minutes' event (`+0x5714` to `+0x571C`, `0x424A60`), and not the regions' counts, which lie after the object (from `0xAAAD58`; docs/re/maps.md) (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code);
- **the interface** (`0x9C`): whether the quest book (the interface manager's `+0xA8`, `0x75A970`) and the inventory (`+0x84`, `0x75A990`) show, a byte each in 64; and `0x9D`, 1 KiB of zeros (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code and **seen**, "What a load puts back" below);
- **the hero** (`0xC3`, 556 bytes): the hero's name and the values that the saves' list and the choice of a campaign's hero show (`0x604300`).

The load (`0x61B6F0`, `---LOADING SAVEGAME.PAK`) refuses a save of another version (`savegame v%d incompatible (need version %d)`) and logs the save's language beside the game's; the test admits the versions 21 to 27, the current one, unless a flag of `0x182EE38` (`0x4000000`) admits any (read 2026-11-25 for #3739, **confirmed** by code).

So the original's save keeps the whole world as it stands: every creature and item of the object manager where it is and as it acts, the sectors' states, the models' tints, the particle systems, the weather, the calendar and the scripts' state, beside the hero, the camera's views and two of the interface's windows ("What a load puts back" below).
Which objects the object manager keeps and whether a creature killed far away stays dead after a load: below, "A creature killed far away" (read and **seen** 2026-11-25 for #3750).
Read 2026-11-22 for #3175 (**confirmed** by code): the object manager writes every object of its table by its own virtual save (`0x602300`), those destroyed among them with their mark `+0x14` bit `0x10`, which its load puts back out of the world (`0x602D10`); the world writes every sector's record (`0x63B580`, `0x623240`): its flags word `+0x30`, 128 bytes at `+0x180`, `+0x200`, its list of 76-byte entries at `+0x204`, and the kept creatures of its fills with the time since they were kept, where that is below a bound.
The flags word's bit `0x800`, a sector whose `Enter` has run since it came into the 3 × 3, comes back with it, and the walk of the 3 × 3 runs a sector's `Enter` only where the bit is clear (`0x6261A0`), so a load runs no `Enter` of the sectors that were about the hero at the save: their `Enter`s' creatures and objects come back from the save as they stood, the scripts' traps among them (docs/re/effects.md, "The scripts' traps").
**Seen** 2026-11-22 in the running original (#3443): a save in the trap cave and its load kept three eaten pills eaten, the load's log rebuilding the field's 221 others, its ghosts and the field among the particle systems, and a save and a load by the Gladiator's quest 30 brought its two gold arrows back where they stood (docs/re/effects.md, "The scripts' traps" and "In `opensacred-game`").
The driver's `load` from within a game opened the saves' window but took the wrong row three times, loading another save or none while it reported that the load did not start; the loads were made by hand in the saves' window (the menu over the game, Save Game, the row's picture, Load, OK).
Read 2026-11-23 for #3492: the driver counted the quick save `GAME00.PAK` among the list's rows, which the window shows apart above them (docs/re/menus.md, "The saves' window"), so that every row it chose was the next save's, and the last save's the empty row, which loads nothing (its `save` over a save of the name it was given wrote into the next save alike); and it took a load as started only once the engine's loading flag showed, which a load from the main menu never shows (**seen**: the driver's `load` of the slot 13 loaded the slot 14 and reported that the load did not start).

## What a load puts back

Read 2026-11-25 for #3739 (**confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise) and **seen** the same day in the running original (the driver of #448; a Seraphim's new game at the start; a probe of the view's follow at `0x6103D2`, where `EDI` holds the controlled object and `ESI` the engine; three runs of the game, their frames kept in the scratchpad).

The load reads the parts in the table's order, each by the function that wrote it, and then sets the hero up again:

- **The engine** (`0x80`): it keeps the running flags word `+0x54`, the save's read and dropped, and clears its bits `0x3C0`, the requests to save and to load among them; outside a network game it takes back `+0x5C`, the right button's art (`+0x5A`, `+0x58`) and the controlled object (`+0x13C`).
- **The views** (`0x82`): it sets the current view's index to none (16), makes a view in each slot that the save holds and that lacks one (`0x6100D0`) and has it read its record, destroys the view of a slot that the save holds none of (`0x610180`), and then makes the saved index current (`0x610210`).
  A view's record puts back its points, its offset and its zoom (`0x6288F0`), sets the device's projection by the zoom as the zoom's setter does, `ortho(±267·z, ±200·z, −600, 2500)`, and posts the interface the zoom's message (9, `0x15`, the zoom times 100), as `cWorldView_setZoom` (`0x628130`) does.
  It holds the zoom read within 0.5 and 2 and then writes the value read back over it (`0x628B4B`–`0x628B8E`), so the bounds hold nothing; as the setters keep the zoom within them, no save holds one outside them.
  The change of the current view carries over the offset of the view current before it, and there is none, so a loaded game's view has the offset (0, 0) whatever the save held, as docs/re/drawing.md, "The view's point" saw, until the inventory's show sets it again (below).
  A new game's engine makes its first view at the zoom 1 (`0x6100D0` with 1.0, from `cEngine_ctor` through `0x60DB60` at `0x60DC03`, and at `0x60B133` in a network game), and no key of `Settings.cfg` holds a zoom (no string of `Sacred.exe` names one): so a loaded game's zoom is its save's, and a new game's is 1.
- **The objects** (`0x8E`): each reads its own record, each creature by the one below, so that each comes back as it stood and as it acted.
- **The mouse** (`0x98`): the record goes back onto the cursor (`0x655580`); after a save without the part, what the cursor held is dropped (`0x6556F0`).
- **The interface** (`0x9C`): the quest book (`UI_WND_QUESTBOOK`) and the inventory (`UI_WND_INVENTORY`) are each shown (the window's event `0x11`) or hidden (`0x12`) through the kernel as the save had them, and the manager is sent the event `0x3F` of `UI_TAB_MR_LOTTO` with 1 (not read).
- **The hero**, after the parts: the interface is sent the hero's type and id (its event `0xC` with 3), the hero takes its own events `0x16` and `0x17` (not read), each of its 19 slots whose item is gone is emptied (`0x556A30`), each item kept loses the mark of a destroyed object (`+0x14` bit `0x10`), a hero of the type 7 takes one more event (not read), the statistics' marks of the two minutes' event are set to the time played (`0x423FD0`), and the copy of the hero's values that the save's test of cheats compares is made anew.

### A creature's record

`cCreature`'s virtual `+0x1C` (`0x54B3D0`), which the hero's file's creature `0xC7` holds too (docs/02 §9), writes and reads:

- the object's base (`cObject`, `0x449AA0`): its id, type, flags `+0x14`, sector, place (`+0x1C`, `+0x20`) and floor among its first 64 bytes;
- its model's (`cObject3D`, `0x44FC50`): its fields from `+0x5C` to `+0xF3`, its direction `+0x70` among them, the vector by which its model turns (docs/re/drawing.md), the slot in play `+0x64`, and its clip's speed, loops and start on Granny's clock (Granny's parameters 2, 3 and 0 of the control `+0x48`, the start less the renderer's clock, which answers 0, `0x401700`); the load clears the control first, so that it sets the three on no control, and then plays the slot in play anew ("What a load gives back of the doings" below, read 2026-11-25 for #3754, **confirmed** by code and **seen**);
- the AI's state and sub-state (`+0xFC`, `+0xFE`) with its order's block to `+0x14F`, and the action and its sub-state (`+0x150`, `+0x152`) with the action's block to `+0x1A3`, its point (`+0x158`, `+0x15C`), floor, distance to stop and target among it (docs/re/ai.md, "Two states");
- its 19 equipment slots (`+0x1A4`), a slot whose object's type has the bit 25 of its first word (`0x5FE790`, `0x426550`; the classes' defaults, docs/re/equipment.md) written 0, the slot 18 (`+0x1EC`) the link of a rider and its horse (docs/re/horses.md, "Mounting");
- 253 bytes from `+0x1F0`, its side, its flags word `+0x1F4`, its gait `+0x1F8`, `+0x200`, its group, its master, its shots' chain and its knock-downs' marks among them (docs/re/fight.md);
- 173 bytes from `+0x2ED`, its way: the points, the segment walked (`+0x36D`), their count (`+0x371`), the speed (`+0x375`) and the search's target (`+0x391`, `+0x395`) (docs/re/movement.md, "The way's points and the walk along them");
- `+0x570`, a horse's owner, and the values at `+0x574` and `+0x578`; its stats (`0x585260`, docs/re/character.md); its followers' list (`+0x39C`, records of 9 bytes); its scripts' queue (`+0x588`, records of `0x44` bytes, read back as written, docs/re/netscript.md); and, where its `+0x200` has the bit `0x40` (the heroes'), the hero's list of hostiles (`0xAD3CE0`, 55 records of 8 bytes, and their count `0xAD3BC8`; docs/re/arts.md, "A combo's run"), which the scripts' data was taken for before (read 2026-11-25 for #3754, **confirmed** by code).

So a creature, the hero among them, comes back facing as it faced, in the same AI's state and action with their targets and points, on the same way at the same gait, its links to its master, its followers, its horse or its rider as they were.
The model's part comes first: the slot's replay runs before the record's later parts are read, on the creature as its constructor left it (its AI's state and action 0, its clips' factor `+0x2C9` 1.0, `0x52DFBC`), so that a dead creature's replay is not held back (below).

## What a load gives back of the doings

Read 2026-11-25 for #3754 (**confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise) and **seen** where marked the same day in the running original (the driver of #448, a Seraphim's new game at the start, the god mode, goblin warriors 76 made by the driver beside her, traces of `0x5930B0` and of the strikes, the probe of the arrows' start `0x5BD41B`, frames in the scratchpad).

**The clips.**
The model's part of a creature's record (`0x44FC50`) clears its control `+0x48` before it reads the clip's speed, loops and start, so that it sets them on no control, and then, where the slot in play `+0x64` is not 0 and the model is made (its virtual `+0x28` with 1), plays that slot anew through its virtual `+0x2C` (the creature's `0x5930B0`, which calls `0x44A6C0`) with the loops read and the speed read as the rate.
So no clip goes on from its point: each starts again from its beginning at the load's moment on Granny's clock, which no save holds.
`0x44A6C0` gives the rate to `0x413610`, which multiplies it by `GameSpeed` (1.25) and the slot's own factor as for any clip (docs/re/motions.md), while the speed read already holds them: a clip in play at a save plays again `GameSpeed` times the own factor faster after the load, until the creature asks for its next clip.
**Seen**: at a quick load the hero's replay was asked for her strike's slot 84 with the loops 1 and the rate 1.925, her strike's speed of 1.25 · 1.54 (docs/re/fight.md, "The archers"), so played at 2.41; a goblin's corpse's for its dying slot 228 with the loops 1 and the rate 1.25.
The replay comes before the record's AI's and action's blocks are read (above, "A creature's record"), so the test of `0x5930B0` that keeps a dead or dying creature (the AI's state 9 or the action 6) from any slot but its dying ones finds the constructor's 0: a corpse plays its dying clip again from its start and lies again at its end.
**Seen**: a goblin lying dead on the bridge at the start was up and falling 0.5 s after a quick load and lay as before 1.1 s after it.
A blend under way at a save (`+0x22D` to `+0x244`, the new control's handle among them) is read back with the 253 bytes from `+0x1F0` after the replay, a handle of the run that saved (**hypothesis**: rare, a blend lasting at most 0.2 s, and not seen).

**A strike or a cast under way.**
The action's block (`+0x150` to `+0x1A3`) comes back whole: its sub-state, its target, its ending flag `+0x16C`, which an art sets once it has acted (docs/re/arts.md, "The special weapon arts"), the shot's counter `+0x178` and the arts' counts of their hit points.
The action reads the clip in play anew at each tick (docs/re/fight.md, "The archers"), so a strike or a shot saved before its hit point hits once, at that point of the replayed clip, and one saved after it does not hit again; a cast saved before it acted acts once, at its share of the replayed clip, and one saved after it ends at its next run (**hypothesis** for the casts, by the same reading; not seen).
**Seen**: a quick save 0.35 s after a click on a goblin, the hero's strike not landed yet; at F8 the strike landed 0.37 s after the load's end, once, with no click, and her stance's clip followed.

**Arrows and bolts in flight.**
An arrow (`cArrow3D`, its save `0x5BDAF0`) keeps its base object, its flags `+0x1E4`, its direction `+0x1E8`, its point `+0x1F4`, seven more of its fields from `+0x200` to `+0x24C` (`+0x200`, `+0x204`, `+0x208`, `+0x20C`, 32 bytes from `+0x214`, `+0x234`, `+0x24C`) and a stamp of the game's clock `+0x23C`, which the load sets to the load's clock (`0xCD7A60`).
An FX object (`cObjectFX`, `0x5B6A50`) keeps its fields from `+0x44`: its kind, flags, caster, target, followed object and bone, the index of its particle system `+0x6E`, its direction `+0x78`, its turn, its offset, its timers `+0xA8` to `+0xB4`, its points, its array `+0xE4`, its cooldown `+0x11C` and its children's list `+0xD0`, and its stamp `+0xA4`, set to the load's clock too; its particle system comes back in the same slot by the particles' part (`0x96`; docs/re/effects.md, "The bolts").
So a bolt or an arrow flies on from where it was, its first step after the load of no more than a frame's time.
**Seen**: a quick save 29 ms after the hero's arrow left her bow (the probe of `0x5BD41B`), aimed at the ground by a Ctrl click; at F8 no arrow was made anew (no reach of `0x5BD41B`) and 0.57 s after the load's end an arrow of the hero's struck the creature beside her, the save's arrow come back.

**The combo's step.**
`+0x2B4` holds the running combo's index (and the 1 that marks it running) and `+0x2B3` its step, both in the 253 bytes from `+0x1F0`; the mark `+0x401` is the stats' `+0x59`, which the stats keep (docs/re/arts.md, "A combo's run").
So a combo comes back at its step: the runner (`0x4FE870`) gives the next step once less than 0.3 of the step's art's clip is left, which the load has replayed from its start, so the step runs whole again before the next.
The creature and point of the order, which the first step takes (`0xAD3EA8` to `0xAD3EB4`, written at `0x530256` and read at `0x542BBE` alone), are globals of no part: a combo saved after its order and before its first step gives that step to what they hold, nothing in a new run (**hypothesis**: a tick's window, not seen).

**The creatures outside the 3 × 3.**
The object manager writes every object of its table (`0x602300`), so its save holds every creature and thing that the sectors' `Init`s and the start script made, wherever they stand, beside those of the 3 × 3's `Enter`s and fills, the items lying and the effects; the `Enter`s' creatures of the sectors that left the 3 × 3 are not in it, their exits having destroyed them (docs/re/spawning.md, "The sectors' functions").
The load makes each object anew and reads its record (`0x602D10`), then sends each creature the event `0x10F`, which empties a hero's slots that name no item and makes its models again (`0x530609`).
A creature's update in a sector without the bit `0x800`, the 3 × 3's, takes it off the timer and out of its cell (`0x5379A0` to `0x537A64`, `Creature unregistered in Sector(%d,%d)`), unless it stands within 6,900 of the hero or follows a player, and goes on with that one update; so a far creature updates once after a load, as one that wandered out of the 3 × 3 does, and stands until its sector comes in (**hypothesis**: that the objects the load makes are on the timer, as new objects are).
Awake, every creature farther than 1,500 from the hero goes dormant at its update (docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake"), so none of them pursues or casts.

**A creature's awake timer.**
The timer's accumulator `+0x208` and its period `+0x204` are among the 253 bytes from `+0x1F0` and come back as they were, with the AI's state and its sub-state, dormant or awake (`+0xFC`, `+0xFE`): the next firing comes after the rest of the period, as without the save.

**`rand()`.**
No part of the save writes the generator's seed, the C runtime's per thread (its data's `+0x14`, `0x849B33`); the engine's making seeds it with the computer's time (`srand(time(0))` at `0x60ACA9` in `cEngine_ctor`), which a new game and a load from the main menu make, and a quick load leaves it as it runs.

**Sounds at a load.**
The load makes each particle system anew (`0x7CC3B0`, `0x7C7CB0`) and reads its record by its class's virtual `+0x18`; each class's loop is started by its start (`+0x10`), the one place that writes its handle `+0xB0` (a scan of the code: 24 starts, no read), and the constructor sets it to none (`0x7619CC`), so no effect's loop sounds after a load; and no FX event is sent at a load, so no start's sound plays (`0x6770E0` with 6 in the event's handler, docs/re/effects.md, "The FX event").
The clips replayed at a load play no sound of their own beyond what their frames give as they play (**hypothesis**: the steps' and the strikes' sounds as at any clip).

**The dying and the dead.**
A corpse comes back with its object, its AI's state 9 and its action 6 and their sub-states, so that it lies where it fell and its end goes on (docs/re/fight.md, "The bodies' end"), its dying clip played again first (above, "The clips"); a dying creature takes its death's steps up again at its sub-state, its clip from its start; a destroyed corpse comes back out of the world with its mark `+0x14` bit `0x10` (`0x602D10`).

**The clocks.**
The game's clock (`m_SimSec`, the double at `0xCD7A60`, which runs while the time is not standing) is held by no part: the engine's making sets it to 0 (`0x60AF4D`, logged `cEngine: m_SimSec`), so a load from the main menu starts it at 0, and a quick load (the render thread, `0x60E5ED`) leaves it as it runs; the records stamped with it, the arrows' `+0x23C` and the FX objects' `+0xA4`, take the load's clock as they are read.
The real clock (the performance counter's count `0x6413E0 +0x10` times 1,000 over its frequency `+0x20`, milliseconds), which runs since the computer's start, is held by no part either: a sector's fills' time (`+0x59C`) is saved as its age and set again from the load's clock (`0x623240`, docs/re/spawning.md, "The sectors' functions"); a caster's deadline (`+0x24D`, whole seconds of that clock, docs/re/ai.md, "The creatures' spells") and a script's queued wait of the condition `0x5F` (docs/re/netscript.md) are read back as written, so that after a load they have passed by the time since the save, or lie in the future after a new start of the computer.
Neither a save nor a load holds the render's frame time back: the first frame after either takes its time, so that the time played and the survival counter take the load's time in (**seen**: 2.2 s at a quick load, docs/re/questbook.md, "The statistics' book"), and the world's tick follows at once (docs/re/ai.md, "The world's tick").

**A passing sight.**
A level-up's sparks are a system 856 of the variant 2 with its FX object (docs/re/effects.md, "In `opensacred-game`"): the particles' part keeps it with every particle, and the load makes it again in its slot, so the sparks come back where they were and live out their life.

## When it saves

- **Save Game** in the menu over a game opens the saves' list (`UI_WND_SAVEGAME`), which saves into the row chosen, its name typed into the row (docs/re/window.md); it asks before it overwrites a save.
- **The quick save** (F9, and Control+Z): where the interface allows it (`0x75ADB0`, false while a merchant's, a smith's, a combat master's or a stash's window is open, and while a tooltip's window shows, a dialogue's among them: docs/re/blacksmith.md, "The Dwarf's smithing"; **seen** 2026-11-18, docs/re/netscript.md, "The talk's own end, and the world meanwhile"), `cEngine_receiveEvent` takes the frame for the save's picture (`cEngine_captureInternal`), names the save, sets its slot 0 (`+0x68`) and the engine's request to save (its `+0x54` bit `0x40`), which the engine's loop carries out (read 2026-11-04, #1988).
  F9 (the game's key code `0x117`) names it `QUICKSAVE` (`0x618E3A`), Control+Z (its character `0x1A`) by the text `UI_QUICKSAVE`.
  F8 (`0x116`) and Control+X (`0x18`) set the slot 0 and the request to load (`+0x54` bit `0x200`) outside a network game: the quick save is the slot 0's file, `GAME00.PAK`, which both load.
- **The autosave**: the engine's event `(0xF, 2)` with the save's slot (`+0x0C`), a number (`+0x10`), whether to take the picture's frame (`+0x14`), whether the interface must allow it (`+0x18`) and a name (`+0x20`) (`0x618FA0`): with the test asked and the interface not allowing a save it is refused with `AUTOSAVE [not allowed now!]`; a name given replaces the save's, the slot −1 takes the first free slot (`0x619B80`: the first `SAVE/GAME%.2d.PAK` from 1 that does not open, the slot 511 where 1 to 509 are all taken), and the number goes to the engine's `+0xAC` before the request to save; `(0xF, 3)` takes the picture's frame alone, and `(0xF, 1)` asks to load the slot.
  The options hold an autosave's switch (`UI_CFG_AUTOSAVE`, `UI_BTN_AUTOSAVE`, `AUTOSAVE` of `Settings.cfg`, which `0x81B6F0` reads): where the file lacks it, the function writes it 0 (`%d` of 0, `0x662DF0`) and answers yes that once, so that a fresh installation's first test autosaves and every later one, the setting 0, does not; the reference installation's file holds 0.
  The scripts post it, not a timer (#1961): the record `AutoSave` (121, `0x4AC740`) takes its number (a field of its tags `0x16`, `0x1D` or `0x1E`, the last read) and, where the number is not 0, outside a network game (`0x182EBEC`) and with `AUTOSAVE` set, posts the engine `(0xF, 3)`, the picture's frame, and then `(0xF, 2)` with the slot −1, the number, no picture, no test of the interface and no name, the second 100 later (**hypothesis**: milliseconds) (read 2026-11-04, #1989).
  `cEngine::save` (`0x619BF0`) names a save whose number is not 0 by the number's text (the game's texts by their numbers, made narrow, 63 characters at most) and clears the number, which the header keeps too: each autosave goes into a slot of its own, named by its quest's text.
  The numbers are 30000 to 30027 (texts of `global.res`); the Seraphim's tree holds 26 records, in the sections of its quests' exits (`QIS_OnExit11`, `QIS_OnExit22`, `QIS_OnExit41`, …) and a few functions of the quests 9, 11, 22, 41 to 45, 50 to 59, 61, 65, 71 to 74, 81 and 670, so that the original autosaves as the main quests are done.
  `AUTOSAVEDELAY` is no key of this release's (no string of `Sacred.exe` names it).

## The picture

Read 2026-11-04 for #1991 (**confirmed** by code; the pictures of three saves decoded and compared with the frames taken just before them in the running original, a mean difference of 0.03 to 0.1 of 255).

- `cEngine_captureInternal` (`0x613670`) locks the 1024 × 768 back buffer, copies it to the engine's `+0x1A0`, makes it ARGB4444 of alpha 15 in place (from 32 bits each channel's top four bits, `0x6609A0`; from 16 bits through 555, `0x660940`, `0x660A60`) and scales it to 128 × 96 into `+0x1A4` (`0x660AD0`, its filter on), 0x8000 bytes.
- The scale: the pixel (x, y) is each channel's whole mean of the four bits of the screen's pixels at (8x − 8, 8y − 8), (8x, 8y − 8), (8x − 8, 8y) and (8x, 8y), those left of or above the screen left out: a sparse sample rather than a box, about a picture's pixel right of and below a centred one, of the whole screen, the interface with it.
- When: the engine's `(0x13, 5, 1)` (`0x613370`), which the menu over a game sends as it opens (`0x6BB6C0`, at `0x6BB795`) and the key S before it opens the saves' window; F9 and Control+Z before the quick save; an autosave's `(0xF, 3)` before its save. A save through the saves' window takes none, so it keeps the frame from before Escape or S.
- In the file: little-endian ARGB4444 words, rows from the top, 256 bytes a row.
- The saves' window draws it through one texture of 128 × 128 (`ui_sl_rendertarget.tga`), filled before each row, as a 128 × 96 quad at a texel a pixel (`0x71DE70`, `0x71DFA0`); an empty row's is filled with `0x5555`, a translucent grey.

## For `opensacred-game`

The game saves its own format (ADR-08), and keeps what the original's save keeps, by its own means: the hero (its values, items, arts, combos, skills, effects, place), the world (the creatures alive and dead by their sectors, the items on the ground, the doors, the containers and the hidden stashes, the followers, the town stash, the explored map), the scripts' state, the calendar and the weather, the difficulty and the campaign; what it does not keep yet is listed below ("What the original keeps that the game does not").
#1095, closed, was split into its pull requests on its issue.

Since #1957 the game saves the hero (`saves`): a save is postcard in `saves/<name>.sav` beside the settings' file, its header first (the version, the save's name, the hero's class, name, difficulty and level, the calendar, the computer's time), which the list reads alone, and a save of another version is refused, as the original's load refuses one.
It keeps the hero's place and floor, its experience, attributes and skills, its health, its effects, its arts, art slots and selected art, its combos, its pack (the gold, the backpack, the items worn, the quests' items, the books read), its weapon sets, its home, its statistics, the time played and the calendar.

## What the original keeps that the game does not

Compared 2026-11-25 for #3739 with `opensacred-game`'s save of the version 46 (`saves`: its `Header`, `Hero`, `Scripts` and `World`), by the game's code; each item tells where the original keeps it, how sure that is, and what the game does; those that the game has kept since (#3740 to #3744) are among what both keep, below.

- **The hero's other orders and actions.**
  The original: its AI's state and order (`+0xFC` to `+0x14F`), an attack on a creature, a walk to an item to take, to a creature to speak to or to a horse to mount, a cast or a weapon art ordered, and the action under way, a strike, a shot or a cast, each with its target's id, and a combo's step (`+0x2B3`, `+0x2B4`, in the 253 bytes from `+0x1F0`) (**confirmed** by code; **seen** for a strike, which lands once after the load, "What a load gives back of the doings").
  The game: not kept but for the steps of its scripts' queue (#2890), its shots' chain (#3568) and its knock-downs' marks (#3673); the hero stands, and the player orders it again.
- **The creatures' doings.**
  The original: every creature's record keeps its AI's state and order, its action, its way, its targets and its gait, so that a pursuit, a flight, a cast, a walk or a townsperson's errand goes on after the load (**confirmed** by code; not seen but for the hero).
  The game: a kept creature comes back where it stood with its health and its heading (`npcs::Kept`), a follower beside the hero, each standing and deciding anew; the steps of its scripts' queue are kept (#2890).
- **The clips' phase.**
  The original: each model's clip's start, speed and loops (`0x44FC50`), which the load sets on no control before it plays the slot in play anew from its start, `GameSpeed` times the slot's own factor faster than it played, a corpse's dying clip among them (**confirmed** by code and **seen**, "What a load gives back of the doings").
  The game: every clip begins anew as its creature comes back.
- **The effects in flight.**
  The original: every living particle system with its own record (the part `0x96`, `0x7CC3B0`), and the FX objects, the bolts among them, with the other objects (`0x8E`, `0x602300`; docs/re/effects.md, "In `opensacred-game`"), so that a fireball in flight, a meteor falling or a cast's glow goes on, its stamp of the game's clock set to the load's (**confirmed** by code; **seen** for an arrow in flight and a level-up's sparks, "What a load gives back of the doings").
  The game: the scripts' traps, their fields of pills, their fences of lightning and their gold arrows alone (#3450, #3456); the spells', the arts' and the bolts' effects in flight and the passing sights are left out.
- **The models' tints.**
  The original: the renderer's 32 slots of vertex colours and their mask (`0x94`; **confirmed** by code, **hypothesis** that they are the tints of the creatures' effects).
  The game: none of the renderer's slots; a model's looks come of its effects and forms, which a save keeps (`effects::Effects`).
