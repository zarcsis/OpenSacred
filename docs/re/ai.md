# AI: the creatures while no foe is near

Recovered from the GOG `Sacred.exe` 2.28 with Ghidra 12.1.4 on 2026-10-20 (#232; the townsfolk #241).
Distances are in world units, 53.66563 a cell.
Status: **confirmed** on the code unless marked otherwise.

## Two states

A creature carries two states, each a short with a sub-state after it: its AI's (`+0xFC`, `+0xFE`) and its action's (`+0x150`, `+0x152`), which the creatures' update `0x5375C0` switches on at every world tick (below).
It runs the AI only for a creature whose simulating client (byte `+0x39`) is the local player or none; the creature maker sets it to the local player for every id above 16, so in the single player's game every creature thinks.

- The AI's states: 0 idle (`0x542B20`), 1 (`0x53A170`), 2 a behaviour or a walk ordered (`0x4EE030`), 4 a melee fight (`0x4E0300`), 5 (in the update), 6 (`0x51CFD0`), 8 a fight at range (`0x4DBFC0`), 9 dead (`0x53E7C0`), 10 to 14 (`0x529C60`, `0x4FD0F0`, `0x4EC560`, `0x4EA890`, `0x4EAC80`).
- The actions: 0 standing (`0x544890`), 1 moving (`0x4F4770`), 2 (`0x546690`), …, 8 (`0x4FF0B0`), 10 (`0x506770`), 14 (`0x514790`).

## The world's tick

The creatures' update (`0x5375C0`) runs on a tick of the world, not at every frame (**confirmed** by a probe, #616).
The render loop (`cEngine::renderThread::run`, `0x60E350`) adds each frame's time to the float `0xAD6EFC`; once the sum is above 0.1 s, it calls `0x60DF00` and `0x60F130` with it and sets it to 0.
Observed: the hero's update came every 100 or 117 ms, with its argument 0.1 or 0.117, so every 6 or 7 frames at 60 a second, 108 ms on average.
The hero, two goblins and two horses changed their regeneration's accumulators at the same moments, and the hero's at each of 18 resets of `0xAD6EFC` in 2 s and at no other time.
Which call of `0x60F130` (`sdk_SACRED_KERNEL_GET`, `0x809170`) reaches the updates is not read (#617).
A probe of a hostile goblin coming at the hero and striking (2026-10-23, #617): every change of its action `+0x150` and of its AI state `+0xFC` fell on a tick, at its offset 0, its strikes' starts and ends among them (a strike of 12 ticks, 1.30 s), while its position changed at every frame as it walked; the hero's position too moves at every frame, its action's changes and the mouse's orders on the ticks (docs/re/movement.md, "The hero's orders and its stop"; #640).
So the creatures decide, and take their chances, on the tick; they move and their clips play at every frame.
A probe of the hero's attack (2026-10-23, #617, the hero's AI and action states and its target read at every frame against the ticks, a goblin within its band clicked about every 1.2 s): each of 29 changes fell on a tick.
At a click's tick the hero took the AI's state 4 in its sub-state 0, at the next its sub-state 2, and at the one after the attack action 3 in its sub-state 1, the AI back to 0; the action's sub-state 2, the hit, came 4 ticks into the strike (0.43 s), and the action ended 5 or 6 ticks after that.
So the actions' switches, a strike's hit and its end among them, wait for the tick as the decisions do.
The hero's look (`0x547070`) ran at 53 ticks and at no other frame, 220 to 350 ms apart.
Its timer, as every creature's, is an accumulator (`+0x208`) of the ticks' time against the period (`+0x204`), read in `0x5375C0` on 2026-10-23: once the sum is above the period (not equal to it), the period is taken off it while it stays above, so that it fires once and keeps what is above, which a sum of more than ten periods does not keep; at 7 frames a tick the looks come 3, 3 and then 2 ticks apart.
A dormant creature's own timer that wakes it then takes the jitter off what it kept (`rand() % 31 · 0.1 − 1.5`, "Dormant and awake" below).
`opensacred-game` runs the regeneration on such a tick (`frame::WorldTick`, #616), and the creatures' decisions (#639): the AI's systems (`ai::open`, `think`, `aside`, `behave`, `pursue`) run only when the world ticks, with the tick's time for their timers, and their rolls are a tick's.
The hero's look runs there too (`crowd::look`, #617), its list of hostiles and the creatures' wakes from one timer (`minds::Look`), which keeps its remainder as the creatures' own timers do; the strikes' hits and the shots' arrows wait for the first tick past their clips' fractions (`npcs::strike`, `attack::engage`), and the hero's attack steps as the original's: the click's order at a tick, its decision at the next, and its strike at the one after when it stands in its band.

## Dormant and awake

An idle creature (AI 0) is dormant in its sub-state 0 and looks about in 1; there is no other sleep.
Three things wake it:

- its own timer: an accumulator (`+0x208`) against its period (`+0x204`: 5 s, 2.5 s for a hostile creature of `CreateNPC`), for a creature whose flags word (`+0x1F4`) has the bits `0x3` or `0x408`; each time it fires it is jittered by −1.5 to +1.5 s, so the next comes 3.5 to 6.5 s later;
- `sdk_WAKE` (`0x59F580`), which `CreateNPC` calls for its hostile creatures, so that they look once as they are made (it skips a creature of `+0x200` bit `0x40000`), and which runs a creature's script on sight (`+0x1F4` bit `0x400000`, `CreateNPC`'s tag `0x60`) when the hero comes within 600 × 400 in the light and in a clear line;
- the hero's look about (`0x547070`, every 0.3 s over the 3 × 3 sectors about it, within about 1,875, docs/re/motions.md, "The fight stance"): it wakes the creatures not hostile to the hero at any distance of that range, and those hostile with bit 0 of their flags within 800, or 400 while the hero moves; to every other hostile one in range it gives the idle action (below), at every look in the single player's game; a creature not hostile within 91 of the hero that moves under an order of its own (its action and its AI's state both other than 0), while the hero's AI state is 0, steps aside: it walks to the first of 10 points at random within 200 of itself along each axis that it can reach by a way.
  **Seen** 2026-10-25 (#227, checks 1 and 10): quest 1's novice, walking under `NPC_Goto` (the action 1 with the AI's state 0), stopped at the hero standing in her way and stayed there, never stepping aside (#977); townsfolk walking by their behaviours met the standing hero head-on and went to a point about 4 cells off, or round it.

Awake (`0x542B20`), a creature farther from the hero than 1,500 along the grid's x or 1,000 along its y goes dormant again.
A peaceful one whose flags match `0x1006DCF8` takes its behaviour (AI 2, below).
A hostile one, in one case of six and where its home's cell lacks bit 2 of its flags, walks home when it is more than 200 from it along an axis, else stops; then it weighs its level against the hero's (by the balance's `BaseOffLevel`, `OffLevelProz` and `UnterExpAnteil`, 0.85; a monster far below the hero's level fights only when the hero comes within its reach, docs/re/fight.md): when that test fails and the hero is out of its reach (the mean of two sizes of the types, 70 to 480, 130 by default, `0x4DBC60`), it goes dormant again and in one case of three takes the idle action; otherwise it attacks, the hero its target (`+0x104`), from up to 800.

## The idle action

`0x54EA30` acts only on a creature that stands (AI 0, action 0, `+0x152` below 100), that no one owns and whose `REFKEY` is its type; when its group fights it alerts the group instead ("Groups" below).

- In one case of ten it fidgets: half of those times with one of its model's `*_FUN_IDLE_*` clips (the slots 180 to 192, each tried with a chance of one half; 39 models have one), the other half with its attack's clip.
  Read 2026-11-07 for #1364 (`0x54EA30`, **confirmed** by code): `rand() % 10 == 9` fidgets; the clip is first its attack's, the action 4's slot of `0x5467A0` (79 + 5 × its class), and in one case of two (`rand() % 2 == 0`) the model's slots 180 to 192 that it has, found once and kept as a mask at `+0xF8`, are tried from 180 up, each taken where `rand() % 2` is not 0, the first taken played, the attack's where none is; the standing action (`+0x150` 0) then plays that slot (`+0x154`) once, its sub-state `+0x152` 101 (`0x65`), as a script's `PlayAnim` does, and the idle action passes over it meanwhile.
- Otherwise, in one case of four (22.5 % in all), it takes a step: a creature that an `Init` function or the start script made walks to a point within 150 of itself along each axis, or home in one case of four; one that an `Enter` function made (`+0x14` bit `0x80000000`, `FillSector`'s among them) walks to its place turned about the hero by −30° to +30°, keeping its distance, and its home moves there.
  The step is an order to walk somewhere (the event 4: AI 2, then the moving action), which sets no gait: the moving action walks a creature whose gait (`+0x1F8`) is 0 (**hypothesis**: one left running by a chase runs).
- A hostile creature 800 to 1,875 from the hero so steps about once a second while it stands, and its own timer adds a roll every 5 s or so; farther than 1,500 it stands.
- Its home is where it was made (`+0x2B9` its sector, `+0x2BD` and `+0x2C1` its place, `+0x2C5` its layer), set by the creature maker (`0x480D40`).
- A step of a creature of an `Enter` function in a group (`+0x249`) moves the whole group (`0x54F2AE` to `0x54F725`, read 2026-10-25, #912): `0x4C4F60` copies the group's list of members from the script manager's map (`0xAB4490`), and every member, the stepper among them, is sent its own place moved by the step and `U[−50, 50]` along each axis, its home set within 50 more of that, by the event 4, a member that walks already sent anew; nothing draws a group back to where its fill placed it.
  So with the hero 800 to the look's range off, a pack whose members roll at each look (`1 − 0.775ⁿ` for `n` standing members) walks nearly all the time, to and fro on an arc about the hero at its distance: seen in the original with the thieves of 48,38 (#227, check 7).
  A creature of an `Init` function or the start script sends itself alone.
- The formation of `+0x25F` `0xD160` (`0x54F446` to `0x54F695`, `0x54F8A8` to `0x54FB08`: the first member turns, the others walk to a file behind it) is never reached in GOG 2.28: no instruction stores `0xD160` in `+0x25F`, and the scripts' tag `0x73` sets it to 64 alone.

## The townsfolk

The townsfolk are the creatures of the flags 8 and `0x400` (`CreateNPC`'s tags `0x2B` and `0x42`, `0x4C` as 8, and the `Enter` functions' creatures without flags that the first update does not pass over) and the jugglers of `0x10000000`; their branch of `0x4EF150` starts at `0x4F0A6A`.
They have no timer of their own: most of what they do ends in the AI's state 0, and they decide again when the hero's look wakes them, every 0.3 s within its range.

### Range, dialogue and the far ones

## Groups

`+0x249` is a group's number (5,000 to 9,999 at random, kept in a map of the script manager's, `0x4A9EF0`), not a leader.
A group's creatures do not follow one another: when one of a group of an `Enter` function takes a step, every member walks by the same step (within 50 more) from its own place, its home moving with it.

`+0x255` (`FillSector`'s `36:n`) serves nothing: its one reader is a branch of the alarm that no caller takes.

- **The tags** (`SetNPCState`'s reader `0x48BB40`): `0x37` sets the record's bit `0x100`, `0x4B` `0x300`, `0x71` `0x900`; `0x39` clears `0x300` and sets `0x400`, `0x65` clears `0x200` only and sets `0x400`.
  The bits add up, so a record of `0x37` and `0x4B` hires as `0x4B`, and a `0x65` after a `0x37` leaves the hire standing.
  The number 0 takes the local hero for the master (`0x603E30`); the applier takes the local hero too for a master left empty or the creature itself (`0x462A34`, `0x462A6A`; **hypothesis**: so another number also gives the hero).
- **`SetNPCState`'s hire** (`0x462A1F` to `0x462AAC`, after the flags word, the group and the other tags, before the stats `0x5796A0`): `sdk_HIRE` (`0x54CF70`) by the hero, with the keep byte 1:
  - its level is the greater of the hero's and the middle `lo + (hi − lo)/2` of the sector's range at the hero's place (1 where `hi` is below 2), through the setter without its scaling but with its hold (`0x564D60(L, 1, 0)`);
  - then, kept, it is set again the same way to `max(1, ⌊H·(1 + P/100) + B + 0.5⌋)` of the hero's level `H` (`0x54D14C`, the tables `0xAD5648` and `0xAD5660` by the difficulty; the hero's level on bronze);
  - its master `+0x251` is the hero's id, and its word becomes `(F & 0xEFF62000) | 0x100`: every bit of the low 16 but `0x2000` is gone, so its townsfolk's, guards' and animals' modes go, and so do the boss's `0x80000` and the juggler's `0x10000000`;
  - its side becomes 3 where it was 6, or where it is alive and hostile to the hero (`0x548F30`);
  - it is woken (`sdk_WAKE` `0x59F580`: the AI's state 0 takes the sub-state 1 where it was 0, 0);
  - the record `(id, kind 0, keep 1)` goes into the hero's list (`0x54B200`, skipped if already there), and its worn items (19 slots at `+0x1A4`, those without `+0xB0` bit `0x10`) take `+0xB0` bit 8 and go into its own list as records of kind 100.
- **The meaning of `0x200`**: it only keeps the word's `0x100`, so `0x4B` makes the fighting follower of "Followers"; `0x37` and `0x71` then clear `0x100` and set 4 (`0x462A99`), even when no hero was found.
  `0x71`'s `0x800` marks it in the first quest of the table (`+0x31C`) whose creature list (records of `0x2C` at `+0x1C`) holds it: the quest's `+0x10` goes from bit 1 to bit 2, and the record's `+4` from `0x20` to `0x40` (`0x462AC2`).
- **`CreateNPC`'s** differ (`0x484774`): `0x4B` calls `sdk_HIRE` the same way; `0x37` and `0x71` instead send the creature the event `0x10B` (`+0x14` 4, `+0x18` the given master's id or the local player's).
  The handler (`0x52E7D2`, `0x52EB3E`) sets `+0x251`, adds 4 to its own word, which stays unmasked, wakes it and lists it as `(id, kind 1, keep 1)`; level and side stay as they were.
- **When the flag 4's behaviour runs**: only standing.
  The state 2 passes the flag 4 first, before `0x100`, while the action `+0x150` is 0, and puts a moving one back to the state 0 (`0x4EE030`).
  The state 0's handler gives a standing one (`+0x152` below 100) the state 2 unless its master stands 150 to 240 from it (`0x5445E6`).
  The hired word holds none of the bits `0x1006DCF8` that give the state 2 otherwise (`0x544303`; `0x20000` and `0x40000` would, if kept), so inside that band it does nothing, in danger or not.
  The state 0's dormancy (`0x542CAE`, bits `0x1006DCFB`) passes it by.
- **The behaviour** (`0x4EF150(4)`), with the local hero for its master, in order:
  1. it sets `+0x261` to 1;
  2. with the hero farther than 1,500, it is put near the hero (`0x4EBF20` at `0x4EF2E8`, with no setting's test) and goes on, its distance measured afterwards; a child of the types `0x43F670` goes dormant instead;
  3. with its master gone, it takes the state 0 and its idle clip (`0x4F40A3`);
  4. danger: the hero's count of near hostiles (`0xAD3BC8`) above 0 with the nearest (`0xAD3CE4`) below 300, or the master fighting (the AI's state 4 or 8 with `+0x104`, or the action 3 or 5 or the state 11 or 6 with `+0x158`):
     - a healer (`+0x2B6` 7) skips this test;
     - its master farther than 500, measured from the master, gives it the state 0 for the tick (`0x4F363D`), so it neither follows nor flees while the danger lasts;
     - within 500 it is ordered to flee (`0x539F60`, below), but the follow overwrites that order unless no step-off point is found;
  5. a healer within 350 of a master at two thirds of its health or less casts `HEILKRAFT` (7) at the local hero (the state 6);
  6. the follow (`+0x152` below 100):
     - nearer than 150, it steps off to the first of 10 points 220 from the master, at an angle of `rand() % 361` taken as radians, that its way reaches (`0x4FCC80`); the floor is the ground, or a random one up to its own; it goes at the master's gait `+0x1F8`, the move's mode 3; with no point found it takes the state 0, and a flight already ordered goes on;
     - farther than 240, it walks to the master's place at the master's gait (mode 0);
     - in between, it stands;
  7. it never forgets the hero: the 900 holds only for partners of an id above 16 with a word of `0x1006DD08` (`0x4EF6AF`), and the meeting only for words of `0x8408` (`0x4EF72B`).
- **Moving** (`0x4F4770`), for a follower of `0x104` not in the states 4, 8 or 1 and of a mode other than 3:
  - with its master farther than 270, it is re-aimed at every tick at the master's place, to stop within 200 (`0x4F4F09`: `+0x174` 1, `+0x176` 200);
  - a flag 4's is not re-aimed while the hero is in danger as above (`0x4F4F21`);
  - when its way is recomputed, its gait becomes the master's, or 2 where that is 0 (`0x4F6100`);
  - farther than 1,500 from a player it is put near only with `0x182EE3C` bit `0x10000` (**hypothesis**: a setting); the `0x100` behaviour's put-near beyond 1,500 wants that bit too.
- **Its flight**: a creature coming at the master, within 350, or within 600 with a ranged weapon, a mark of `0x25` or a `+0x2B6`, raises the alert (`0x4E0010`).
  Each flag 4's follower of the master's list within 400 of it and not moving then runs (`0x539F60`): the action 1 at the gait 2, to 700 beyond its own place along the line from the master, on the master's floor, its AI state kept.
  Where the master is nearer than 10 or farther than 1,000, it runs instead to a random point within ±400 of itself along each axis.
  A moving one, or one beyond 400, ends the alert for the rest of the list; the healer's exemption there reads the master's `+0x2B6` (sic, `0x4E0230`).
  The run goes on while the hero is in danger; otherwise the move turns it back once it is farther than 270.
  For a partner that is no hero, the source of `0x539F60` is the creature itself: it runs to a random point within ±400 of itself and takes the state 4 with the sub-state 3.
- **Fights**: it never fights by its own decisions, and the alert gives an event `0x100` only to followers of `0x100`.
  An idle hostile's look picks it only if it fights or holds `+0x1F4` bits `0x50000`, so it is no target of theirs; a hostile's guard behaviour (`0x4EEE40`) may take it as the nearest creature hostile to it within 500 (**hypothesis**).
  Struck, it does not answer.
- **Its death**: at the death's step (`0x53FB40`) its master dismisses it (`0x54D380`: master −1, `+0x1F4 &= 0xDFFFFEFB`, record removed, worn items put back), so a dead hired follower leaves the hero's list.
  With `+0x2B7` bit 8 (`SetNPCState`'s `0x6B` above 0) and a player for its master, it stays and rises 20 s after its death (`0x53E460` at `0x53FBDC`).
  With that bit and no player for its master, the strike's resolution skips its health's update (`0x56F859`; **hypothesis**: unharmed).
- **The hero**:
  - dead, its count of hostiles is 0, so there is no danger, and the follower keeps to the corpse's place;
  - risen, the hero fills every listed creature's health (`0x53E460`'s loop);
  - teleported (`sdk_ENGINE_TP` `0x54D9D0`), every listed follower takes the hero's new place for its home (`+0x2B9`), `+0x2B8` bit 2 and its timer, and is put near the hero, or on the hero's place where no point is found;
  - no code relevels followers at a level-up (**hypothesis**: none among the callers of `0x564D60`).
- **Letting go** (`0x462BB9`, the bit `0x400` without `0x100`):
  1. it calls the quests' hook `0x471790(master, id, 2, +0x94)`: in a quest of the type 1 whose record of the creature has the flag 8, the quest takes `+0x10` bit `0x80`, or, its byte `+9` being 0, goes from the state 2 to 4 through `0x471000` (**hypothesis**: an escort delivered);
  2. it then sends the creature, as controlled by the local player, the event `0x10B` with no master;
  3. the handler (`0x52E702`, `0x52EA6E`) has the master's `sdk_PARTY_REMOVE` drop its record (the last moved into its place) and put its worn items back on (`0x5563D0`), rises it where it has `+0x2B7` bit 8, sets its master to −1 and clears `+0x1F4` bits 4 and `0x100`;
  4. last, `0x54E6D0(1)` stops it if it acts or is out of the state 0 and is alive: the action 0, `+0x152` 1, the state 0 and the sub-state 0, and its idle clip;
  5. its side, level and home stay (the home is moved by a teleport of the hero), and so does the word that the hire stripped: it stands idle unless a mode in the same record (applied before) or the scripts move it.
- **A quest's release** (`0x471790` mode 1, `0x472300` to `0x47245E`): in a quest of the type 2 (`+9` 1, state bit 2) with enough of its creatures in the hero's list, each record of the flags 4 or `0x10` is let go: flag 4 cleared, master −1, removed from the list, `+0x200` bit `0x200` cleared, and an order (the event 4) to walk to the hook's creature's place (**hypothesis**: the quest's giver at its solution).

Not read: what `0x182EE3C` bit `0x10000` is; what reads a record's kind (0, 1 or 100); `0x6B`'s clearing in the strike's resolution at `0x56B296`; the quests' hook beyond the branches above.

### A hireling's hands

Read 2026-11-02 (#1771) by code, **confirmed** unless marked otherwise.
The portraits' column (`cUI_Mercenary`) takes a left press on a follower's entry, 1 to 4, in its input `0x6D6C70`.
Its test `0x6D6B90` sends the press to `0x6D6760` with the cursor empty or holding no item (its kind not 1), with a shield held (the category 13), and with a weapon held (the category 5) whose kind's first attack's slot the follower's model has (`0x956F80` by `Weapon.pak +0x1E`: 79 + 5·kind for the kinds 0 to 12, 241 for 13, 139 for 14; `0x4117B0` on the model of its type `0x426250`).
Any other press with a potion held (the category 9) uses it (`0x54AA80`; **hypothesis**: the follower drinks it) and empties the cursor; any other item does nothing.

`0x6D6760`:

- **The cursor empty.** The item of the right hand (the slot 13), else of the left (12), comes off (`0x556A30(slot, 1)`) onto the cursor.
  A creature of no hero's type has no default item in a hand (`0x426580` answers the types 1, 2, 3, 6 and 8 alone), so an emptied hand stays empty.
- **An item held** that the follower may not use (`0x54ABE0`): the event (8, `0x11`, the follower's id), which the sound manager plays nothing for (docs/re/sound.md), and nothing else.
- **An item held that it may use.** Its grip: `0x5CEC00` answers 1 for the kinds 3, 7 and 12, 3 for 0, 1, 5, 6 and 10, else 2, and 2 for the types 4067 to 4072 by their record's parent, else their own (`+0x24`); a shield's is 1; the grips 2 and 3 take the right hand, 1 the left.
  `0x5CECD0` marks an item of both hands: the kinds 2, 3, 4, 7 and 12 and those six types.
  - An item of one hand goes into the hand of its grip (`sdk_EQUIP`, `0x555E00`), or, with the follower's skill of two weapons (6, `0x582450`, its level above 0), into the right where the left holds an item or the right none, else into the left.
    The item the hand held comes off onto the cursor; where it held none, an item of both hands comes off the left, else the right.
  - An item of both hands goes into the hand of its grip where one hand is empty, the other hand's item first coming off onto the cursor; with both hands full nothing moves and the cursor keeps it.
  - The remark: the event (8, `0x10`, the follower's id, `⌊100·s⌋`), `s = (v/(v₁₂ + v₁₃) − 1)/2 + 1/2` held between 0 and 1 (`0x6D6660`, the float 100.0 `0x890998`), `v` the item's query 4 and `v₁₂`, `v₁₃` the hands' old items', each at least 1; a weapon's query 4 is the sum of its record's four most damages (`+0x24` to `+0x2A`, `cWeapon3D`'s `0x5CED50`), any other item's 0 (`0x5C70C0`).
    It is posted after any item held that it may use, also where both hands were full and nothing moved.
- Who may use (`0x54ABE0`, `0x5BF810` with the follower's type): the rules of docs/re/inventory.md, "Who may use an item", whose class tests a hireling's type passes but for the kind 3; the bit 6 refuses only the types below 32, and the types 1800 to 1804 take it and lose the classes' bits; the classes' bits test the types 132 (the Seraphim's), 133, 319 to 321, 326, 327 and 693 (the Dark Elf's), 105 and 134 (the Wood Elf's) and the heroes', and refuse every other type an item of some classes but not all; then the level, the attributes and the skill asked, of the follower's.

**Seen** 2026-11-02 in the original (the driver of #448, `spawn 322`, `hire`, a traced `sdk_EQUIP` and event): the sword 1711 held over the hireling 322's entry went into its slot 13 with the event (8, 16, its id, 100), its empty hands; a press with the cursor empty took the sword back onto it.

## The creatures' spells

Read 2026-11-02 for #1844 in `Sacred.exe`, code only (Ghidra and byte scans of `.text`); **confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise.

- A creature does not choose its spells from its arts' list, which `Creature.pak` leaves empty (docs/02 §6.2): the casters and their spells are compiled in by the creature's type.
  `0x43C540` gives each type a mark, kept at `+0x25F` (the creature maker, `Morph`, the scripts' tag `0x73`), and the fight's specials (`0x4D4E50`) switch on it, and inside it on the type, and order a cast by writing the AI's state 6 and its order block `+0x100`…`+0x13F` themselves; no code posts the event `0x105` to its own creature.
  Every writer of the state 6 (a byte scan): 16 in `0x4D4E50`, one in the melee's approach (`0x4E1FDE`, the big demons) and the followers' healer (`0x4EF4E3`, `0x4F3F0F`).
- The order block: `+0x100` the target, `+0x104` its sector's word, `+0x108`/`+0x10C` its place, `+0x114` its floor, `+0x110` whom it fights after the cast, `+0x120` the spell, `+0x13C` a variant (the magic skeletons' fireball); the state 6 (`0x51CFD0`) copies it into the action 4 (the spell, `0x51D150`), waits while that runs, and then sets the state 0 and sends the creature the event `0x100` against `+0x110` where that lives and is hostile.
- The specials run from the melee (`0x4E0300`) alone, at each of its decisions (sub-state 6) while the creature stands or walks; the ranged state 8 calls them for marks that cast nothing.
  Their answer 0 sends the creature to fight hand to hand, 1 is handled, 2 leaves the melee.
  A caster keeps a band from its target (the special mode, `+0x128` bit 0; its sub-state 1 walks to 270 from the target where it stands nearer than 240 after a cast or farther than 300, at most 25 moves), stands facing it for `+0x129` decision ticks, and casts by a roll where a straight way (`0x4FCC80`) leads to the target and its own deadline `+0x24D` has passed; else it runs to a point 280 (300) about the target or stands.
