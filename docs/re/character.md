# The character's sheet: the statistics window and the level's points

Read 2026-10-30 (#1089, #1521) in the GOG `Sacred.exe` 2.28, **confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise; the places are pixels of the 1024 × 768 screen, or of the window where it says so, and "image N" is the entry N of the interface's images' table (`0x9ECEB0`, docs/02 §6.7).
The values are docs/re/fight.md's; the points that each level gives are its "The hero's levels".

## Spending the points (`0x6A6110`)

- A click on the header bar turns the upper panel between the skills and the attributes.
- A shown "+" i:
  - in the attributes' list with attribute points and i up to 5: `0x580E90(i)` adds 1 to the attribute's extra points (`+0x1C + i`), takes 1 from `+0x23` and makes the stats anew (`0x5796A0(0, 1)`), with no sound;
  - else, the learning mode off, in the skills' list: the slot i holding a skill and skill points left, where its base is below the hero's level, (9, `0x13`) and `0x5824A0(i, 1, 1)`, which adds 1 to the base, takes 1 from `+0x42` and makes the stats anew, else (9, `0x14`) and nothing (docs/re/sound.md: neither plays); the slot empty or no skill points left, with skills to learn, the learning mode on;
  - else, the learning mode on, in the skills' list: the list of `0x581070`, the class's `Creature.pak` skills (its 16 bytes at `+0x16`) less those held, the first eight in order; a "+" i learns its skill (`0x581120`) into the first empty slot at the base 1, takes 1 from `+0x44` and makes the stats anew, with no sound, and the mode is left;
  - after any, the taskbar's message 7 with 2, which stops the "+"'s glow; `0x580E90` and `0x581120` send it with 1, which hides the "+", once no points of any kind remain.
- The stats made anew keep the current health while the most rises (**seen**: the Seraphim's 155/155 at level 3 became 155/161 with two points on `a0`).
- A click at the gold (x from 723, y from 368) drops gold: none below 10, all below 1,000, else a tenth, thrown as a pile 20 to 50 about the hero (`0x4AEBD0` of the code `0x7E60`: 5132 below 50, 5133 below 350, 5134 below 1,000, else 5135).
- The taskbar's "+" (`0x6E1BC0`): the message 8 shows it glowing (a level-up, `0x57EFCE`, and the hero's set-up where points wait, `0x585260`), 7 with 2 stops the glow, 7 with 1 hides it; its click opens the inventory.

## The skills

The name of the skill n is text 9399 + n and its description 9499 + n, for 1 to 33.
The classes' lists at `Creature.pak +0x16` begin with their two starting skills; the first eight offered, with only those two held (**hypothesis**: the templates hold no others): the Seraphim 12, 25, 1, 3, 4, 10, 9, 22; the Gladiator 5, 6, 24, 7, 4, 10, 11, 8; the Mage 15, 17, 16, 18, 2, 4, 3, 22; the Dark Elf 13, 27, 26, 6, 10, 8, 23, 22; the Wood Elf 14, 12, 19, 7, 25, 10, 22, 3; the Vampiress 10, 28, 25, 4, 5, 11, 9, 23; the Dwarf 29, 31, 5, 21, 11, 7, 25, 9; the Daemon 32, 12, 4, 5, 6, 3, 25, 10.
The slots' order matters: the list shows them in order, a new skill takes the first empty one, and the stats take the skills in that order (`0x5823E0`, `0x582450` the first slot that holds a skill).

## In `opensacred-game`

Since #1522 the window's "+" spend the points as above (`statistics::spend`, `opensacred_rules::fight::Base::raise_attribute`, `raise_skill`, `learnable_now`, `learn`): the hero's spent points `extras` and its skills' base levels `levels` kept on its `fight::Base` (the night's form takes them over), its values made anew after each (its attack, defence, damage and resistances with the points' parts of docs/re/fight.md, its regeneration, its most health by `a0 + a3` with the points, its current health kept, its arts' skills); the learning mode shows the skills to learn and the "+" are drawn by the rows as above.
The taskbar's points "+" (`points`) shows and glows as the hero rises a level, its glow stopped by a point spent and hidden once none remain, and a click on it opens the inventory; it keeps beside the portraits' column on a wider window.
**Seen** in the game beside the original's driver, the Seraphim at level 3 and 5: the two skills, the "New Skill" row and its count, the learning mode's eight skills of the list above, Magic Lore raised to 3 and refused at the hero's level, Long-handled Weapons learnt into the third slot; two points on `a0` making `A` 34, `D` 31.6 and the most health 161 (the current kept), and at level 5 a point each on `a1` and `a3` making the most health 204 and the resistances' factors 1.2299, 1.2299, 1.2012 and 1.2476 times their sums, as the original's `stats`; the "+" and its pulse beside the original's.
