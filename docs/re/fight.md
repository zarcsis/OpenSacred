# The fight

Recovered from the GOG `Sacred.exe` 2.28 with Ghidra 12.1.4 on 2026-10-20 (#276), by a subagent of the session with its key points checked.
Distances are in world units, 53.66563 a cell; "straight" is the distance of `0x5370B0` and `0x4B2350`, √(dx² + dy²), 32,000 beyond ±32,000 along an axis.
Status: **confirmed** on the code unless marked otherwise; where it says so, also by the vectors that `opensacred-oracle` records by running the functions themselves (#363, docs/03 §3.4).
The creature's fields are those of docs/re/ai.md: the AI's state `+0xFC` (sub-state `+0xFE`), the action's `+0x150` (`+0x152`), the target `+0x104`, the gait `+0x1F8` (1 walk, 2 run), the level `+0x3FE`.
The balance's values are named by their keys and read from the image of `bin/Balance.bin` (docs/02 §6.3, `tables/balance.ron`); the difficulty is the int `0x182EE44`, 0 to 4 (**hypothesis**: bronze to niobium), and a balance row of six takes the sixth value for 0, else the value `0x182EE44 − 1`.

## Who fights: the test of the levels

An awake hostile creature (`0x542B20`, docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake") weighs its level against its target's, the local player's hero, or the owner (`+0x1EC`) of an NPC it chose that is no rider.
Its level `L` is first brought back to the bronze scale, the inverse of the difficulty's scaling of the levels (`0x5522D0`; forward `0x552230`): 1 up to `BaseOffLevel`, else `(L − BaseOffLevel) / (1 + OffLevelProz/100)`, rounded, at least 1 (the image's `+0x11D0` and `+0x11B8`; 0 and 0 on bronze, where it keeps `L`).
With `E` that level and `H` the target's, `r = (E + 9)/(H + 9)` and the threshold `t` is `(H + 6)/(H + 9)` up to level 10, `(H − 6)/(H + 9)` from 92, else `UnterExpAnteil` (0.85); the test passes when `r > t` and `(r − t)/(UnterExpVoll − t)` is above 0.04: exactly when the hero would earn more than the least experience from the creature (below).
A failed test sends the creature dormant again, and in one case of three to its idle action, only when its target is also beyond its reach, and it is no boss (`+0x1F4` bit `0x80000`) nor champion (`+0x2B8` bit `0x40`) outside a group, nor a creature of a script on sight: a weak monster lets the hero pass and fights only when the hero comes within its reach, and a lone boss or champion always fights.
Otherwise it attacks a target within 800, alive and hostile to it (`0x422820`), when a straight line of walkable cells leads to it (`0x4FCC80`); without one it goes for the nearest closed door of its sector (`0x4D4800`, "Doors in the way" below), staying idle when there is none, and when no way leads to it at all (`0x4F7710`) it takes its idle action.
It takes the AI's state 8 with a ranged weapon in a hand (item category 5, weapon kinds 7, 12, 13, 14), else 4; in a group, in one case of two, it raises the group's alarm (`0x46AE70`, docs/re/ai.md, "Groups").
The reach `0x4DBC60(a, b)`, a `__thiscall` on the creature `a` with `b` on the stack (`a` again when `b` is null), is the mean, in integers, of the two creatures' sizes by their types (`+0x10`), 130 for a type that has none of its own (read again 2026-10-23, #510):

- 70: the Wood Elf (5), the Vampiress by day (6), 333;
- 80, or 110 with a weapon in a hand (`0x546F80(0)` not 0): the Dark Elf (4), the types 274 to 303;
- 100: the Seraphim (1), 32 to 34, 38, 45, 48 to 51, 192, 316, 699;
- 110: 259;
- 120: the Gladiator (2), 37, 44, 182, 188, 194, 260, 307, 308, 315, 322, 670, 671, 689, 690;
- 170: 190, 193, 348;
- 200: 187, 202 to 208, 253 to 255, 344, 350 to 352, 359;
- 300: 183;
- 420: 55;
- 480: 52, 74, 88, 100 to 103;
- the Mage (3), the Vampiress by night (7), the Dwarf (8), the Daemon (9) and every other type 130.

So the Seraphim and a goblin warrior (76, of the default) are 115 apart, the Gladiator and one 125; `opensacred_rules::minds::size` and `reach` give them, the game's hero its size by its class and what it holds, an NPC its own by its type and its items (#642).

## Sides

Status: **confirmed** by code (read 2026-10-23, #580) unless marked otherwise.
Whether a creature is hostile to another is a matter of their sides (`+0x1F0`), not of their flags words: `0x548F60` and `0x549000` ask `0x423580`, which reads the client's table of 16 × 16 bytes at `0x890A30` (kept in a copy at `0x8EB548`, which it checks and copies again) by the first creature's side as the row and the other's as the column, 0 hostile and 1 a friend.
The heroes are of side 1, their types' `Creature.pak` class; the side 1 is a friend of the sides 0, 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 and hostile to the sides 2, 5 and 8 to 15, and so are those to it; the side 13 is hostile to every side, the sides 0 and 6 friends of every side but 13, and the hostile sides are friends of one another but for 5 and 8, 5 and 12 and 9 and 15 both ways and 15 against 5 and 8; the table is not symmetric, the hostile sides being friends of the side 4, the horses', which is hostile to them.
`0x423580` also makes the type 75 (`med_spider.grn`) hostile to every creature but those of the types 75, 99 and 109, makes the types 189 and 334 hostile to the types 245 and 188 and the other way about, takes a horse's rider's side for the horse, has a creature of `+0x200` bit `0x10000000` keep friends with those of its list, and turns its answer over for a creature of `+0x1F4` bit `0x40000`.

A creature's side is its type's `Creature.pak` class (`+0x04`, through `0x43ADC0`), which its constructor (`0x52DE40`) gives it; `0x52E420` sets another, or the class again.
The creature maker `0x480D40`, which `FillSector` calls with each entry's hostility, puts a creature it makes hostile on the side 2 when its class is not hostile to the local hero or is the animals' (6), and sets bit 0 of its flags word; one it makes peaceful on the side 3 when its class is hostile to the hero and not the animals', and clears bit 0 (`0x480F24` to `0x4810A7`).
`CreateNPC` (`0x482510`) keeps a hostility from its tags, the last of them counting: `0x08` hostile, `0x0E` peaceful and the modes `0x2B`, `0x2E`, `0x30`, `0x32` and `0x42` peaceful too; `0x8D`, handled after the tags (an event `0x109` and `+0x200` bit `0x10`, not read further), makes it peaceful whatever comes before; the tag `0x85` puts it on the side 2 first (`0x48344E`).
Without a hostility, the modes `0x4000` and `0x8000` (`0x46`, `0x4C`) make it peaceful (`0x483616`).
A hostile one then goes on the side 2, or 14 for the mode `0x4000` or the side 7, unless it is hostile to the hero already and no animal; a peaceful one on the side 3, or 7 so, when it is hostile to the hero (`0x483605` to `0x4836B2`); a creature without either keeps its class's side.
So the start's captain of the garrison of 52,39 (`0E 46 13`, the type 297 of class 7, the guards' side) is a friend of the hero, though its mode 2 has it stand in the hero's way (docs/re/movement.md).
`CreateNPC` wakes a creature of the flags' bit 0 alone, and gives it the timer's shorter period (`0x483A03`, `0x48396E`).
In the Seraphim's tree 58 records of the start script (the animals of a goodie quest and others, with `08` and the mode `0x4000`), 42 of the quests' `OnEnter` functions and 17 of the sectors' `Init`s (of the classes 11 and others, `08` without a mode) are hostile by their sides and were peaceful by their flags' bits; the captain is the start script's one the other way.

## Melee: the AI's state 4

`0x4E0300` keeps a creature within a band about its reach `R`: `R − 15` to `R + 15` (`R + 45` for a player's).
Deciding (sub-state 0):

- At a third of its health or less, within 199 of its target, not a boss nor of the classes 1 and 5, once a fight (`+0x1F4` bit `0x200`), in one case of eight a tick of the world while its target does not attack, it flees 700 from the target along the line.
- Within the band it strikes (sub-state 2); outside it, it comes on (sub-state 1), at a run in 7 cases of 11.
- Then a creature that is the second, third or fourth of the hero's list of hostiles (`0xAD3CE0`, docs/re/motions.md, "The fight stance"; the list's count above 1) and stands nearer than `R − 15` to the first of that list takes sub-state 5 instead (`0x4E1404`).

Coming on (sub-state 1), it gives up farther than 2,000 (the AI's state 0); a creature of `+0x2B8` bit `0x10` takes a bow (`BOW_SHORT` or `BOW_LONG`, `0x4D4A80`) for a target beyond its reach + 100 and shoots; nearer than the band it steps back; else it walks or runs to the target, stopping within the band.
The bow's test is made in the sub-states 0, 1 and 2 alike (read 2026-10-21, #396; `0x4E0C63`, `0x4E1B78`, `0x4E43C5`), as soon as the distance to the target is taken, after the give-up beyond 2,000 in the sub-states 1 and 2; `0x4D4A80` takes the AI's state 8, deciding (sub-state 0), from which the creature shoots in its band ("At range" below).
In the band (sub-state 2), a creature at full health away from home, farther than 2,500 or 5,000 from it, walks home (the leash; partly read); else it strikes: the attack action 3 (`0x4E4CF0`) with its target, and the AI back to state 0, from which the idle handler, once the action ends, takes it into state 4 again.
A creature standing in sub-state 2 strikes even beyond the band when a straight line leads to the target; without one it looks for a way to the target (`0x4F7710`) and goes on (sub-state 0) when the way ends within `R + 80` of the target along each axis, else goes for a door ("Doors in the way"), and without a door gives up (the AI's state 0; `0x4E4931`).
A creature still moving (the action 1) in sub-state 2 is steered by its target alone: within the band it strikes at once, beyond it it walks straight to the target, nearer it steps back.

**The strike's end and its chain** (read 2026-10-22, #466, in the code and in the original frame by frame with the driver of #448).
The attack action has two halves: its sub-state 1, which plays the clip to the hit (the strike resolves as it turns into sub-state 2), and its sub-state 2, which plays it out.
As sub-state 2 ends, the creature strikes again at once (sub-state 1 anew, its target kept) when all of these hold, else it ends the action and its AI's state 4 takes the sub-state 6, from which the AI decides again:

- its type's class is not 1 (`0x426650`), unless the flag `0x20` of the global `0x182EE3C` is set, then in two cases of three;
- its target is alive, hostile to it and seen, and a straight line of walkable cells leads to it (`0x4DB8A0`, `0x4FCC80`);
- its target stands nearer than `R + 30` (`0x5370B0`'s distance; `R` of `0x4DBC60`, the mean of the two types' sizes, 130 but for the types it names, the Seraphim's 100);
- its mark (`+0x25F`, a word that the creature's maker `0x52DE40` takes from its type by `0x43C540`) is 0, or `rand() & 3` is 0: a marked creature chains in one case of four.

The marked types: 34, 35, 41 to 48, 52, 53, 55 to 68, 70 to 76, 80, 81, 88, 94, 96 to 98, 100 to 103, 105, 118, 125 to 127, 130, 133, 140, 141, 143 to 146, 182 to 187, 190 to 192, 195, 202 to 208, 212, 216, 218, 219, 247 to 256, 258, 262 to 271, 304 to 306, 311, 312, 319 to 321, 326, 329 to 333, 335, 336, 342 to 344, 348, 350 to 354, 363, 512, 567 to 571, 573 to 583, 693, 697, 700 and 701; the goblin warriors (76) among them, of the mark `0x400` (observed).
The AI's state 4 then steps about every 0.1 s (observed: the sub-state 6 for 6 or 7 frames, the sub-state 2 for 6 or 7, then the strike; **hypothesis**: paced by the render thread's call of the creatures' events every 0.1 s, `0x60F130`).
Observed with a goblin warrior of level 1 at the Seraphim standing 119.5 off (`R + 30` 145): the halves 38 to 40 frames each, the hit 633 to 733 ms into the strike; a chained strike 77 to 82 frames after the last one's start, one through the AI 89 to 93; 15 of 47 strikes chained (about the one in four), 1.49 s apart on average over 77 strikes.

Places (sub-state 5, one frame, read 2026-10-21, #383; `0x4E224E` to `0x4E23A4`):

- By default the creature's point is its target's position, its move to stop at `R` (`+0x174` 15, `+0x176` `R`, `+0x17E` the target), and it walks there (the action 1) when farther than `R + 15` from the target.
- In place of that, the second of the list takes the point `R − 15` from its target along the world's `+x`, the third along `−y` and the fourth along `+y` (while the list's count is above 1, 2 or 3), its move to stop within 40 of the point (`+0x174` 1, `+0x176` 40: in the AI's state 4 the move `0x4F4770` ends within `+0x176` of its point when `+0x174` is 1), walking there when the point is farther than `R + 15` from it.
- Then a creature not of class 5 runs in one case of two (`+0x1F8` 2), and it takes sub-state 2 with its target.
- The AI runs at every world tick (docs/re/ai.md, "The world's tick"), and at the next one sub-state 2 steers a moving creature by its target alone (above): a place holds for a tick, and the creature then strikes, comes on to the target or steps back as the others do (**hypothesis**: the spreading hardly shows in play).

The attack action plays one of the model's attack slots (`0x5467A0`, action 4: 79 + 5 · the class of what it holds, docs/re/motions.md) at its attack speed (`0x584FE0`, at most 220) times `+0x210`, 1.0, which an archer's shots raise by 0.1 up to 1.7 ("At range" below).
The code was read as raising it for each strike that a hero chains while the last one runs as well (#312), which play does not bear out: a hero's `+0x210` stayed 1.0, its button held or clicked again and again (observed 2026-10-23, #704; "The hero's attack" below; **hypothesis**: something other than its plain strikes, an art or a combo, raises a hero's).
A strike hits (`0x4EA1F0`) at a fraction of its clip given by the clip's `MotionDesc +0xA0` (0.5 where that is 0), and a second time at `+0xA4` when that is above 0 or the creature holds two weapons: `floats_62[1]` and `[2]` of `tables/motions.ron` (docs/02 §4.1; 827 of the 989 attack clips carry a first fraction, 173 a second).
It lands when the target is then within three times the reach (1.5 times for a player's; **hypothesis** that the base is `R`), sending it the event 5 with the attacker, the weapon in hand and a factor of 1, or 0.5 for each of two weapons.
That is `0x4EA3F0`, a function of its own, which `0x4EA1F0` calls near its end, after the checks of the fraction and of the distance (`0x4EA3BF`): it reads both hands, sets the factor and posts the event (read 2026-10-20, #322, #333).
It has 58 calls in 14 functions, 5 of them in the melee state's `0x4E0300`; the others are not followed.
In one case of four an NPC whose target attacks plays another clip instead (**hypothesis**: a parry).

## At range: the AI's state 8

`0x4DBFC0` keeps an archer 300 to 800 from its target: within 300, up to ten times a fight, it backs off; beyond 800 it comes on, stopping within 450; between, without a straight line, it moves to a point near the target, turned −30° to +30°; else it shoots (the action 5, "The archers" below), its `+0x210` ramping between 250 and 650.
It gives up farther than 2,000, and goes back to melee without a ranged weapon.
A shot ends in the AI's state 0, and the idle handler takes a target up again only within 800 along a straight line ("Who fights" below), so an archer gives up a target gone beyond 800 at its next decision and seldom comes on: **seen** 2026-10-25 (#227, check 26), a thief 308 of 50,41 that shot at the hero from about 630 dropped its target and went back to its idle steps as the hero walked out to about 875, and again to about 940 (#972).

Its sub-states, read 2026-10-21 (#391), **confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise:

- Before them, a creature without a ranged weapon in a hand (`0x54A2A0`) and without `+0x200` bits `0x600000`, or one of `+0x14` bit `0x2000`, goes back to melee (the AI's state 4); within 150 of its target a creature of class 5 (`0x426A80`) whose `+0x25F` is not 10 does so in one case of four, its bow put away.
  That bow is the item of its slot 12, put at the creature's place (`0x5FCCB0`) and handed on by the event `0x14` (**hypothesis**: dropped on the ground), and no other weapon is given; without an item in slot 12 it keeps what it holds (read 2026-10-21, #396).
- Deciding (sub-state 0), it clears its count of back-offs (`+0x12B`); standing or moving (the action 0 or 1), it shoots when its target is from 250 to 650, and else, unless `0x4D4B70` sends it to melee ("The archers" below), takes the sub-state 1.
  In another action it waits: a shot that has left (the action 5, its sub-state not 0) raises `+0x210` by 0.1 up to 1.7, and any other action resets it to 1.
- Moving to its band (sub-state 1), against a target hostile to it (else its idle action), the distances integers of `0x5370B0`:
  - farther than 2,000 it gives up (the AI's state 0);
  - nearer than 300, not marked (`+0x1F4` bit `0x200`, which a boss's or a champion's levelling sets too) and with fewer than ten back-offs counted, it waits while it moves, and standing, unless `0x4D4B70` sends it to melee, backs off to the point as far beyond itself as its target is on the other side, at a walk with `+0x200` bit `0x800` in one case of two (**hypothesis**: walking backwards, facing the target), else at a run, a walk for class 5, and counts the back-off;
  - farther than 800 it comes on to its target's place, stopping within 450 (`+0x174` 1, `+0x176` 450), at its gait as it is, whether it moves or not;
  - from 300 to 800 without a straight line (`0x4FCC80`) it waits while it moves, and standing walks to its place turned about its target by `rand() % 61 − 30` degrees, its distance kept, stopping within 25;
  - else it shoots, whether it moves or not: the action 5 with its target, the AI's state 0 and the count of back-offs 0, from which the idle handler takes it into the state 8 again.
- A creature with an owner (`+0x14` bit 2 and `+0x1EC`) within 800 of its target skips the moves and shoots.

So an archer shoots from 300 to 800 along a straight line, from 250 when it has just decided, and nearer only after ten back-offs in a row; a back-off ends in a shot as soon as it is 300 away, and "ten times a fight" is ten times between two shots.

## Doors in the way

Status: **confirmed** by code (#383, read 2026-10-21) unless marked otherwise.
`0x4D4800` serves a creature not of the classes 2, 5 (unless its `+0x25F` is set) and 6 (`Creature.pak +0x04`, `0x426AD0`, `0x426A80`, `0x426A30`; by their models the beasts, the undead and the animals).
It takes the objects of the creature's sector that the script manager keeps (`0xAB4490`; a list of more than 1,000 is emptied instead, a guard) and of them the nearest door, straight from the creature: an object of category 10 with a trigger (`+0x34`) whose state (`+0x0A`) is neither barred (bit 15, which keeps a door from opening, `0x415F00`) nor open (bit 0) and whose flags word (`+0x04`) has bit `0x2000`, on the creature's layer or on the ground and the first floor either way.
That bit is a hero's mark: the door's event handler (`0x44BCD0`) sets it when the event `0x21`, 1 or 2 comes from an id of 16 or below, so a creature goes only for a closed door that a hero has opened or closed before.
With such a door it sends itself the event 7 with the door and its position, as the hero's click does (`0x6172C0`), and returns true; without, false.
The event 7 takes the AI's state 1 (`0x53A170`) with the door as its target:

- it walks to the door's outer point when it stands on the ground, its inner point when on a building's floor (`0x43B290`, docs/re/ai.md, "Houses and doors"), by a way (`0x4F7CE0`, `0x4F8AF0`), stopping within 20; with no way there, to a nearby cell of the ground nibble 9 or 10 (not read); already within 25 of the point along each axis, it stands;
- a creature of `+0x200` bit 2 (not read) does so only for the 19 gates, palisades, fences and cages among the door types (5674, 5676 to 5679, 5740 to 5750, 5756 to 5758), and for another door takes its `+0x1EC` as its target instead;
- standing (sub-state 2) within 300 of the door, on its layer or the ground and the first floor either way, it calls the quests' hook (`0x471790`, not followed) and, unless the door's object has flag `0x4000`, sends the door the event `0x21`; then the AI's state 0.

The door turns (`0x44BCD0`): one not barred opens when closed (its clip `0xAA`, the trigger's bit 0 set) and closes when open (`0xAB`); a barred one only closes; and the event from a creature that is no hero leaves open a door that a hero marked.
So the creature opens the door, and its idle state (`0x542B20`) finds its target again, the door's cells now walkable (docs/re/movement.md: a door's cell may be walked on while its trigger's bit 0 is set).
**Seen** 2026-10-25 (#227, check 28) at the merchant's door of 50,40, the hero inside on the building's floor and a goblin warrior outside:
- with the door closed and unmarked, as it starts, the goblin searched every 0.3 s and found none, staying idle;
- with the door opened and closed again by the hero, the goblin walked to its outer point (the AI's state 1, the door its target) and the door opened;
- it then stayed outside, since no straight line leads from the ground to a hero on the building's floor, and an open door is no candidate.
The hero's door, open, stayed open for two minutes at dusk while the townsfolk inside walked through its cell, where an unmarked door that a passer-by had opened was closed again within 0.5 s.
`0x4D4800` is called in three places: the idle state's choice when no straight line leads to the target (`0x5442E8`; without a door the creature stays idle); the melee's sub-state 2 when the target is beyond the band, no straight line leads to it and the way ends farther than `R + 80` from it (`0x4E4931`; without a door the AI's state 0); and the move (`0x4F4770`, at `0x4F5652`) in the AI's state 4 when it finds no way to its point twice, the second time after a jitter of up to 50 along each axis, while the local hero is alive and hostile to it (`0x549000`).
**Hypothesis**: the hero's left click on a door takes the same way, since `0x6172C0` sends the hero the event 7 with the object under the cursor (`0x626C50`) and the state 1 handles a door for any creature; that would answer #372, which found no door opening on the hero's side.

## Bosses and champions

Status: **confirmed** by code (#316) unless marked otherwise.
A boss is a creature of `+0x1F4` bit `0x80000`, a champion one of `+0x2B8` bit `0x40`.
`FillSector` makes them after the creature maker (`0x47E6B7`…`0x47E863`): the types 203, 342 and 349 always champions; 37, 48, 55 and 84 a boss or a champion by a coin; 127 and 49 the same in one case of two, else as the others; any other creature hostile to a living hero a champion when `rand() % 1001` is below `HarteSau` (20); 343 and 212 only take a champion's levelling (`+0xB0` bit `0x400`, which a champion has too).
Read again 2026-10-23 (#621): the two take the marker level 1 first, as a champion does (`0x47E6F3`, the level's setter with the level 1; for 343 after a roll of `rand() % 5` whose outcome leads to the same call), then the bit, without the mark.
They are made at the marker level 1; the black rider (256) is a boss at its fill's level.
`CreateNPC` makes a boss with the level tag `0x1F` of 1000 (at the marker level 1, `0x4843C9`, docs/re/spawning.md) or the tag `0x72` (only in the dynamic quests' records), never a champion; the script command `0x49E760` with the level 1000 makes a group's creatures bosses (**hypothesis**: `SetNPCState`).
At its first strike or hit (`0x56B130` for both sides, `0x550E50`), a boss or champion of level 1 or 1000 (the strike calls the levelling for a boss with `LevelUpBoss`, and with `LevelUpChamp` at `0x56B682` and `0x56B6A5` for a creature of the mark `+0x2B8` bit `0x40` or of `+0xB0` bit `0x400`, so for a fill's 343 and 212 too, #621) takes its opponent's level plus `LevelUpBoss` or `LevelUpChamp` (2 and 2), at least `OffLevel` plus its sector's lowest level, and, unless its type is "big" (`Creature.pak` flag `0x02`), at most `LevelKap` plus its sector's highest plus the same bonus; it is then marked (`+0x1F4` bit `0x200`), its health refilled and its worn items made again at the new level.
An archer makes this levelling in its state 8 against the local hero rather than its target.
The hero's look (`0x547070`, at `0x547377`) levels so, by the hero, each boss, champion or creature of a champion's levelling of level 1 or 1000 in its range that is alive and hostile to the hero, or of `+0x1F4` bit `0x40000` (read 2026-10-23, #630); one not hostile to the hero waits for its first strike or hit, whose opponent may be a creature: the start's boss 689, a guard peaceful on the side 7, stood at 13 and 733 health after its fight with a bear of level 11 (#630).
`cCreature_render` calls the levelling too (`0x599B6C`, not read).
By the mark, not by the levelling's bit (`0x5658F0`, `0x56B9EB`; #621), its most health is times `HPFakBoss` (2.5) or `HPFakChamp` (2.0), three times that for the types 701, 137 and 53, and 1.8, 2.8 or 4 times for a dragon or flyer (class 10) by the difficulty; a boss attacks with `A` times `AWFakBoss` (1.5) and defends with `D` times `VWFakBoss` (2.0), a champion with `AWFakChamp` (1.3) and `VWFakChamp` (1.5); its kill's experience is twice or 1.5 times.

## The archers

A creature of `+0x2B8` bit `0x10` takes a bow once (`0x4D4A80`): the state 8, its slot 13 emptied, `BOW_SHORT` (1732) or `BOW_LONG` (1733) at random made at its level and held in slot 12, the bit cleared.

## A strike's resolution

The event 5 (`0x531DC8`) goes to `0x56B130`; a target dead or dying, talking, or farther than 1,500 from the attacker ignores it.
The attack record (`0x568D70`) gives, for each of the four kinds of damage (physical, fire, magic, poison), a damage rolled between a least and a most, the four resistances, the attack `A` and the defence `D`, from the weapons in hand, the items' bonuses and `Creature.pak`'s bonuses; the stats' recomputation (`0x5796A0`) scales them by the level and the attributes (the balance's `BalStatOff`, `BalChar*`).
An NPC's own come from its `Creature.pak` record and its level (below).

- At range, with a weapon of the kinds 7, 12, 13 or 14 (`0x427ED0`), `A` keeps its value to `FernAWMin` (250) of the distance between the attacker and the target, falls linearly to `1 − FernAWProz` of it (0.75) toward `FernAWMax` (1,000) and is `FernAWProz` of it (0.25) from there, a jump (`0x56B449`, read 2026-10-21, #383); such an attacker of class 10 (the dragons and flyers) has its damage share scaled alike.
- Bosses and champions: the attacker's `A` times `AWFakBoss` or `AWFakChamp`, the defender's `D` times `VWFakBoss` or `VWFakChamp`; a boss or champion of level 1 or 1000 takes, when first fighting, the attacker's level + `LevelUpBoss` or `LevelUpChamp`, within its sector's levels raised by `OffLevel` and `LevelKap` (`0x550E50`).
- The difficulty: an NPC (an id above 16) attacking a player (16 or below) has `A` times `ProzAW` and each kind's damage that the attack record rolled times `ProzDmg`, each product on the x87 unit stored as a float (`0x56BBB8`, read 2026-10-23, #620); a player attacking an NPC meets `D` times `ProzAW` and resistances times `ProzRes`.
- The hit chance (`0x43EEE0`) is `p = 1 − 1/(1 + A/D)^k` with `k = −log₂(1 − 0.5) = 1`, that is `A/(A + D)` (`A` raised for a combat art); the strike misses when a roll of 0 to 1000, over 1000, exceeds `p`.
- Each kind's damage, through the same function, becomes `damage²/(damage + resistance)` (a larger `k` for an NPC attacker above the defender's level), times `BalanceDmg` and `BalanceRes`, then times `(100 − damping)/100`, the defender's `Creature.pak +0x42` for the kind and the difficulty; criticals, life leech and the skills' specials follow (partly read).
- That function, `0x43EEE0(x, y, c, p, out)` (`cdecl`), returns `x·w` and writes the float `w = 1 − 1/(1 + x/y)^k` to `out` when it is not null, with `k = −ln(1 − p)/ln(1 + c)`; the resolution passes the base `c` 1, so `k = −log₂(1 − p)` (read 2026-10-21, #406).
  For `y` within ±0.001, the bounds included (the double 0.001, so that the float 0.001, a little above it, divides), it takes 10,000 for `x/y`: against a defence within ±0.001 the chance is 0.9999 whatever the attack, so the roll of 1,000 misses, and a damage `d` against a resistance within ±0.001 becomes `d·(1 − 10,001^−k)`, 0.9999·d for `k` 1, which the sum's truncation can take a point off (15 becomes 14.9985).
  It holds `p` at 0.99 when it is 1 or more and at 0 below 0, so that a share between 0.99 and 1 stays, and `c` at −1 or above (#407).
  The resolution's share of the damage is `(1 + n/100)/2`, the sum kept as a float, for an attacker that is no player's (an id above 16) `n` levels above its defender: 99 levels above it is 0.995 and `k` about 7.64, more than the 6.64 of 98 levels and of 100 and more.
  **Confirmed** by the vectors that `opensacred-oracle` records from the function (#363).
- The sum of the four kinds, a float, is then times the factor of the attacker's level, `0x56A890(attacker, defender)` (`cdecl`, called at `0x56D05C`; read 2026-10-23, #615).
  It is 1 unless the attacker is an NPC (an id above 16) striking a player (an id of 16 or below), and neither a boss (`+0x1F4` bit `0x80000`) nor of a champion's levelling (`+0xB0` bit `0x400`: every champion, and the fill's types 343 and 212; "Bosses and champions").
  With `L` the attacker's level (`+0x3FE`, `u16`), the band `i` is the first of 0, 1 and 2 with `AllOverLv[i] ≤ L ≤ AllOverLv[i + 1]`, else 3 whatever the level: the loop goes to the computation when its pointer reaches the last band, without the test.
  The factor is `WaffenAll[i] + (WaffenAll[i + 1] − WaffenAll[i])/(L − AllOverLv[i])` on the x87 unit (`FLD` and `FSUB` of the floats, `FIDIV` of the integer, `FADD`), at the world's 53 bits, and 1 when it comes to 0 or below.
  A quirk: the step is divided by the level's distance from its band's start rather than interpolated, so that with the rows of `Balance.bin` (`WaffenAll` 0.6, 0.65, 0.75, 0.85, 0.95; `AllOverLv` 0, 4, 10, 20, 35) the factor is 0.65 at level 1, 0.625 at 2, 0.6125 at 4, 0.75 at 5, 0.667 at 10, 0.85 at 11, 0.76 at 20, 0.95 at 21 and 0.855 at 40, and goes down toward 0.85 above.
  At the level 0 it would divide by zero; `opensacred-rules` takes 1 there.
  The product of the factor and the sum, on the x87 unit, is stored as a float.
  Its twin `0x56A800` takes `MagieAll` (0.58, 0.63, 0.68, 0.76, 0.85) by the caster's level for two spell functions (`0x5ABD80`, `0x5B2600`); `FernAll` and `RuestAll` have no reader found.

- Then the sum is times the event's factor (`+0x1C`: the hit's share of a strike, halved with two weapons, or a combat art's factor, docs/re/arts.md) when that is above 0, below 500 and not 1, and held at 1 or more (#508, #615); the skills' specials and the effects that follow are not read.

The attack record (`0x568D70`) multiplies each kind's least and most by its multiplier, the resistances by theirs, and `A` and `D` by theirs, truncated when not 1.
Its product of `A` or `D` and its multiplier stays on the x87 unit up to its truncation (`__ftol`), never stored as a float.
It rolls each kind's damage as a whole number between its ends in hundredths when the physical most is below 100, in tenths below 1,000, in units below 10,000; a negative most counts as 1 and a negative least as 0.

## Health

A creature's health is its stats' (`+0x3A8`) `+0x130` (the creature's `+0x4D8`), its most `+0x12C` (`+0x4D4`) and the value shown `+0x128` (`+0x4D0`), which follows the health over time.
The most (`0x5658F0`) comes from `Creature.pak`'s base bytes `b0`, `b3` (`+0x0C`, `+0x0F`), the stats' attributes `a0` and `a3` (`+0x10`, `+0x16`) and the level `L`: `S = b0 + b3 + ⌊(L − 1)·b0/10⌋ + ⌊(L − 1)·b3/10⌋`, `g = 1.5 + G · max(0, 60 − b0 − b3)` with `G` the float at `0x891298`, 0.0039963233, the most `⌊S^g · (a0 + a3)/S · 10/31⌋` in double precision, that is `⌊S^g · 10/31⌋` for a creature without extra points or items' attribute bonuses, whose `a0 + a3` is `S` (**confirmed** by code and by the vectors of `opensacred-oracle`, #363, #408; read before as `⌊(b0 + b2) · S^(g − 1) · 10/31⌋`), raised by the skill 10 by its per cent of the curve `HPoffVW`, `HP__sVW`, `HP__wVW` (4, 50, 130) and taken whole (#492; the Dwarf's 133 to 138 at level 1, as the original's), by `HPFakBoss` or `HPFakChamp`, and for a creature not a hero by a factor of its level between the balance's `HP_s1`…`HP_w5` points and by `ProzHP`; 0 becomes 100, and a new creature starts full.
The skill 10's raise of a most `m` is `⌊m·v·0.01 + m⌋` on the x87 unit (read 2026-10-23, #598): `v` the per cent of the skill's first slot at its level, computed there and kept, never stored as a float, and 0.01 the float at `0x890CB0`, a little below a hundredth; at the world's 53 bits (#589) a raise that comes to a whole number in real numbers falls just below it.
For example, the type 557 at level 82 has the most 2,850 before the factors and its skill 10 at level 27, `v` = 90.2105…: 5,420, where `⌊m·(1 + v/100)⌋` with a float's sum gives 5,421.
Among the NPC types at the levels 1 to 100 only 557 and 689 at level 82 meet such an edge; `fight::hero_health` and `fight::most_health` take the raise so.
Each factor is taken whole in that order: the curve, the skill 10's raise, the boss's or champion's factor, an item effect's 104/100 (`+0x148` bit `0x80000`), 100 for 0, then the level's factor times `ProzHP` (read 2026-10-23, #579: the boss 577 of 55,39 at level 3, of the curve's 98, has `⌊⌊98 · 2.5⌋ · 0.8⌋ = 196` in the original, not `⌊⌊98 · 0.8⌋ · 2.5⌋ = 195`).
The level's factor is the first weight of the first pair of points whose levels hold the level plus the level times the pair's slope, the level taken whole; the last pair, `HP_s4` to `HP_s5`, is taken without that test, so that above the last point the factor goes on along its line: 1.0 + `L`·0.1/15 on file, 1.4 at level 60, 1.67 at 100 and 2.44 at 216 (**confirmed** by code and by the vectors of `opensacred-oracle`, #363, #418).
The 0 that becomes 100 is taken before the factors, so a most that they take below 1 is 0 (**confirmed** by the vectors of `opensacred-oracle`, #363, #417); no creature of `Creature.pak` reaches it, the least sum of its bytes `b0` and `b3` being 7 (type 135), which gives 9 at level 1.
It is computed only for a creature of the local client, its `+0x39` that of the client's object of `0x7D84A0` (**hypothesis**: the creatures of another client have theirs from it).
It regenerates (`0x562730`) by its regeneration value (`+0x4E0`, above) times `RegInFight` for an NPC, and for a player `RegInFight` fighting, `RegEnemyNear` with a hostile within 1,000, else `RegNoEnemy`.
