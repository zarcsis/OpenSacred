# The fight

Recovered from the GOG `Sacred.exe` 2.28 with Ghidra 12.1.4 on 2026-10-20 (#276), by a subagent of the session with its key points checked.
Distances are in world units, 53.66563 a cell; "straight" is the distance of `0x5370B0` and `0x4B2350`, √(dx² + dy²), 32,000 beyond ±32,000 along an axis.
Status: **confirmed** on the code unless marked otherwise; where it says so, also by the vectors that `opensacred-oracle` records by running the functions themselves (#363, docs/03 §3.4).
The creature's fields are those of docs/re/ai.md: the AI's state `+0xFC` (sub-state `+0xFE`), the action's `+0x150` (`+0x152`), the target `+0x104`, the gait `+0x1F8` (1 walk, 2 run), the level `+0x3FE`.
The balance's values are named by their keys and read from the image of `bin/Balance.bin` (docs/02 §6.3, `tables/balance.ron`); the difficulty is the int `0x182EE44`, 0 to 4, bronze to niobium ("The difficulty" below), and a balance row of six takes the sixth value for 0, else the value `0x182EE44 − 1`.
Implemented in `opensacred-rules` (`fight`, `fight/magic`, `minds`, `arrows`, `undead`, `set_bonuses`, `drops`, `music`), `opensacred-game` (`fight`, `attack`, `ai`, `npcs`, `motions`, `arrows`, `undead`, `bodies`, `knock_down`, `health_rings`, `rank_symbols`, `level_sparks`, `doors`, `equipment`) and `opensacred-oracle` (`fight`); the vectors' test `crates/opensacred-rules/tests/oracle.rs`.

## Who fights: the test of the levels

An awake hostile creature (`0x542B20`, docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake") weighs its level against its target's, the local player's hero, or the owner (`+0x1EC`) of an NPC it chose that is no rider.
Its level `L` is first brought back to the bronze scale, the inverse of the difficulty's scaling of the levels (`0x5522D0`; forward `0x552230`): 1 up to `BaseOffLevel`, else `(L − BaseOffLevel) / (1 + OffLevelProz/100)`, rounded, at least 1 (the image's `+0x11D0` and `+0x11B8`; 0 and 0 on bronze, where it keeps `L`).
With `E` that level and `H` the target's, `r = (E + 9)/(H + 9)` and the threshold `t` is `(H + 6)/(H + 9)` up to level 10, `(H − 6)/(H + 9)` from 92, else `UnterExpAnteil` (0.85); the test passes when `r > t` and `(r − t)/(UnterExpVoll − t)` is above 0.04: exactly when the hero would earn more than the least experience from the creature (below).
A failed test sends the creature dormant again, and in one case of three to its idle action, only when its target is also beyond its reach, and it is no boss (`+0x1F4` bit `0x80000`) nor champion (`+0x2B8` bit `0x40`) outside a group, nor a creature of a script on sight: a weak monster lets the hero pass and fights only when the hero comes within its reach, and a lone boss or champion always fights.
Otherwise it attacks a target within 800, alive and hostile to it (`0x422820`), when a straight line leads to it (`0x4FCC80`, asked as a line of sight, its flag 1, which passes a fence, the water and the creatures between: docs/re/movement.md, "The straight way", read 2026-11-22 for #3069); without one it goes for the nearest closed door of its sector (`0x4D4800`, "Doors in the way" below), staying idle when there is none, and when no way leads to it at all (`0x4F7710`) it takes its idle action.
It takes the AI's state 8 with a ranged weapon in a hand (item category 5, weapon kinds 7, 12, 13, 14), else 4; in a group, in one case of two, it raises the group's alarm (`0x46AE70`, docs/re/ai.md, "Groups").
The reach `0x4DBC60(a, b)`, a `__thiscall` on the creature `a` with `b` on the stack (`a` again when `b` is null), is the mean, in integers, of the two creatures' sizes by their types (`+0x10`), 130 for a type that has none of its own (read again 2026-10-23, #510):

- 70: the Wood Elf (5), the Vampiress by day (6), 333;
- 80, or 110 with a weapon in a hand (`0x546F80(0)` not 0): the Dark Elf (4), the types 274 to 303;
- 100: the Seraphim (1), 32 to 34, 38, 45, 48 to 51, 192, 316, 699;
- 110: 259;
- 120: the Gladiator (2), 37, 44, 182, 188, 194, 260, 307, 308, 315, 322, 670, 671, 689, 690;
- 170: 190, 193, 348;
- 200: 187, 202 to 208, 253 to 255, 344, 350 to 352, 359;
- 300: 183;
- 420: 55;
- 480: 52, 74, 88, 100 to 103;
- the Mage (3), the Vampiress by night (7), the Dwarf (8), the Daemon (9) and every other type 130.

So the Seraphim and a goblin warrior (76, of the default) are 115 apart, the Gladiator and one 125; `opensacred_rules::minds::size` and `reach` give them, the game's hero its size by its class and what it holds, an NPC its own by its type and its items (#642).

## Sides

Status: **confirmed** by code (read 2026-10-23, #580) unless marked otherwise.
Whether a creature is hostile to another is a matter of their sides (`+0x1F0`), not of their flags words: `0x548F60` and `0x549000` ask `0x423580`, which reads the client's table of 16 × 16 bytes at `0x890A30` (kept in a copy at `0x8EB548`, which it checks and copies again) by the first creature's side as the row and the other's as the column, 0 hostile and 1 a friend.
The heroes are of side 1, their types' `Creature.pak` class; the side 1 is a friend of the sides 0, 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 and hostile to the sides 2, 5 and 8 to 15, and so are those to it; the side 13 is hostile to every side, the sides 0 and 6 friends of every side but 13, and the hostile sides are friends of one another but for 5 and 8, 5 and 12 and 9 and 15 both ways and 15 against 5 and 8; the table is not symmetric, the hostile sides being friends of the side 4, the horses', which is hostile to them.
`0x423580` also makes the type 75 (`med_spider.grn`) hostile to every creature but those of the types 75, 99 and 109, makes the types 189 and 334 hostile to the types 245 and 188 and the other way about (`0x423699` to `0x4236DB`; `opensacred-rules` since #2905, `minds::hostile`), takes a horse's rider's side for the horse, makes a creature of `+0x200` bit `0x10000000` the animals' foe (below), and turns its answer over for a creature of `+0x1F4` bit `0x40000`, which the adder of the effect 14 sets (the Seraphim's conversion and the Vampiress's master's bite, docs/re/arts.md, "The effects").

**The animals' foe** (`+0x200` bit `0x10000000`; read 2026-11-22 for #3077 in the code, with a scan of `Sacred.exe` for the stores and the tests of the bit, **confirmed** by code and by the tables, and **seen** in the running original).
The reading of #580, by which such a creature keeps friends with those of a list of its own, had the test the wrong way about.

- **What sets it.** The stats' recomputation (`0x5796A0`) clears the bit at its start (`0x579CF3`) and sets it in its loop of the items' bonus slots for the bonus 864 (`0x360`, the case `0x57A9AD` of the switch at `0x57A5B2`), whatever the bonus's value; nothing else stores it.
  So a creature has the bit while an item it wears or holds carries the bonus 864, whose one line in an item's tooltip is the text 1144 (docs/re/inventory.md, §9).
- **Which items.** Four items of `Weapon.pak` carry the bonus among their eight bonus slots, the uniques 7203, 7222, 7245 and 7325 (`tables/weapons.ron`), with the values 3, 2, 2 and 2, which nothing reads; none of the 572 weapon modifiers of `wpmod.bin` does, so no made item rolls it.
- **What it does.** `0x423580` reaches the test only where the table calls the two creatures friends (a pair that the table calls hostile skips every rule after it).
  There (`0x42370C`), a first creature of the bit against a second of the `Creature.pak` class 6, the animals' (`0x426A30`), is hostile unless the second's id stands in the first's list of followers (`+0x39C`, entries of 9 bytes, docs/re/ai.md, "Followers") or the second has a master (`+0x251` above 0); and the same the other way about, a second creature of the bit against a first of the class 6 (`0x423794`).
  The horse's rider's side and the turn of `+0x1F4` bit `0x40000` follow it.
- So the animals, which the table makes the friends of every side but 13, are the foes of a hero that wears one of the four items, and that hero theirs, but for the hero's own followers and the animals that follow another.
  Every reader of `0x423580` takes it: the cursor's choice of a living foe (docs/re/cursor.md), the strikes' and the arrows' tests (`0x548F60`), the looks.
- **Seen** 2026-11-22 under the driver of #448 (a Seraphim's new game at 3250,2500): a rabbit 516 of the side 6 made beside her was peaceful to her (the driver's `creatures`, which asks `0x549000`); with the item 7203 put into her hand (`wear 7203`, the slot 13) the same rabbit and the fills' rabbits about her were hostile to her, and a creature of the side 3 beside them peaceful as before.
- `opensacred-game` does not follow it: its hero strikes no animal, whatever it wears.

**A ridden horse's side** (read again 2026-11-22 for #3083, **confirmed** by code; what strikes one in play: docs/re/horses.md, "What strikes a ridden horse").
`0x423580(a, b)` asks whether `a` is hostile to `b`, and takes a rider's side for `b` alone, the one asked about: where `b` is of the horses' class 4 (`0x4266F0`) and a creature sits in its slot 18 (`+0x1EC`), and `a`'s side is a friend of `b`'s own by the table, the answer is the table's for `a`'s side against the rider's (`0x42381F`–`0x423846`).
It takes the place of everything read before it on that way, the types' rules (75; 189 and 334 against 245 and 188) and the friends' lists of `+0x200` bit `0x10000000`; the turning over by `+0x1F4` bit `0x40000`, of `a` or of the horse itself, not of its rider, comes after it.
Where the table has `a`'s side hostile to the horse's own, that stands and the rider is not asked: of the horses' side 4 only the side 13 is so, and a black rider's horse stands on its rider's side 5 already (docs/re/horses.md, "The black riders").
As `a`, the one that asks, a horse answers by its own side whoever rides it; the side 4's row is the heroes' row, friends of the sides 0, 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 and hostile to the others.
So a hero's horse is in every test a creature of the heroes' side while he rides it, and without a rider one that the heroes' foes take for a friend and that takes them for foes.
Which of the two is asked is each caller's own: `this.0x548F60(other)` and `this.0x549000(other's id)` ask whether `this` is hostile to `other`.
The creatures' fights ask it of themselves against their target (`0x4E0A06`, `0x4DC3D3`, `0x4E7B50`), as do the guards' search for the nearest foe (`0x4EEF02`), the spells' objects that test their caster against each creature in reach (`0x5AE5B3`, `0x5AFAD6`, `0x5AFBDA`, `0x5AFCBB`) and the gate of every hit, its attacker against the struck one (`0x531E62`): for those a ridden horse is a foe and an unridden one a friend.
The gate is where the rule tells in play: a foe's hit that reaches a hero's horse comes to nothing without a rider, and with one stands and is handed on to him, the horse's health untouched (**seen** 2026-11-22, docs/re/horses.md, "What strikes a ridden horse").
**Seen** again 2026-11-23 for #3083 with the god mode off (docs/re/horses.md, "What strikes a ridden horse"): the arrows of a fill's archers struck the rider himself in the sector where he mounted (37 strikes) and through his horse after he rode across a sector's border (37 strikes, hits of 131 to 145), the spells' hits of three orc shamans struck him twice in his mount's sector, once himself and once through the horse, and the horse's health never fell until a lethal blow felled it in his place.
So the rule takes no health of the horse: what it does in play is to let the arrows and the spells that find the horse rather than the rider, which is every one after a crossing, strike the rider, and, with the rider still found in the sector of his mount, to make a spell's hit there strike him twice.
An arrow asks it of the creature on its way against its shooter (`0x5BBD2A`), as do the spells' objects' gathering of their targets (`0x5AB818`, by `0x548F30`) and the fight's test of a straight line to a target (`0x4DB8ED`): there a horse answers by its own side, ridden or not.

A creature's side is its type's `Creature.pak` class (`+0x04`, through `0x43ADC0`), which its constructor (`0x52DE40`) gives it; `0x52E420` sets another, or the class again.
The creature maker `0x480D40`, which `FillSector` calls with each entry's hostility, puts a creature it makes hostile on the side 2 when its class is not hostile to the local hero or is the animals' (6), and sets bit 0 of its flags word; one it makes peaceful on the side 3 when its class is hostile to the hero and not the animals', and clears bit 0 (`0x480F24` to `0x4810A7`).
Read again 2026-10-25 (#984): first it sets bit 0 for a class that is no friend of the heroes' side 1 (`0x480F82`); then with the hostility −1, neither, it asks `0x549000` whether the creature, on its class's side, is hostile to the local hero, and makes it hostile so, else peaceful (`0x480FD5`), so that a fish of class 6, a crab of class 0 or a guard of class 7 comes out peaceful and a goblin hostile; the original's driver's `spawn` passes −1 unless told.

## Melee: the AI's state 4

`0x4E0300` keeps a creature within a band about its reach `R`: `R − 15` to `R + 15` (`R + 45` for a player's).
Deciding (sub-state 0):

- At a third of its health or less, within 199 of its target, not a boss nor of the classes 1 and 5, once a fight (`+0x1F4` bit `0x200`), in one case of eight a tick of the world while its target does not attack, it flees 700 from the target along the line.
  Where its target holds the stats' flag `0x4000` of set 58's six pieces (`UI_SET_FLUCHT`, docs/re/sets.md) and the target's level and `2·d + 2` (`d` the difficulty's index, `0x182EE44`) are at least the creature's level on bronze's scale (`0x5522D0`), it flees not: it stops (`0x54E6D0(1, 0)`) and sends itself the strike's event 5 from its target, of no weapon, the fixed damages 255, 0, 0 and 0, the hit's kind and word `0xFF` and the factor 1.0 (`0x4E1052`–`0x4E115A`), which the strike's resolution takes for an attack of 100,000 (`0x56D0A5`), a sure hit; the fight's flag is not set, so it may be struck down again (read 2026-11-11 for #2547, **confirmed** by code).
- Within the band it strikes (sub-state 2); outside it, it comes on (sub-state 1), at a run in 7 cases of 11 (`0x4E13C4`–`0x4E13E3`), and at the next tick, its move pointed at the target, a type not of class 5 (`0x426A80`) at a run in two cases of three more (`0x4E2102`–`0x4E215B`): a walk in some 4 of 33 (read 2026-11-10 for #2489, **confirmed** by code; **seen**: 9 of 10 come-ons of the start's outlaws ended at a run).
  The step back nearer than the band (`0x4E20DC`–`0x4E2102`) takes the same upgrade, and sets `+0x200` bit `0x800`; its point is the creature's own place mirrored about its target's, twice its place less the target's (`0x4E20EE`–`0x4E20FF`, read 2026-11-10 for #2513, **confirmed** by code), so that a creature on its target's own point steps to where it stands, a move that ends at once; every move of the sub-state 1, the step back too, leaves the creature in the sub-state 2 (`0x4E210A`), where it strikes once it stands ("In the band" below); a caster's walk to its band (`+0x128` 1) runs nearer than 240 for a type not of class 5 (`0x4E1C80`–`0x4E1CB3`), and farther than 300 walks to 270 at the gait above.
  A move begun before (an idle step, a walk home) the sub-state 1 points at the target at once (`0x4E2066`–`0x4E20FA`); only the creature's own flight, the sub-state 3, runs to its end.
- Then a creature that is the second, third or fourth of the hero's list of hostiles (`0xAD3CE0`, docs/re/motions.md, "The fight stance"; the list's count above 1) and stands nearer than `R − 15` to the first of that list takes sub-state 5 instead (`0x4E1404`).

Coming on (sub-state 1), it gives up farther than 2,000 (the AI's state 0); a creature of `+0x2B8` bit `0x10` takes a bow (`BOW_SHORT` or `BOW_LONG`, `0x4D4A80`) for a target beyond its reach + 100 and shoots; nearer than the band it steps back; else it walks or runs to the target, stopping within the band.
The bow's test is made in the sub-states 0, 1 and 2 alike (read 2026-10-21, #396; `0x4E0C63`, `0x4E1B78`, `0x4E43C5`), as soon as the distance to the target is taken, after the give-up beyond 2,000 in the sub-states 1 and 2; `0x4D4A80` takes the AI's state 8, deciding (sub-state 0), from which the creature shoots in its band ("At range" below).
**The leash** (`0x4E4023`–`0x4E419C`, read whole 2026-11-10 for #2489, **confirmed** by code): a creature not of class 1 (`0x426650`) and of no master (`+0x251` 0 or below), at its full health (`0x549460`), whose target is a player (or one a player controls, for a creature of an id above 16), whose home's sector (`+0x2B9`) is not the one it stands in (`+0x18`), and whose target stands farther than 533 (`0x5370B0`), walks home (`0x54E6D0(1, 0)`, the AI's state 2, its order to `+0x2BD`, `+0x2C1`, its floor `+0x2C5`) where its level `+0x3FE` less 3 is above the target's (`0x4B2770`) and it stands farther than 2,500 from home, or where it stands farther than 5,000 from home; never in melee, the target within 533.
In the band (sub-state 2) it strikes: the attack action 3 (`0x4E4CF0`) with its target, and the AI back to state 0, from which the idle handler, once the action ends, takes it into state 4 again.
A creature standing in sub-state 2 strikes even beyond the band when a straight line leads to the target; without one it looks for a way to the target (`0x4F7710`) and goes on (sub-state 0) when the way ends within `R + 80` of the target along each axis, else goes for a door ("Doors in the way"), and without a door gives up (the AI's state 0; `0x4E4931`).
That line is asked with the target's id and no flag (`0x4E4887`, read 2026-11-22 for #3069, **confirmed** by code), so that another creature on it stops it (docs/re/movement.md, "The straight way"): **seen** the same day, two orcs of the second ring about the hero on open grass found no line from three places, an orc of the first ring on the next cell of each.
A creature still moving (the action 1) in sub-state 2 is steered by its target alone: within the band it strikes at once, beyond it it walks straight to the target, nearer it steps back.

**The strike's end and its chain** (read 2026-10-22, #466, in the code and in the original frame by frame with the driver of #448).
The attack action has two halves: its sub-state 1, which plays the clip to the hit (the strike resolves as it turns into sub-state 2), and its sub-state 2, which plays it out.
As sub-state 2 ends, the creature strikes again at once (sub-state 1 anew, its target kept) when all of these hold, else it ends the action and its AI's state 4 takes the sub-state 6, from which the AI decides again:

- its type's class is not 1 (`0x426650`), unless the flag `0x20` of the global `0x182EE3C` is set, then in two cases of three;
- its target is alive, hostile to it and seen, and a straight line of walkable cells leads to it (`0x4DB8A0`, `0x4FCC80`, asked with its own id, so that no creature between stops it: docs/re/movement.md, "The straight way");
- its target stands nearer than `R + 30` (`0x5370B0`'s distance; `R` of `0x4DBC60`, the mean of the two types' sizes, 130 but for the types it names, the Seraphim's 100);
- its mark (`+0x25F`, a word that the creature's maker `0x52DE40` takes from its type by `0x43C540`) is 0, or `rand() & 3` is 0: a marked creature chains in one case of four.

The marked types: 34, 35, 41 to 48, 52, 53, 55 to 68, 70 to 76, 80, 81, 88, 94, 96 to 98, 100 to 103, 105, 118, 125 to 127, 130, 133, 140, 141, 143 to 146, 182 to 187, 190 to 192, 195, 202 to 208, 212, 216, 218, 219, 247 to 256, 258, 262 to 271, 304 to 306, 311, 312, 319 to 321, 326, 329 to 333, 335, 336, 342 to 344, 348, 350 to 354, 363, 512, 567 to 571, 573 to 583, 693, 697, 700 and 701; the goblin warriors (76) among them, of the mark `0x400` (observed).
The AI's state 4 then steps about every 0.1 s (observed: the sub-state 6 for 6 or 7 frames, the sub-state 2 for 6 or 7, then the strike): paced by the world's tick, the render thread's call of `0x60F130` every 0.1 s (docs/re/ai.md, "The world's tick"; **confirmed** by a probe, #617).
Observed with a goblin warrior of level 1 at the Seraphim standing 119.5 off (`R + 30` 145): the halves 38 to 40 frames each, the hit 633 to 733 ms into the strike; a chained strike 77 to 82 frames after the last one's start, one through the AI 89 to 93; 15 of 47 strikes chained (about the one in four), 1.49 s apart on average over 77 strikes.

Places (sub-state 5, one frame, read 2026-10-21, #383; `0x4E224E` to `0x4E23A4`):

- By default the creature's point is its target's position, its move to stop at `R` (`+0x174` 15, `+0x176` `R`, `+0x17E` the target), and it walks there (the action 1) when farther than `R + 15` from the target.
- In place of that, the second of the list takes the point `R − 15` from its target along the world's `+x`, the third along `−y` and the fourth along `+y` (while the list's count is above 1, 2 or 3), its move to stop within 40 of the point (`+0x174` 1, `+0x176` 40: in the AI's state 4 the move `0x4F4770` ends within `+0x176` of its point when `+0x174` is 1), walking there when the point is farther than `R + 15` from it.

## At range: the AI's state 8

`0x4DBFC0` keeps an archer 300 to 800 from its target: within 300, up to ten times a fight, it backs off; beyond 800 it comes on, stopping within 450; between, without a straight line, it moves to a point near the target, turned −30° to +30°; else it shoots (the action 5, "The archers" below).
Its chain `+0x210`, the factor of its shots' rate, rises where it is called to its fight again while a shot of its own plays ("The shots' chain" below).
It gives up farther than 2,000, and goes back to melee without a ranged weapon.
A shot ends in the AI's state 0, and the idle handler takes a target up again only within 800 along a straight line ("Who fights" above), so an archer gives up a target gone beyond 800 at its next decision and seldom comes on: **seen** 2026-10-25 (#227, check 26), a thief 308 of 50,41 that shot at the hero from about 630 dropped its target and went back to its idle steps as the hero walked out to about 875, and again to about 940 (#972).
From a shot's end to the next shot's start the creature goes through its idle state (read and **seen** 2026-11-21 for #3303, the driver of #448 probing `0x542B20`, `sdk_WAKE`, `0x547070`, `0x4E0300`, `0x4DBFC0` and `0x4DCB80` on an ice druid, a soldier 279 and four thieves 304; the ticks read again and laid out one by one 2026-11-23 for #3572, **confirmed** by code and **seen** on the ice druid, "The archers" below):

- the shot's end leaves it standing and dormant (the action 0, the AI's state 0 and sub-state 0, "The archers" below);
- the hero's look wakes no hostile creature that fights, in the AI's state 4 or 8 with a target (`+0x104`) or in the action 3 or 5 or the AI's state 6 or 11 with the action's target (`+0x158`), its shot among them (`0x5477EF`–`0x547839`), and the hero's update, the look within it, runs before every creature's in a tick (docs/re/ai.md, "The world's tick"), so the look of the end's own tick finds the shot still playing and wakes nothing: the first look of a later tick wakes the creature, one to three ticks after the end (the looks come 3, 3 and 2 ticks apart);
- its idle handler takes its target up in that same tick, the creature updated after the hero (`0x542B20`; the search of a way at `0x544217` for a creature with no ranged weapon in a hand): the state 8 with a ranged weapon in a hand (`0x544285`), else the state 4 (`0x54427A`), whose decision at the next tick takes the state 8 for a creature of the effects 16 or 17 and returns (`0x4E03CD`, docs/re/arts.md, "The Seraphim's");
- the state 8 decides at the tick after, and the shot it orders starts in that same tick, the update running the AI's switch and then the action's (`0x5375C0`).

So the next shot starts one tick after the look that wakes the creature with a ranged weapon in a hand and two ticks after it without, so two to four ticks after the last shot's end for an archer (0.20 to 0.45 s, the median 0.23 s over 140 shots of the soldier) and three to five for the druid, whose hands hold no ranged weapon (0.30 to 0.67 s, the median 0.43 s over 182).
The reading of #3303, a look at the end's own tick waking the creature and its idle handler at that tick or the next, gave the same totals; the druid's ticks were **seen** one by one for #3572 (the look one to three ticks after the end in 356 shots, its idle handler's search in the look's tick, the state 8 at the next and the shot at the one after in 355, "The archers" below), the archer's one tick from the look to the shot by code alone, which the soldier's two to four ticks fit.

**The shots' chain** (`+0x210`; read 2026-11-21 for #3313 in the code alone, with a scan of `Sacred.exe` for the stores to the field; **confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise).
The factor is a float that the creature's maker sets to 1.0 (`0x52DFB6` in `0x52DE40`) and that a shot's start and a strike's multiply into their clips' rates ("The archers" below; "Melee: the AI's state 4" above).

- **What a shot that plays is.**
  The decision reads the action and nothing else: `+0x150` is 5 and `+0x152` is not 0 (`0x4DC503`–`0x4DC514`), so the shot's action past its start, in its loose (1) or its end (2), which lasts from the tick at which the shot starts to the second tick after its arrow or the effect 16's bolt left, the first after the effect 17's second bolt ("The archers" below).
  It reads neither the arrow's object, nor the counter `+0x178`, nor the distance: an arrow that still flies after its shot's action has ended counts for nothing, and a shot whose arrow has not left yet counts.
- **The raise** (`0x4DC51A`–`0x4DC577`).
  While the factor is below 1.7 (the float at `0x890ED8`) it takes 0.1 more (the float at `0x8907DC`), in single precision: seven raises from 1.0, the last to 1.7000002.
  Raised or not, the AI takes the state 0 in its sub-state 0 again, the order's first three words (`+0x100`, the target `+0x104`, `+0x108`) go into the action's (`+0x154`, `+0x158`, `+0x15C`), so that the shot that plays turns to the order's target and an arrow not loosed yet flies at it, and the shot's flag `+0x18C` is set, which a hero's type alone reads.
  The shot that plays keeps its clip's rate, which an NPC's shot takes at its start alone (`0x4DCCFD`): the raise is the next shot's, and every later one's.
- **The reset in the state 8** (`0x4DC5B5`).
  In its shot not yet started (the action 5 in its sub-state 0) and in any action but 0, 1 and 5 (a strike's 3, a cast's 4, an art's), the decision sets the factor to 1.0 and returns in the state 8 and its sub-state 0, so again at each tick until the creature stands or moves.
  The update runs a creature's AI and then its action in one call (the two switches at `0x539833` and `0x539928` of `0x5375C0`), and a shot takes the sub-state 1 in the update that orders it, so that no call from another creature finds a shot in its sub-state 0.
  A decision taken standing or moving leaves the factor as it is: a shot ordered so neither raises nor resets it.
- **What sends an NPC to its fight while its shot plays.**
  The order of a shot leaves the AI in the state 0 (above); the idle handler's own choice of a target passes over a creature in the action 3 or 5 (`0x543076`–`0x54308B`), and the hero's look wakes none that fights (docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake").

  - the event `0x100` (`0x52FA63`, docs/re/ai.md, "Foes among the creatures"): a creature not in the AI's state 4 or 8 deciding takes the state 8 with a ranged weapon in a hand or `+0x200` bits `0x600000`, else 4, the event's creature its target; one that decides already ignores it, so that the calls between two of its updates raise once.
    Its group's alarm sends it (`0x46AE70`, docs/re/ai.md, "Groups") to each other member of the group in the AI's state 0, whatever its action (`0x46B17F`), so to one whose shot or strike plays too, where the member that raises the alarm fights: the AI's state 4 or 8 with a target, or the action 3 or 5 or the AI's state 6 or 11 with the action's target, which the event carries.
    The alarm is raised by a member that takes a target up in its idle handler, in one case of two (`rand() % 2` not 0, its state and target written before, `0x54428E`–`0x5442DC`); by a member that a strike's event reaches (`0x5337CE`, and once more in one case of three at the handler's tail, `0x535DA6`–`0x535DD3`); and by the idle action of a standing member whose group fights (`0x54EB19`), which fights no one itself and so only wakes the others (`sdk_WAKE`).
  - the awake idle handler, for a creature in the action 3 or 5 whose flags word `+0x1F4` has bit `0x2` (`CreateNPC`'s mode of the tag `0x13`, docs/02 §7.3) and of whose group another member fights (`0x46B370`): it takes that member's target, the hero in one case of three, in the state 8 with a ranged weapon in a hand, else 4 (`0x544303`–`0x5443F5`);
    one of a behaviour's flags (`0x1006DCF8`) takes its behaviour there instead (the AI's state 2, `0x544401`–`0x54440F`), and a guard's behaviour sends itself the event `0x100` (docs/re/ai.md, "Foes among the creatures"; not read again);
    any other goes dormant again, so that a wake in mid-shot, its own timer's or its group's, does nothing to a fill's creature of the flags word 1;
  - a strike taken (the tail of the event 5's handler, `0x535A72`–`0x535C45`, under a flag of the handler that was not read): an NPC hostile to the local hero and to its attacker, in none of the AI's states 1, 4, 6, 8 and 11 and with no script's order queued (`+0x588`), takes the state 8 with a ranged weapon in a hand, else 4, the attacker its target (docs/re/ai.md, "Foes among the creatures"), but one in the action 3 or 5 only from an attacker whose mark's (`+0x25F`) low byte has bit `0x80` (`0x535AC6`–`0x535B36`; of the maker's marks `0x1FE` and `0x1FF` alone, the types 133, 319 to 321, 326 and 701), which is none of a hero's type, to which the maker gives no mark (`0x43C540`);
  - the shot's own start, where the target of a creature not of a hero's type stands farther than 1,000 (`0x4DCE48`, "The archers" below): the state 8 decides at the next tick, the shot playing; no order of the state 8 itself gives such a shot, which shoots within 800.

## The archers

A creature of `+0x2B8` bit `0x10` takes a bow once (`0x4D4A80`): the state 8, its slot 13 emptied, `BOW_SHORT` (1732) or `BOW_LONG` (1733) at random made at its level and held in slot 12, the bit cleared.

## A strike's resolution

The event 5 (`0x531DC8`) goes to `0x56B130`; a target dead or dying, talking, or farther than 1,500 from the attacker ignores it.
The attack record (`0x568D70`) gives, for each of the four kinds of damage (physical, fire, magic, poison), a damage rolled between a least and a most, the four resistances, the attack `A` and the defence `D`, from the weapons in hand, the items' bonuses and `Creature.pak`'s bonuses; the stats' recomputation (`0x5796A0`) scales them by the level and the attributes (the balance's `BalStatOff`, `BalChar*`).
An NPC's own come from its `Creature.pak` record and its level (below).
The resolution makes two records (read 2026-11-22 for #3417, **confirmed** by code): the struck one's against its attacker (`0x56B6CC`), of which it takes the four resistances and `D`, but for a poison's own strike (the record's `+0x18` 17, `0x56B6AA`), for which it fills that record with zeros (`0x56B929`, "The poison" below); and, for a blow of no fixed damages alone, the attacker's against the struck one (`0x56B965`), of which it takes the four damages and `A`.
Each record adds its holder's items' bonus slots against the opponent's class, the struck one's side, `0x80` for one ranged and `0x81` for one that rides or is ridden, and the purple potion's part against the undead (docs/re/items.md, "The slots against a class of opponents (`0x568D70`)"); the rest of either record is read by nothing.

### The poison

Read 2026-10-28 (#1354), **confirmed** by code unless marked otherwise.

- After the hints of the kinds of damage, a strike that landed may poison its target (`0x56DFB3`): not a hit of the kind 17 or 18 (the event's `+0x18`; 17 the poison's own strikes, below), where the poison's damage as rolled `p` (`[esp + 0xE0]`, after `ProzDmg`) passes the target's poison resistance `q` (`[esp + 0x160]`, after `ProzRes`) by more than 99 (the float `0x891258`), or by more than a roll of `rand() % 100 + 1`, drawn only then.

- The adder (`0x582D50` at `0x583E40`, read 2026-10-28) takes a poison into the first effect 30 held: nothing where its source is that entry's (a creature's poison neither renews nor stacks on its own); else the entry's source becomes the new one's where its dose, value times seconds, is the larger, the value becomes `(v₁·s₁ + v₂·s₂)/max(s₁, s₂)` and the seconds the longer, its whole value staying; without one it is added at the list's end.

## An NPC's attack, defence and damage

Status: **confirmed** by code (#281) unless marked otherwise.
A creature's stats (`+0x3A8`) are made by `0x586C00` with its type: the six base attributes `b0`…`b5` (`+0x4A`…, `u16`) are `Creature.pak +0x0C`…`+0x11`, the extra points (`+0x1C`…) 0, the skill slots `Creature.pak +0x14`, `+0x15` and, for a type not of class 1, `+0x16`…`+0x1B`, the row of the table of `A` and `D` (`+0x126`) 6 (a hero's loader `0x585C60` sets 0–5).
The level's setter `0x564D60` gives an NPC (an id above 16) only its first `n` skill slots, each at level `⌊L/3⌋`: `n` is 0 below level 3, 1 for 3–6, 2 for 7–20, 3 for 21–35 and 8 above; then it recomputes the stats.

The attack record (`0x568D70`), after the slots against the opponent's class and the purple potion's part (docs/re/items.md, "The slots against a class of opponents (`0x568D70`)"), each value held at 0 and above, multiplies each kind's least and most by its multiplier, the resistances by theirs, and `A` and `D` by theirs, truncated when not 1.
Its product of `A` or `D` and its multiplier stays on the x87 unit up to its truncation (`__ftol`), never stored as a float.
It rolls each kind's damage as a whole number between its ends in hundredths when the physical most is below 100, in tenths below 1,000, in units below 10,000; a negative most counts as 1 and a negative least as 0.
In hundredths each end times 100 on the x87 unit is truncated toward 0 (`__ftol`, so the float 3.33, 3.3299999…, gives 332), and the damage is the whole number times the float 0.01 stored as a float; in tenths likewise by 10 and the float 0.1 (read 2026-10-23, #620).
From 10,000 it is the least plus a roll of 0 to 100 hundredths of the span, `(most − least)·r·0.01 + least`.
If every kind's damage is below 1, the physical one becomes 15; `ProzDmg` comes after the record, so it takes the rolled damage, 15 among them, and the step goes by the most before it.

The skills (`0x566070`) give, for a slot of level `s`, `v(s) = off + 2·(w − off)·(1 − 1/(1 + (s − 1)/S))` per cent from a triple of balance keys: the skill 2 raises every damage multiplier (`WKoff`…), 8 `A`'s and `D`'s, 9 `D`'s, more with a shield, 10 the regeneration and the health, 11 each resistance's; the weapon skills raise `A`'s and the attack speed while the creature holds the matching weapon.

Each `v(s)` is computed on the x87 unit and stored as a float; times the float 0.01 (`0x890CB0`, a little below 0.01) it is added to its multiplier, which is stored as a float again.
The recomputation adds the items' bonuses to `+0xE6` and `+0xEA` first, each stored in turn, then the effects', then calls `0x566070` for the skills in their slots' order.

## Health

A creature's health is its stats' (`+0x3A8`) `+0x130` (the creature's `+0x4D8`), its most `+0x12C` (`+0x4D4`) and the value shown `+0x128` (`+0x4D0`), which follows the health over time.
The most (`0x5658F0`) comes from `Creature.pak`'s base bytes `b0`, `b3` (`+0x0C`, `+0x0F`), the stats' attributes `a0` and `a3` (`+0x10`, `+0x16`) and the level `L`: `S = b0 + b3 + (L − 1)·b0/10 + (L − 1)·b3/10`, the divisions of integers truncated toward 0, `g = 1.5 + G · max(0, 60 − b0 − b3)` with `G` the float at `0x891298`, 0.0039963233, the most `⌊S^g · (a0 + a3)/S · 10/31⌋` in double precision, that is `⌊S^g · 10/31⌋` for a creature without extra points or items' attribute bonuses, whose `a0 + a3` is `S` (**confirmed** by code and by the vectors of `opensacred-oracle`, #363, #408; read before as `⌊(b0 + b2) · S^(g − 1) · 10/31⌋`), raised by the skill 10 by its per cent of the curve `HPoffVW`, `HP__sVW`, `HP__wVW` (4, 50, 130) and taken whole (#492; the Dwarf's 133 to 138 at level 1, as the original's), by `HPFakBoss` or `HPFakChamp`, and for a creature not a hero by a factor of its level between the balance's `HP_s1`…`HP_w5` points and by `ProzHP`; 0 becomes 100, and a new creature starts full.
The skill 10's raise of a most `m` is `⌊m·v·0.01 + m⌋` on the x87 unit (read 2026-10-23, #598): `v` the per cent of the skill's first slot at its level, computed there and kept, never stored as a float, and 0.01 the float at `0x890CB0`, a little below a hundredth; at the world's 53 bits (#589) a raise that comes to a whole number in real numbers falls just below it.
For example, the type 557 at level 82 has the most 2,850 before the factors and its skill 10 at level 27, `v` = 90.2105…: 5,420, where `⌊m·(1 + v/100)⌋` with a float's sum gives 5,421.

Each factor is taken whole in that order: the curve, the skill 10's raise, the boss's or champion's factor, an item effect's 104/100 (`+0x148` bit `0x80000`), 100 for 0, then the level's factor times `ProzHP` (read 2026-10-23, #579: the boss 577 of 55,39 at level 3, of the curve's 98, has `⌊⌊98 · 2.5⌋ · 0.8⌋ = 196` in the original, not `⌊⌊98 · 0.8⌋ · 2.5⌋ = 195`).
