# Decision log: deviations from the original

Every deliberate difference between the new game's behavior and Sacred Gold 2.28 is recorded here: formulas, balance, UI, engine limitations. Fidelity to the original is not a goal (PLAN.md, ADR-01), but deviations must be visible and justified.

| Date | Subsystem | Original | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-16 | Models (glTF) | A bone's scale/shear is a full 3×3 matrix; 361 bones of the models have shear | Superseded the same day by the next row: the glTF node kept the diagonal, which distorted posed wolves and other sheared rigs | glTF node transforms are translation, rotation and scale; shear cannot be stored in one node |
| 2026-10-16 | Models and animations (glTF) | A bone with shear in its bind pose or in a track (1,202 bones of the converted files) | Two nodes: an outer one with the translation, the rotation `R · U` and the scale `D` of `SS = U · D · Uᵀ`, and the bone's own node turned by `Uᵀ`; the bind pose is exact. A track whose scale/shear has shear (1,532 tracks) is sampled at its keys and at least every 1/60 s, glTF blending the samples linearly | glTF scales along a node's own axes only; the eigenvectors of an interpolated matrix cannot be interpolated by glTF, so those tracks are sampled |
| 2026-10-16 | Models (glTF) | Up to 7 bone influences on a vertex (60 vertices in 16 meshes) | The 4 largest, renormalized | `JOINTS_0`/`WEIGHTS_0` carry 4, and Bevy reads no more |
| 2026-10-16 | Models (glTF) | 2,476 triangle corners in 144 meshes use the normal `(0, 0, 0)` | The normal of their triangle | glTF requires unit normals; how the original lights such a corner is unknown |
| 2026-10-16 | Models (glTF) | 179 meshes are drawn only with unnamed color materials: bone shapes and other 3ds Max helpers | Not exported | Hypothesis: the game does not draw them either (openheilig's trace of `ELVE_SORCESS.GRN`); to be revisited if a helper turns out to be visible |
| 2026-10-16 | Animations (glTF) | A rotation curve blends unnormalized control quaternions and normalizes the result | A cubic spline through the normalized curve, with keyframes added where it strays by more than 0.001°; within 0.0025° of the original on every curve | glTF wants unit keyframe values, so the exact quadratic of unnormalized controls cannot be stored |
| 2026-10-16 | Animations (glTF) | Where a span's hemisphere turn differs from the previous span's, the original curve jumps at the key (1,242 keys of the converted files) | The glTF curve follows the later span | glTF curves are continuous; which side the game shows at the key is not known |
| 2026-10-16 | Animations (glTF) | A bone that no track of the playing clips moves takes its bind pose (Granny builds the pose from the rest pose every frame; hypothesis) | A clip holds every bone that another clip of the same file moves in its bind pose, with constant channels (21,384 bones) | An engine keeps what the last clip left on a bone that the next clip does not move |
| 2026-10-16 | Animations (glTF) | Tracks of helper bones that a model lacks (`Bip01 Footsteps`, camera and light targets, `*Nub` ends: 34,062 tracks in the model files) | Left out of that model's file; the motion's own file keeps them | glTF channels need a node of the file |
| 2026-10-16 | Animations (glTF) | The two `FX_*_IDLE_BH.GRN` give each bone a split and an interleaved track | The first track of a bone plays | glTF allows one channel per node and path; which track the game plays is not known |
| 2026-10-17 | Models (textures) | A texture whose name is not in `texture.pak` is `IGNORED` by the game's binding (community-reported); what it then shows is not known | The file's embedded image (26 textures: 18 VTex, 8 one-colour raw RGBA placeholders of weapons) | Without it citizens, farmers and zombies would have no texture; hypothesis: Granny keeps its own decoded image |
| 2026-10-17 | Rendering (`opensacred-game`) | Liquid cells are drawn with the frames of the sector's liquid materials, a frame over 4 × 4 cells, the frames cycling in 2,048 ms (docs/re/liquids.md); how the layers blend, and the clear waters' second layer, are not recovered | The materials flagged at `+0xCC` (the clear waters) at 70 % opacity over the bed, the rest opaque, no second layer; every surface fading toward the shore, each corner by the share of dry cells around it | The vertical slice needs water to read as water; the blending follows the original's once known |
| 2026-10-18 | Day and night (`opensacred-game`) | Each vertex of the world takes the colour of the night: a ramp of the light around it from the light sources, with 255 less the night's level added; the art is multiplied by it, and blended as it is drawn (docs/re/daynight.md) | The frame drawn is multiplied by the colour in a fullscreen pass, after everything but the interface, the light's level taken per pixel from the 32 nearest light sources (the light statics and the hero), and the grey ramp in the whole frame when the hero's sector is a dungeon | The world's materials (the ground's, the sprites', the models') are changed by pull requests that wait (#29, #38, #41, #51), and a pass over the frame needs none of them; 32 sources cover a screen of the start's lamps. What the original blends over darkened art (the liquids, the shadows) is darkened after the blending here |
| 2026-10-18 | Walking (`opensacred-game`) | The hero walks the walkable cells of its layer (docs/re/movement.md); a building's inside is a layer of the building's own cells, entered through its door; the way round an obstacle is found by the fewest steps from cell to cell in a 64 × 64 window | The cells' walkability and the search of the original, on the ground and on the buildings' inside layers (the sectors' third chunk), from one to the other at a cell walkable on both (a door, every door open; an opening of the monastery, whose layer has no door); the way does not slip between two cells that touch at a corner, nor a straight line; the hero heads for the farthest cell of the way on its level that it reaches in a straight line; a target it cannot reach it runs straight toward, up to what blocks it. The hero's height inside is the ground's. The NPCs and the objects do not block | The doors' triggers have no states in the game yet; how the client changes the hero's layer at a door, walks between the cells of a way, goes round the creatures and treats a target out of reach is not read; the inside layers' second heights (upper floors, hypothesis) are not used |
| 2026-10-17 | Rendering (`opensacred-game`) | The ground first, then the objects in five draw lists, each by cell diagonals (docs/02 §6.1) | The depth buffer: every sprite is a quad in the plane of the screen at the depth of its cell's centre, the ground lies behind all objects, the underlay types (lists 0 and 2, by the mask) lie between, and list 4 comes 1,000 units forward | The hero is a 3D model that must interleave with the sprites; a large sprite owned by one cell can still be ordered wrongly against the hero |
| 2026-10-18 | Rendering (`opensacred-game`) | A creature or an object of the world is drawn at its cell's place in the draw lists, after the cell's statics, and an ordinary sprite without the depth test (docs/re/drawing.md, "The models") | A model's vertices take the depth of the centre of the cell its root stands in, after the cell's statics, plus a hundredth of their own depth from it (`models.wesl`); a weapon takes its holder's; every model goes in list 3 | The sprites are ordered through the depth buffer (the row above): at their true depth, the models were cut by every sprite whose plane crossed them (#48). A hundredth keeps a model's parts a tenth of a unit's steps of the depth buffer apart and the model within half a unit of its place, so only the sprites of its own diagonal within a few cells can still cross it |
| 2026-10-17 | Rendering (`opensacred-game`) | A character's shadow is its mesh flattened onto the ground, drawn through the stencil (docs/05 §1) | A soft dark ellipse under each character, in the original's place for shadows, draw list 1 (over the ground, the liquids and the underlays of list 0, under those of list 2 and every other object) | The flattened mesh needs its own pass; the ellipse grounds the characters until then |
| 2026-10-17 | Sound (`opensacred-game`) | Each region has its music; how the original picks it is not recovered (docs/02 §10.3) | The tracks of the `music` profile's first group (`MUSIC_WOOD01`–`03`) play one after another everywhere, a random one first, fading in | The starting region's woods fit them; the regions' music replaces them once recovered |
| 2026-10-17 | Interface (`opensacred-game`) | The original lays its interface out in code from the `gui_main_*` atlases (docs/02 §11) | A minimal HUD of whole textures: the portrait, a health bar and the gait in the upper left, scaled with the window's height | The atlases' layout is not recovered; the interface is redesigned in M5 with the original's textures as skins |
| 2026-10-17 | Sound (`opensacred-game`) | The ambient loop follows the nature underfoot by day and by night, and some regions and sieged villages have their own (docs/re/sound.md) | The day's loops by the nature, always, on a channel of their own beside the music; no night loops and no regions' loops | The game has no day and night and no regions yet; the client plays the loops and the music through one function, `cMSS::playMusic`, and whether both sound at once is not read |
