# The creatures' weapons, before their bonuses

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#309).

## The original

A creature's items are made by the item maker at the level of its fill or its creature (docs/re/items.md): their damage, resistances, `A` and `D` by their templates, a magic bonus, the triples of class bits and skills, a quality pass that adds bonuses and sockets; a fixed item keeps its own record.
An armed creature fights with its items' values instead of `Creature.pak`'s damage bonuses.

## Ours

The items in an NPC's hands are made at its level by their templates on bronze, with the same rolls each time the NPC is made, and their damage, resistances, `A` and `D` go into its values.
The magic bonus, the triples, the quality pass and the item's price are left out; an item without a template, a fixed one among them, gives nothing, and an NPC armed with no made weapon keeps the default damage of an unarmed one.
The hero holds nothing yet.

## Why

The magic bonus's part in the fight is partly read (whether the recomputation adds an unconditional bonus as the attack record adds a conditional one), and the quality map is not read; a creature's weapon is most of its damage, and its making is read.
The items of the hero come with its inventory.
