# The gameboxes named by their art

Texts (`opensacred-convert text`), 2026-10-25 (#686); under the maintainer's decision of [2026-10-23-text-repairs](2026-10-23-text-repairs.md).

## The original

The eleven gamebox items, 5150 to 5160, are named by the strings 77105259 to 77105268 and 77105372, the hashes of their numbers, which all hold the same single word, their records' internal name as well (docs/02 §8).
Each has a model of its own, 953 to 963 (`gamebox_*.grn`), a box whose art is that of another game, by its model's name and its texture.
Where the items are found and whether the player carries them is not read.

`opensacred-convert text` names each box by its art, without naming the game it shows:

## Why

One word for eleven different boxes tells them apart nowhere they show.
A box names another product, a title and a trademark of its owner, so the names describe what the box shows rather than what it sells (#686), as the project's own words (docs/02 §8).
The art was read from the models' textures in the converter's output, which the repository does not carry.
The maintainer changes any of them through an issue.
