# The hero's levels, before the character sheet

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#300).

## The original

The hero's kills give it experience, and the experience levels (docs/re/fight.md, "The hero's levels"): each level gives an attribute point and skill points that the player spends on the character sheet, opens new skills to learn at some levels, recomputes the hero's values, refills its health, raises its companions, and shows a level-up effect.
A kill's experience weighs the difficulty, the hero's level, the killer's bonuses, the bosses and champions and the party.

## Ours

The experience, the levels by it and the points of each level follow the original; the points are counted, but there is no character sheet to spend them on, so the attributes grow only by the level, and the hero's skills stay at level 1.
A new level makes the hero's values and most health anew and refills its health; there is no level-up effect, no companion and no message but the log's.
A kill's experience has bronze's cap, decay and fade, without the multiplayer, the killer's bonuses, the bosses and champions (#284) or the party.
The creatures take the hero's level when they are made, as the original's do, so those already made keep theirs.

## Why

The character sheet and the skills' choice are the interface of M5; the multiplayer and the parties are not in the game, the killer's bonuses need the items (#294), and the bosses and champions come with #284.
A hero that grows by its kills is what the fight needs next, and its rules are read.
