# Walking

Walking (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-18; changed 2026-10-20 (#212, #216); brought up to date 2026-10-23 (#692); the course kept from the order 2026-10-25 (#981, #989).

## The original

The hero walks the walkable cells of its layer (docs/re/movement.md); a building's inside is a layer of the building's own cells, entered at its entrances and left at its exits, cells marked in the low nibble of their ground byte, and its floors are layers above it, reached by cells marked so too; the way round an obstacle is found by the fewest steps from cell to cell in a 64 × 64 window.

## Ours

The cells' walkability and the search of the original, on the ground and on the buildings' inside layers (the sectors' third chunk), onto a building's inside at its entrances and out at its exits as the original goes, a door that names a trigger opening and closing its trigger's cells with it, so that a closed door keeps the creatures out until it opens (#372, docs/re/movement.md, "In `opensacred-game`"); the way does not slip between two cells that touch at a corner, nor a straight line; the hero heads for the farthest cell of the way on its level that it reaches in a straight line over the way's own cells; a target it cannot reach it runs straight toward, up to what blocks it.
The course, straight on or the way, is found once as the hero or an NPC takes its order, as the original's is (#981, #989), not anew at every frame; the original's line from cell to cell is tested between the cells' centres as well as from where the walker stands.
The hero stands where the original's creatures do, on the second corner heights of its level's cells, a bridge's deck over the water, and on a building's upper floor at the floor's height above them (its object's `+0x33`, #120, #141); it climbs the stairs between a building's floors as the original's creatures do, but no teleports, of which the files have none (#104).
The creatures stop the hero's and the NPCs' steps, and the cells that the trigger patches close stop every creature's, as the original's (#212, #216; docs/re/movement.md, "In `opensacred-game`"); the goals and the followers are not followed.
Until 2026-10-20 the NPCs and the objects did not block, and until #372 every door counted as open, its state changing no cells.

## Why

How the client walks between the cells of a way, goes round the creatures and treats a target out of reach is not read.
