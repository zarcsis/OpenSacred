# Walking

Walking (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-18.

## The original

The hero walks the walkable cells of its layer (docs/re/movement.md); a building's inside is a layer of the building's own cells, entered through its door; the way round an obstacle is found by the fewest steps from cell to cell in a 64 × 64 window.

## Ours

The cells' walkability and the search of the original, on the ground and on the buildings' inside layers (the sectors' third chunk), from one to the other at a cell walkable on both (a door, every door open; an opening of the monastery, whose layer has no door); the way does not slip between two cells that touch at a corner, nor a straight line; the hero heads for the farthest cell of the way on its level that it reaches in a straight line; a target it cannot reach it runs straight toward, up to what blocks it.
The hero's height inside is the ground's.
The NPCs and the objects do not block.

## Why

The doors' triggers have no states in the game yet; how the client changes the hero's layer at a door, walks between the cells of a way, goes round the creatures and treats a target out of reach is not read; the inside layers' second heights (upper floors, hypothesis) are not used.
