# The hostile creatures come at the hero, but do not strike yet

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#279). It replaces in part docs/decisions/2026-10-20-idle.md.

## The original

An awake hostile creature that passes the test of the levels, or has the hero within its reach, attacks a hero within 800 to whom a straight line of walkable cells leads, or else seeks a door or a way to it (docs/re/fight.md, "Who fights"); in the AI's state 4 it keeps the band of its reach, the mean of two sizes of the types, and strikes in it; it flees when hurt, keeps a leash home, and shoots from afar with a ranged weapon.

## Ours

A hostile creature comes at the hero and keeps the band of a reach of 130, the default size's, for every type, and stands there turned to the hero: it does not strike until #280.
Without a straight line it does not seek a door or a way, and goes on stepping about as before; it neither flees nor keeps a leash, and an archer comes as close as the others (#284).

## Why

The types' sizes, the strikes and the hits come with the next features of the fight (#280, #282); a creature that comes at the hero shows the original's fight beginning, which the steps about of the earlier decision did not.
