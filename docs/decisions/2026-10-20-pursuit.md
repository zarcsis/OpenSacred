# The hostile creatures come at the hero

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#279); changed 2026-10-22 (#466) and 2026-10-23 (#642).
Replaces in part [2026-10-20-idle](2026-10-20-idle.md).

## The original

An awake hostile creature that passes the test of the levels, or has the hero within its reach, attacks a hero within 800 to whom a straight line of walkable cells leads, or else seeks a door or a way to it (docs/re/fight.md, "Who fights"); in the AI's state 4 it keeps the band of its reach, the mean of two sizes of the types, and strikes in it; it flees when hurt, keeps a leash home, and shoots from afar with a ranged weapon.

## Ours

A hostile creature comes at the hero and keeps the band of their reach, the mean of their types' sizes as the original's (#642; until then 130, the default size's, for every type), and strikes there turned to the hero (#280), its attack's clip at its attack speed (#306) without the items' and the weapon skills' parts (#294); its hits did nothing to the hero until it had its health (#292; docs/decisions/2026-10-20-hits.md).
Without a straight line it does not seek a door or a way, and goes on stepping about as before; an archer comes as close as the others (#284).
A wounded creature flees as the original's (#304), and the leash takes it home farther than 2,500 from it, the nearer of the original's two distances, which of them holds when is not read.
As a strike ends, the next chains or waits as the original's do (#466, docs/re/fight.md, "The strike's end and its chain"), the chain's own tests of the distance and of a straight line left out, since a creature strikes in its band alone; the wait is 0.2 s, the original's AI's two steps of about 0.1 s, while our minds decide at every frame.

## Why

The doors in the way and the archers come with #284; a creature that comes at the hero shows the original's fight beginning, which the steps about of the earlier decision did not.
