# The liquids

Rendering (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17; narrowed 2026-10-19 (#103, #118).

## The original

Liquid cells are drawn with the frames of the sector's liquid materials, a frame over 4 × 4 cells, the frames cycling in 2,048 ms, each cell's surface flat at height 0 and each corner as opaque as the bed is deep there (docs/re/liquids.md).
At `DETAILLEVEL` 2 the clear waters have a second layer under their surfaces, added to the frame, brighter over the deeper bed: the frame that the liquids' batcher kept from the frame before, of the material of the last liquid cell drawn then, each quad's corners moved 0.2 pixels inward over the zoom (docs/re/liquids.md).
By night each corner of a surface takes the light at its point of the screen.

## Ours

The surfaces as the original's; by night the frame's pass darkens them with the rest of the world (docs/decisions/2026-10-18-night.md).
The second layer takes each cell's own material's frame of the moment, and its quads' corners lie on their beds' corners.

## Why

The original's second layer takes another cell's frame by the order of its batcher, which shows only where two materials meet in a frame, a frame behind.
The game's quads share their edges, so the rasterizer adds each pixel at a cell's edge once, without the original's inward corners, whose fifth of a pixel is not seen.
