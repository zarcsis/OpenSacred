# The liquids

Rendering (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17.

## The original

Liquid cells are drawn with the frames of the sector's liquid materials, a frame over 4 × 4 cells, the frames cycling in 2,048 ms (docs/re/liquids.md); how the layers blend, and the clear waters' second layer, are not recovered.

## Ours

The materials flagged at `+0xCC` (the clear waters) at 70 % opacity over the bed, the rest opaque, no second layer; every surface fading toward the shore, each corner by the share of dry cells around it.

## Why

The vertical slice needs water to read as water; the blending follows the original's once known.
