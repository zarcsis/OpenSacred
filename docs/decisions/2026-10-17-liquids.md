# The liquids

Rendering (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17; narrowed 2026-10-19 (#103).

## The original

Liquid cells are drawn with the frames of the sector's liquid materials, a frame over 4 × 4 cells, the frames cycling in 2,048 ms, each cell's surface flat at height 0 and each corner as opaque as the bed is deep there (docs/re/liquids.md).
At `DETAILLEVEL` 2 the clear waters have a second layer under their surfaces, added to the frame, brighter over the deeper bed (#118).
By night each corner of a surface takes the light at its point of the screen.

## Ours

The surfaces as the original's, without the clear waters' second layer; by night the frame's pass darkens them with the rest of the world (docs/decisions/2026-10-18-night.md).

## Why

The second layer's texture is not followed: the pass binds none of its own (#118).
