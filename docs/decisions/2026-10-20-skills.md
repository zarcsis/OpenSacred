# The creatures' skills, before the weapons' and the combat arts

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#293); brought up to date 2026-10-23 (#692).

## The original

The skills (`0x566070`) give each counted slot a per cent by its curve: the skill 2 on the damages, 8 on the attack and the defence, 9 on the defence, 10 on the regeneration and the health, 11 on the resistances, 12 and 14 on the combat arts, and the weapon skills on the attack and the attack speed while the creature holds the matching weapon (docs/re/fight.md, "An NPC's attack, defence and damage").
An NPC counts its first slots by its level, at a third of it; a hero its slots at the levels it has learned.
Every creature regenerates by its value, the skill 10 raising it.

## Ours

The skills 2, 8, 9, 10 and 11 count, the skill 10 on the regeneration and, since #492 and #498, on the most health (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-health.md); so do the skills that shorten the combat arts' times, on them (#507, docs/re/arts.md, "The regeneration"), and of the weapon skills the fist fight's, 24, for an unarmed creature (#592); the combat arts' 12 and 14, the other weapon skills and the riding do not.
A hero counts its type's two slots at level 1 (**hypothesis**: the template's levels are not read).
An NPC regenerates at `RegInFight` all the time, as the original's rate for one.

## Why

The skills that count are the common ones (2, 11, 8, 9 and 10 make most of the slots in the data), whose effects are read; the other weapon skills need the weapons' predicates and the items (#294).
