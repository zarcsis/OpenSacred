# The jump's rate at 495 to 500

The combat arts (`opensacred_rules::arts::jump_rate`, `opensacred-game`'s `making`), 2026-10-24 (#754).

## The original

The Seraphim's jump 1017 plays its clip at a rate by her distance `d` from her target (`0x5038F0` at `0x505C4C`): 5 within 10, 1 beyond 500, and `5 − ⌊d/99⌋` from 10 to 500 (docs/re/arts.md, "The Seraphim's").
From 495 to 500 that is 0: the clip stands still, and the art waits on its points (what is left of the clip below 0.75 and 0.4) that never come, until another order ends it.

## Ours

The rate is at least 1, so that a jump from 495 to 500 plays as one from 396 to 494.

## Why

A clip that stands holds the hero in her art, which plays as a defect of the original rather than a rule; the six units where it happens lie at the edge of a jump's ordinary distances, and the rate beside them is 1.
