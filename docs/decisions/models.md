# Models

The converter's models and animations, GRN → glTF, and their textures (docs/02-data-formats.md §4; no docs/re file covers them).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith's apron made opaque | `opensacred-convert textures` writes texture 8202, the blacksmith's apron, with every texel opaque and its colour unchanged, where two strokes of alpha 0 cut the flaps below the belt. | The strokes cut through the flaps' middle over a colour painted whole beneath them, a mask rather than a design; the maintainer's choice (#401). | repair | #401 |
| Bones no clip moves held at bind pose | Each clip holds every bone that another clip of the same file moves, at its bind pose, as constant channels (21,384 bones), where Granny builds the pose from the rest pose every frame (**hypothesis**). | An engine keeps what the last clip left on a bone that the next clip does not move. | limitation |  |
| Dwarf's cannon strap on the clavicle | The five positions of the strap of the dwarf's leather cannon (model 1415) weighted wholly on `Bip01 Pelvis` are weighted wholly on `Bip01 L Clavicle`, the positions unchanged. | The strap's sides from them stretched up to 22 times in the dwarf's clips; the clavicle is the bone that 10 of the 13 sides from those positions run to, and the play does not change. | repair | #399 |
| Dwarf's earth amulet on the chest | The dwarf's earth amulet (model 792) gets its siblings' `Root` as the parent of `Bip01`, and its 46 positions are bound rigidly to `Bip01 Spine3`, where the original binds them to a bone of its own that the dwarf's skeleton lacks. | It then sits on the dwarf's chest where the other 18 amulets sit, rather than 0.34 units off, and the play does not change. | repair | #398 |
| First of a bone's two tracks played | Where the two `FX_*_IDLE_BH.GRN` give a bone both a split and an interleaved track, the first plays. | glTF allows one channel per node and path, and which track the original plays is not known. | limitation |  |
| Forearms spelled one way | `opensacred-convert models` writes the forearms' two spellings, `ForeArm` in 176 files and `Forearm` in the others, as `Forearm` in every file's nodes and clip bindings, where the client looks a shield's bone up under both (`0x44C670`). | A clip played on another file's skeleton finds its bones by the chain of their names, which one spelling keeps alike; no skeleton has both, and only a shield's place on its forearm changes. | repair | #355 |
| Four bone influences a vertex | A vertex keeps its 4 largest bone influences, renormalized, where the original allows up to 7 (60 vertices in 16 meshes). | `JOINTS_0`/`WEIGHTS_0` carry 4, and Bevy reads no more. | limitation |  |
| Helper bones' tracks in the motions' files | Tracks of helper bones that a model lacks (`Bip01 Footsteps`, camera and light targets, `*Nub` ends: 34,062 tracks in the model files) are left out of that model's file and kept in the motion's own. | glTF channels need a node of the file. | limitation |  |
| Helper meshes left out | The 179 meshes drawn only with unnamed colour materials, bone shapes and other 3ds Max helpers, are not exported. | **Hypothesis**: the original does not draw them either (openheilig's trace of `ELVE_SORCESS.GRN`); to be revisited if a helper turns out to be visible. | deviation |  |
| Hemisphere turns follow the later span | Where a rotation span's hemisphere turn differs from the previous span's (1,242 keys), the glTF curve follows the later span, where the original's jumps at the key. | glTF curves are continuous, and which side the original shows at the key is not known. | limitation |  |
| Instruments' socket renamed | The four instruments that the jugglers play (models 746 to 749) have their socket `Weapon_bone01` or `Weapon_Bone01` renamed `Bone_weapon_01`, the right hand's socket of every other item. | The game then attaches an instrument by the right hand like any item; how the client attaches them is a **hypothesis**, and the play does not change. | repair | #353 |
| Large dragons' tails blended into the thighs | The tail roots of the eight large dragons (models 1193 to 1200) are blended into the thighs over two rings of positions, 2/3 and then 1/3 of the tail, the positions unchanged. | The root weighted wholly to a thigh tore into long thin triangles, stretched 10.6 times in the dying clip; with the rings no side there stretches more than the rest of the mesh's most, 3.9 times. | repair | #400 |
| Missing textures from the embedded images | A texture whose name is not in `texture.pak` takes the file's embedded image (26 textures: 18 VTex, 8 one-colour raw RGBA placeholders of weapons), where the original's binding ignores it (**community-reported**) and what it then shows is not known. | Without it some townsfolk, the farmers and the zombies would have no texture; **hypothesis**: Granny keeps its own decoded image. | repair |  |
| Rotation curves as cubic splines | A rotation curve becomes a cubic spline through the normalized curve, keys added where it strays by more than 0.001°, within 0.0025° of the original on every curve, where the original blends unnormalized control quaternions and normalizes the result. | glTF wants unit keyframe values, so the exact quadratic of unnormalized controls cannot be stored. | limitation |  |
| Sheared bones as two nodes | A bone whose scale/shear has shear (1,202 bones) becomes two nodes, an outer one with the translation, the rotation `R · U` and the scale `D` of `SS = U · D · Uᵀ` and the bone's own turned by `Uᵀ`, so that the bind pose is exact. A track whose scale/shear has shear (1,532 tracks) is sampled at its keys and at least every 1/60 s, where the original interpolates the whole matrix. | glTF scales along a node's own axes only and cannot interpolate the eigenvectors of an interpolated matrix; the diagonal alone in one node distorted the posed wolves and other sheared rigs. | limitation |  |
| Zero normals take their triangle's | The 2,476 triangle corners in 144 meshes whose normal is `(0, 0, 0)` take their triangle's normal. | glTF requires unit normals, and how the original lights such a corner is not known. | limitation |  |
