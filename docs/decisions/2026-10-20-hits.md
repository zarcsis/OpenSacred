# A strike's hit, before the skills, the weapons and the hero's health

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#282); changed 2026-10-20 (#300), 2026-10-22 (#470), 2026-10-26 (#656).

## The original

A hit (docs/re/fight.md, "A strike's resolution") weighs the attacker's attack, damage and the defender's defence, resistances and damping, which the stats' recomputation makes from the attributes, the level, `Creature.pak`'s bonuses, the skills and the items in hand and worn (docs/re/fight.md, "An NPC's attack, defence and damage"), and the difficulty's factors on the strikes between a player and a creature.
Criticals, life leech and the skills' specials follow; the health falls, the number shows over the target, and a creature at 0 dies: its dying action, its death's scripts, its group left, its killer's experience.
The creatures regenerate their health.

## Ours

An NPC's attack, defence, damage and resistances are its attributes', its level's, its bonuses' and its skills' (#293, docs/decisions/2026-10-20-skills.md), with the values of the items in its hands (#309, docs/decisions/2026-10-20-weapons.md).
The hero took no damage and did not strike until #292 and #283 (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-hero-health.md, docs/decisions/2026-10-20-hero-attack.md).
No criticals, life leech or skills' specials; the drops since #414 and the deaths' scripts since #656 (docs/re/netscript.md, "The deaths' functions"), and the experience of the hero's kills alone (#300, docs/decisions/2026-10-20-levels.md); a dead creature lies where it fell until its sector goes out, and comes back whole as the sector comes back (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-spawning.md, #329).
The number of the damage rises over the target as the original's is seen to (#470): yellow, or red over the hero, for 2 s, 30 pixels a second at zoom 1, at full strength for 0.7 s and fading after; the original's code of it is not read, so its times are the frames' (gone 1.5 to 2.2 s after the hit), and its font is the interface's.
Until #470 it rose for a second in a light orange.

## Why

The skills, the items' generation and the hero's own values are features and research of their own (#292–#294); a hit that takes a creature's health down and lays it dead is what the fight needs next, and the values it takes are the original's as far as they are read.
