# The animals' chances in time, and what waits

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#238).

## The original

The peaceful creatures' behaviours (docs/re/ai.md, "The peaceful creatures' behaviours") run at every frame while the creature stands, and take their chances there: an animal strolls about home in one frame of 101.
A wild animal flees farther, 800, from a creature that attacks; a flyer on the ground takes off from one within 200; an animal whose cell another shares strolls too.

## Ours

The chance of a stroll is a rate in time, 30 in 101 a second, as the frame's chance gives at 30 frames a second (**hypothesis** for the original's rate): at any frame rate an animal strolls about every 3.4 s.
No creature attacks yet, so the fear reaches 300 (200 for a farm animal) only; the flyers flee on foot as the others; the stroll of a crowded cell comes with #240.

## Why

A chance taken at every frame would make the animals of a fast machine restless and those of a slow one still; the original's frame rate is not read (docs/re/ai.md, "Open"), and a rate keeps the chance whatever it is.
The attacks come with the fight's rules (M3), the flights with the flyers' layer, which the creatures do not have yet; the crowded cells belong with the others' stepping aside (#240).
