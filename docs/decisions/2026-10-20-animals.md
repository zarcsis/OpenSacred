# The animals' chances in time, and what waits

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#238); changed 2026-10-20 (#240) and 2026-10-23 (#608).

## The original

The peaceful creatures' behaviours (docs/re/ai.md, "The peaceful creatures' behaviours") run at every tick of the world's update while the creature stands, every 6 or 7 frames at 60 frames a second, 108 ms on average (#616), and take their chances there: an animal strolls about home in one tick of 101.
A wild animal flees farther, 800, from a creature that attacks; a flyer on the ground takes off from one within 200; an animal whose cell another shares strolls too.

## Ours

The chance of a stroll is a rate in time, the tick's chance at its 108 ms on average, at any frame rate about one stroll in 11 s (#608; until then 30 in 101 a second, as a frame's chance at 30 frames a second would give).
The fear reaches 300 (200 for a farm animal) only, also from a creature that attacks; the flyers flee on foot as the others; an animal whose cell another shares strolls at once (#240).

## Why

A chance taken at every frame would make the animals of a fast machine restless and those of a slow one still, and the game has no world's tick yet (#617); a rate keeps the tick's chance whatever the frame rate.
An attacker's 800 is not followed yet, though the creatures fight the hero now (#279, #283); the flights wait for the flyers' layer, which the creatures do not have yet.
