# The drops without uniques, set pieces and the bosses' own

Drops (`opensacred-rules`), 2026-10-21 (#414).

## The original

A killed creature calls the drop as many times as its rank and the time played give, and each call's passes make potions, runes, gold, weapons, armour and jewels by the balance's chances, of a quality key by the weights of the tail of `Balance.bin` and of a type that `0x5C7FA0` chooses by the category, the level and the templates (docs/re/items.md, "Drops").
Besides these:

- a rune (category 28) comes before the gold, by a chance of the hero's level, its class and the runes it holds;
- the key 0 makes, in 30 % of the passes, a unique of the item's family, and the key 1 a piece of a set for the hero's class, both raised to the creature's level;
- some twenty bosses' types add their own bonus and calls, make treasures and particular items (`0x533E2E`);
- the scripts' `SetDrop` records give a creature type an item at a chance in place of a call's random drops, and a hero who wears more than four pieces of a set gets a potion in one call of ten;
- in a network game the members of the killer's party near it raise the chance by `MPThrowfactor` each;
- a killer of the effect 868 makes a healing potion after the calls, by its value, and a creature of the effect 856 knocks gold out of what it strikes.

## Ours

`opensacred_rules::drops` follows the calls, their bonus by the rank, the time played and the luck, the chance, the passes and their tries, the bonus compounded by the creature's worth, the quality key, the potions, the runes (since #1183, by the hero's level, its class, the runes in its backpack and its arts' levels), the gold with its pile and amount, the weapons, the armour, the jewels, the type choice and the item's levels and key as the original's, and makes the items' values by the item maker.
But:

- The runes the hero carries are those of its backpack alone, the game having no storage.
- No uniques and no set pieces: the key 0 makes the item with the key 14 and the key 1 the item with the key 2, as the original does where it finds no unique or set piece; the set pieces are kept out of the type choice as in the original.
- No bosses' drops by their type: a boss or a champion takes the common bonus and calls of its rank, and the types 343 and 212 of the fills, which the original gives a champion's levelling without the rank, drop as the common creatures the game makes them.
- No `SetDrop` and no set's potions: every call draws its random drops.
- No party's factor, the game having no network play, and no effects 856 and 868, the game having no effects on its creatures yet.
- The time played, the luck and the treasure of the effect 42 are what the game passes; it has no effects, so the luck is 100 and the treasure 0.
- The hero can wear an item when the item's class bits allow the hero's type, its least level is at most the hero's and a skill it needs the hero has at its level; the item's own test of the hero's type (`0x54ABE0`'s virtual call) is not read, and the attributes it would test are 0 in every record that the drops choose from.
- The rolls come from the game's generator, not from the C library's `rand()`, so a death drops what the original's might, not what it would.

## Why

The uniques and the set pieces have their own makers and the set bonuses, and the bosses' cases are a table compiled into the client whose values docs/re leaves for the maintainer (docs/re/items.md, "How many drops, and the bonus"): none of them has a counterpart in the game yet.
`SetDrop` serves the quests, which the game does not run.
The fallbacks are the original's own, so a drop of the key 0 or 1 is still an item of its kind and level, and the keys 0 and 1 come in 1.6 % of the passes of a creature that weighs 1.
