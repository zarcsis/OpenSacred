# The characters' shadows

Rendering (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17.

## The original

A character's shadow is its mesh flattened onto the ground, drawn through the stencil (docs/05 §1).

## Ours

A soft dark ellipse under each character, in the original's place for shadows, draw list 1 (over the ground, the liquids and the underlays of list 0, under those of list 2 and every other object).

## Why

The flattened mesh needs its own pass; the ellipse grounds the characters until then.
