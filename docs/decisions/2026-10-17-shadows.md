# The characters' shadows

Rendering (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17.
Replaced by [2026-10-19-model-shadows](2026-10-19-model-shadows.md) (#101).

## The original

A character's shadow is its mesh flattened onto the ground, drawn through the stencil (docs/05 §1), with the model, in the model's place in the draw lists (`cGranny::renderShadow`, #101).

## Ours

A soft dark ellipse under each character, in its model's place among the sprites, as a flat part of the model on the ground (#102): over the ground, the liquids, the underlays and the sprites drawn before the model, such as the floor or the stairs it stands on; under the model and every sprite drawn after it.
The start script's other objects (chests, barrels) have none.

## Why

The flattened mesh needs its own pass; the ellipse grounds the characters until then (#101).
