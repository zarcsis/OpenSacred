# Fight

Who fights whom and how: the bosses, the hero's attack, the pursuit, the archers and the doors in the way (docs/re/fight.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Archers' melee switch and rearming | The switch to melee and the bow-taking follow the original's rolls, but class 5 is taken by no `+0x25F`, a put-away bow is gone, a change of weapons ends the walk, both chances fall in one frame, the new weapon's dice are seeded, a creature back with its sector holds its first weapons, and a levelled boss backs off. An arrow leaves from 80 over the feet of a shooter whose model has no left hand. | The fields and the event behind those cases (`+0x25F`, the bit `+0x2B8` `0x10`, `+0x1F4` bit `0x200`, the event `0x14`) are not kept, nor a creature's state beyond its place and health when its sector goes. | deviation | #391, #396, #670 |
| Bosses and champions levelled when made | A hostile fill's boss or champion takes its level as it is made, by the hero's level then, rather than at its first fight by its opponent's, and a script's boss of level 1000 is made as its sector first comes in rather than at the game's start. | The original's look levels a hostile one by the hero's level as the hero comes near, and the hero's level changes slowly, so that the two seldom differ. | deviation | #318, #540, #630 |
| Creatures picked near the cursor's line | A click picks a creature within 45 units of the cursor's line of sight, up to 110 over its feet, where the original picks it by its model's shape on the screen. | The game does not keep the models' bounds. | deviation | #283 |
| Doors' measured points and giving up | A creature walks to a door's outer or inner point as the game measures it from the models, or to the cell beside the door, and straight on where no way leads; a melee creature without a way or a door gives the fight up, there being no standing sub-state beyond the band. The hero's list of hostiles is taken by the flags' bits 0 and 1. | The client's table of the doors' points, its cells of the nibble 9 or 10 and the melee's sub-state 2 beyond the band are not read; giving up keeps a creature from walking into a wall again and again. | deviation | #392, #431 |
| Level 1 in sectors without levels | A creature made in a sector without `SpawnValues` takes level 1 rather than the original's 0 (`fight::held_level`), so that its most health is about a seventh lower. | The game's formulas take levels from 1, with terms of `L − 1`, and what the original's make of level 0 is not read. | deviation | #541, #562 |
| No poison against no resistance | A strike's poison gives no effect 30 against a poison resistance of 0. | The original divides by the resistance on the x87 unit, its share then infinite and its whole value and key indefinite (`0x80000000`); no creature's or hero's resistance comes to 0, as each comes of its attributes. | deviation | #1354 |
| Pursuit's leash, chain and wait | A creature at full health in the band walks home beyond 2,500 from it, the nearer of the original's two leash distances; the strike chain skips its own tests of distance and straight line; and an unchained strike waits 0.2 s, the original AI's two steps, where the game's minds decide every frame. | Which of the two leash distances holds when is not read, and a creature strikes in its band alone. | deviation | #279, #466, #642 |
