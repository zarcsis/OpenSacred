|---|---|---|---|---|

| Archers' melee switch and rearming | The switch to melee and the bow-taking follow the original's rolls, but class 5 is taken by no `+0x25F`, a put-away bow is gone, a change of weapons ends the walk, both chances fall in one frame, the new weapon's dice are seeded, a creature back with its sector holds its first weapons, and a levelled boss backs off.
