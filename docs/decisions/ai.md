# AI

The creatures' life while no foe is near (docs/re/ai.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creature walking by the standing hero steps aside | At each of the hero's looks, the hero standing and not attacking, a creature not hostile to her that walks under an order of its own within 91 of her walks to the first of 10 points within 200 of itself that it reaches (`ai::aside`, `opensacred_rules::minds::Mind::steps_aside`), where the original's look finds such a point (one the hero may step on and reach) and sends it with the event 4 of the mode 0, whose handler stops the creature and reads nothing of the point, so that it stands dormant until the next look wakes it (docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake"). | The point's search reads as a step aside meant and lost to the handler's mode 0, which three other senders of the mode with a computed point share; stepping aside keeps a walking creature out of the hero's way without halting it beside her; the maintainer's choice (#3650). | repair | #240, #3648, #3650 |
| Parting and stepping aside by a creature's own cell | Every creature counts only its own cell, not the eight about a horse or a big model, the `+0x200` bit `0x10` and a script's hold do not keep it from parting, and a creature steps aside while the hero does not attack, which stands for the hero's AI state 0 (the step aside itself, where the original's look stops the creature: "Creature walking by the standing hero steps aside"). | The models' sizes, the scripts' hold and the hero's AI states are not read into the game, and without them a few creatures part or step aside where the original's would stand. | limitation | #240, #247, #251, #639 |
| The dryads' leap's search | A dryad's search for the point of its leap tests every point from where the dryad stands, and one whose path to the hero fails is passed over, where the original's moves the dryad onto that point for the path's test and leaves it there (`0x4D884E`), its later points about it, so that a search that finds none leaves the dryad 300 to 500 off, moved there with no leap. | A defect of the original's code: the leap's search means to keep the dryad where it stands until it leaps, as it does for a point found. | repair | #2566 |
| The guards' torches | The guards that take a torch by night are those whose home cell's coordinates sum to an odd number, rather than those of an odd id; a hornet's flank and the hornet queen's circle take the same sum for their id's parity and low bits (#2562). | The original's ids are its object manager's, given as the objects are made, which the game does not number; the home cell gives about as many guards a torch, the same ones at every start. | limitation | #61, #2562 |
| The raising's nearest dead | The black magicians' raising (the spell 43) stands up the dead monster nearest its caster in its sector that passes the raising's tests (`casts::raisable`), where the original takes the first of the sector's list of objects that passes them (docs/re/arts.md, "The creatures' own spells"). | The game keeps no list of a sector's objects in the original's order; the corpse raised passes the same tests in the same sector, one a cast, and which of them the original's order finds first tells the player nothing. | limitation | #1856, #2950 |
| Townsfolk's traders, doors and danger | A trader's visit, and a walk off a door's point that takes its sub-state 12 (#3424), end where the townsperson stands rather than in a house, and a townsperson goes into a house by the door's points as the game measures them, giving up where it stops short rather than trying another door. In danger it runs 700 from the threat, finishing its walk first, rather than into the nearest house, and the fights' targets put none in danger. | The client's table of the doors' points (`0x43B290`) is not read, and the run is what the original's townsperson does where no door is near. | limitation | #247, #248, #251, #254, #431, #3424 |
