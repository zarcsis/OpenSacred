# AI

The creatures' life while no foe is near (docs/re/ai.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parting and stepping aside simplified | Every creature counts only its own cell, not the eight about a horse or a big model, the `+0x200` bit `0x10` and a script's hold do not keep it from parting, and a creature steps aside whatever the hero's AI state. | The models' sizes, the scripts' hold and the hero's AI states are not read into the game, and without them a few creatures part or step aside where the original's would stand. | deviation | #240, #247, #251, #639 |
| The dryads' leap's search | A dryad's search for the point of its leap tests every point from where the dryad stands, and one whose path to the hero fails is passed over, where the original's moves the dryad onto that point for the path's test and leaves it there (`0x4D884E`), its later points about it, so that a search that finds none leaves the dryad 300 to 500 off, moved there with no leap. | A defect of the original's code: the leap's search means to keep the dryad where it stands until it leaps, as it does for a point found. | deviation | #2566 |
| The guards' torches | The guards that take a torch by night are those whose home cell's coordinates sum to an odd number, rather than those of an odd id; a hornet's flank and the hornet queen's circle take the same sum for their id's parity and low bits (#2562). | The original's ids are its object manager's, given as the objects are made, which the game does not number; the home cell gives about as many guards a torch, the same ones at every start. | limitation | #61, #2562 |
| Townsfolk's traders, doors and danger | A trader's visit ends at the trader rather than in a house, and a townsperson goes into a house by the door's points as the game measures them, giving up where it stops short rather than trying another door. In danger it runs 700 from the threat, finishing its walk first, rather than into the nearest house, and the fights' targets put none in danger. | The client's table of the doors' points (`0x43B290`) is not read, and the run is what the original's townsperson does where no door is near. | deviation | #247, #248, #251, #254, #431 |
