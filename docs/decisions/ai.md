| A caster's deadline put on the load's clock | A save keeps a caster's deadline of the real clock, from which it may cast again, as the seconds from the save's time, and a load puts it on its own clock (`opensacred_rules::minds::Mind::rebase`, `ai::SavedMind`), so that a caster waits after a load what it had left to wait, where the original's load takes the deadline (`+0x24D`) back raw, already passed by the time of the load, so that every caster may cast at once (docs/re/saves.md, "What a load gives back of the doings"). | A save and a load change nothing of the world they keep; a load in a new run would otherwise free every caster near the hero of its wait. | better | #3757 |
