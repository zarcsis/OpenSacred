# Day and night

Day and night (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-18.

## The original

Each vertex of the world takes the colour of the night: a ramp of the light around it from the light sources, with 255 less the night's level added; the art is multiplied by it, and blended as it is drawn (docs/re/daynight.md).

## Ours

The frame drawn is multiplied by the colour in a fullscreen pass, after everything but the interface, the light's level taken per pixel from the 32 nearest light sources (the light statics and the hero), and the grey ramp in the whole frame when the hero's sector is a dungeon.

## Why

The world's materials (the ground's, the sprites', the models') were being changed then by pull requests (#29, #38, #41, #51), and a pass over the frame needs none of them; 32 sources cover a screen of the start's lamps.
What the original blends over darkened art (the liquids, the shadows, the lamps' glows, whose colour it does not darken) is darkened after the blending here.
