# The creatures open a door from its point, and give up without a way

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-21 (#392); changed 2026-10-22 (#431).
Replaces in part [2026-10-20-pursuit](2026-10-20-pursuit.md), where a creature without a straight line to the hero sought neither a door nor a way.

## The original

A creature not of the classes 2, 5 (unless its `+0x25F` is set) and 6 with no straight line to its target goes for the nearest closed door of its sector that is not barred and that a hero turned before, on its layer or on the ground and the first floor either way (`0x4D4800`, docs/re/fight.md, "Doors in the way").
It walks to the door's outer point on the ground or its inner point on a floor (`0x43B290`), by a way, else to a nearby cell of the ground nibble 9 or 10; a creature of `+0x200` bit 2 goes only for the gates, palisades, fences and cages; standing within 300 of the door it calls the quests' hook and, unless the door's object has flag `0x4000`, opens the door, then takes the AI's state 0.
It does so from the idle state's choice without a straight line (`0x5442E8`), from the melee's sub-state 2 standing beyond the band when the way to the target ends farther than `R + 80` from it (`0x4E4931`, which without a door gives the fight up), and from the move in the AI's state 4 that finds no way to its point twice, the second time to the point moved by up to 50 along each axis (`0x4F5652`).
In the melee's decision, the second to the fourth of the hero's list of hostiles near the first take their places about the hero for a frame (`0x4E224E`): one whose place lies within `R + 15` of it does not walk and takes the sub-state 2 standing, where it strikes, even beyond the band along a straight line.

## Ours

The creatures go for the doors and take their places as the original's do (docs/re/fight.md, "In `opensacred-game`").
But:

- A creature walks to the door's outer or inner point as the game measures them from the doors' models, placements and the floors' grids (#431, docs/re/ai.md, "Houses and doors"), not by the client's table of its types (`0x43B290`), from which they lie about a cell's side (the medians 23 and 49 units); at a door without a point on its side, to the cell beside the door's that may be walked on at its level, the nearest to it, or toward the door itself; with no way to its point it walks straight on as far as it gets, the original's cells of the nibble 9 or 10 not being read.
  Until #431 every creature walked to the cell beside the door's.
- No door is barred: the game reads no trigger's bit 15, so every closed door that a hero turned counts.
- No creature of class 5 goes for a door, the game keeping no `+0x25F`; no creature keeps to the gates by `+0x200` bit 2, whose meaning is not read; the quests' hook is not called, and no door's flag `0x4000` keeps it shut.
- The walk to the door is at a walk, the gait of the original's state 1 not being read, and at the door the creature looks for the hero again at once (**hypothesis**: the original's state 0 takes it up so).
- The game has no standing sub-state 2 beyond the band, its creatures there coming on: a creature fighting in melee that comes on without a straight line asks for a way at each order to come on, to the hero's cell or to the hero's point moved by up to 50 along each axis, over the cells alone and not the creatures in the way; without one it goes for a door, and without a door it gives the fight up, as the band's sub-state does, the original's move without a door not being read.
  The game's way ends at the hero's cell or not at all, so that no way stands for one that ends farther than `R + 80` from the hero; it is searched over the game's window of 64 × 64 cells (docs/re/movement.md, "The way") rather than `0x4FABE0`'s 55 × 55, to the hero's cell brought nearer along the line beyond 25 cells as the original's.
- A creature whose place about the hero lies within `R + 15` of it decides as the others, coming on beyond the band rather than striking from there, and the game's creatures decide whenever they stand, after a strike or a walk, where the original's decide after a strike.
- The hero's list of hostiles takes the creatures by their flags' bits 0 and 1 (docs/re/motions.md, "The fight stance"), without the original's test of their list at `+0x588`.

## Why

The client's table of the doors' points, the barred bit, `+0x25F`, `+0x200` bit 2, the quests' hook and the state 1's gait are not read, and the game's fight has no sub-state 2 beyond the band; the measured points bring a creature where it opens the door from as the original's do, and the cell beside the door where a door has none, and giving up without a way keeps a creature from walking into a wall again and again.
