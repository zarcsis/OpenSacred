# Combat arts

The heroes' combat arts and spells (docs/re/arts.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arts' tooltips' numbers from their code | The arts' tooltips take these numbers from the code that applies them: a factor's per cent rounded to the nearest, where the original's `P` truncates a negative one toward zero, one point short (−19 for 1071's ×0.8); the attack's bonus of the strike's `1.3 + 0.125·L`, where the original's takes the float 1.3 a little short and truncates (+29 at the level 0); 1016's strike, the Daemon's 1101 to 1103 and their bolts whole, as their effects and objects take them, where the original's lines round them; the bats that 1035 to 1037 keep by their records' factors, where the original's line takes constants compiled in; the flamethrower's burn at least 2 s and the cannon's damages crossed with the other kind's multiplier, as the shot deals them; the call of death's most bonus of its word `v1` (+90 % at the level 1), where the original's takes a tenth of its duration (+3 %); the choir's slowing of its objects' `clamp(2M/(15·L), 0.5, 1)` against the hero's level, where the original's weighs `0.75·a/(5·L)`; the teleport's farthest reach, `v0 − 1` steps of 10 in metres, where the original's prints the count of its steps; the war cry's allies' attack of half its own and its time `W60/100`, and the Daemon's 1097's two shares their whole values, where the original's take the defence's half and the float 0.01 a little short; and the kicks' and 1003's, 1091's and 66's chances against a creature of the hero's level unscaled by the difficulty, as the strike weighs that level, where the original's scale it (`0x5893B0`). | The tooltip tells what the art does; the arts' code stays the original's (#1737's rule). | repair | #1694, #1741 |
| Arts' tooltips tell every effect | The tooltips of the fire skin, the quickening, the speed, the shield wall and the mana's spring list every effect that their objects' action 6 gives, from what gives it (`spells::effects`), and name the spring's both regeneration speeds, where the original's leave out the fire skin's defence, the quickening's and the speed's attack and the shield wall's physical resistance, and name the spring's the mental regeneration; the death's kiss shows its bleeding a second, its curse of the target's attack and defence and their 5 s (`arts::bleed_value`, `curse`, `CURSE_SECONDS`), where the original's shows the bleeding once and the skull's flight as the duration; and an art of no case of its own shows its regeneration, where the original's shows nothing (`0x5893B0`). | The tooltip tells what the art does (#1737's rule). | repair | #1694, #1742 |
| Combat master's button lit where a press makes the combo | The combat master's button lights, and its total shows in the affordable yellow, only where the gold is above the cost, as its press asks (`combos::affordable`, `makes`), where the original's lights them with the gold equal to the cost too, a press then making nothing (`0x6D26D0`, `0x6D22A0`; seen). | The window tells whether a press makes the combo; the price and the press stay the original's (#1737's rule). | repair | #1540, #1743 |
| Daemon's blast edge and level | A creature beyond the blast's radius `R` takes no strike, where the original's unsigned `R − d` gives it a killing blow, and the knockback's chance takes the charge's level for the object's level word. | The unsigned difference is a defect that the factor's form, falling to 0 at `R`, does not ask for, and the level word has no value of its own on that way. | deviation | #760 |
| Fireball's start and level flight | The Mage's fireball leaves from where he stands and flies level at the bolts' height along the line on the ground, where the original's flies along a direction in three dimensions whose height's part shrinks at each frame. | Where the original's fireball starts and at what height is not read. | deviation | #754, #872 |
| Seraphim's jump rate at least 1 | The Seraphim's jump plays its clip at a rate of at least 1, where the original's rate is 0 from 495 to 500 and holds her in the art. | A clip that stands still plays as a defect of the original rather than a rule, and the rate beside those six units is 1. | deviation | #754 |
| Spells page's key cleared with its binding | A key F1 to F6 pressed again over the art that holds it takes its label off and the hero's binding with it (`Arts::unbind`), where the original's takes it off the page's list alone, so that the key still names nothing until the page's next showing puts the label and the binding back (`0x6C1F30`). | The label tells which key makes the art (#1737's rule). | repair | #1536, #1743 |
