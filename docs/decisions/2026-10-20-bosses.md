# The bosses and the champions, levelled when made

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#318); changed 2026-10-23 (#630).

## The original

The fills make bosses and champions by their types and by chance, at the marker level 1; at their first strike or hit they take their opponent's level plus two within their sector's levels, refill their health and make their items again (docs/re/fight.md, "Bosses and champions").
`CreateNPC` and a script command make bosses by a level of 1000 or a tag, and the bosses have behaviours of their own; they show as such on the screen.

## Ours

A fill's boss or champion is made as the original's, and takes its level when it is made, by the hero's level then rather than its first opponent's at its first fight; its health, attack, defence and kill's experience take their factors.
So does a script's boss of `CreateNPC`'s level 1000 (#540), made as its sector first comes in, though the original makes those of the start script as the game starts.
Since #630 a boss, a champion or a creature of a champion's levelling not hostile to the hero, which the original's hero's look does not level (`0x547377`), takes its level again at its first strike or hit by its opponent's, a creature's or the hero's, as the original's does: the start's boss 689 at 13 after its first blow at a bear of level 11; the hostile ones keep the level they are made at, which the original's look gives them by the hero's level as the hero comes near.
The bosses of `CreateNPC`'s tag `0x72` and of the script command `0x49E760`, the bosses' behaviours (`0x4D4E50`) and their look are not followed yet, nor the black rider's real level.

## Why

The game's only opponent is the hero, whose level changes slowly, so the level at the making and at the first fight differ seldom, and the making needs no sector's values at the fight; the other scripts' bosses and the behaviours are features of their own, the bosses' behaviours not read (docs/re/fight.md, "Open").
