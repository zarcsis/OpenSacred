# Sound

The converter's sound and the game's music, ambience and noises (docs/re/sound.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Armour and weapon remarks weigh their own kind | The hero's `NOARMOR` and `NOWEAPON` remarks name the magic or the poison where three times its own share of the damage let through passes the physical's, where the original's strike weighs the fire's share for both (`0x56DCE0`, `0x56DE47`). | The remark tells which kind hurt or failed; the fire's share names a magic or a poison that hardly hurt, and never one that came alone (#1737's rule: a text tells what the code does). | repair | #1673, #1739 |
| Companions' click lines by their names' keys | A click on the novice companion or on Prince Valor plays their click lines (`SFX_CLICKMYSHNOVIZIN_*`, `SFX_CLICKMYSHVALOR_*`): the game compares the creature's name with the texts of the keys 677 to 679 and 322, where the original compares it with the German names "Novizin" and "Prinz Valor", which match no English text, and never plays the eight lines. | The maintainer's decision (#2652); which keys the German names stand for is a hypothesis (the novice's lines of an item given are of the keys 677 to 679), as no German texts are at hand. | repair | #2652 |
| Companions' location lines named right | `opensacred-convert netscript` makes the places' announcements (`*_audiomeldungen`) name the companions' location lines they mean, `SFX_LOC<NN>_SH<COMPANION>` for the `SFX_Loc<N>_SH` that the branch testing the companion with the hero plays (`script_repairs::COMPANION_SOUNDS`, 23 lines in each of the 20 trees, two of them without the number, given by the hero's line of the same branch); the original matches none of these names with a sound and never plays the 23 samples. Each branch then waits in the companion's queue for the line's length (a `Wait` of `0x5F` in hundredths, `script_repairs::companion_waits`) before the call of the hero's line that it queues there, where the original's queue would run the call at the companion's next tick and the hero's line, the line by name, would stop the companion's as it starts; the companion stands meanwhile, up to 6.4 s, as a creature with a script's queue leaves its idle thought. | The maintainer's decision (#2684), heard whole (#2688): the call queued in the companion's queue tells that the hero answers once the companion has spoken. Prince Valor's line of the location 36 stays silent, as no sample is it. | repair | #2684, #2688 |
| Desert bridge's hero's line with Vilya | `opensacred-convert netscript` makes the desert bridge's announcement call the hero's line of its own location (`sprich_2.21`, the location 67) in its branch that tests Vilya with the hero, where the original calls the line of the location 2 (`sprich_2`) after her line of the location 67. | The maintainer's decision (#2684): the branch without her, and both branches of every other announcement, call the location's own line. | repair | #2684 |
| Gladiator's first too weak line his own | The Gladiator's too weak region's roll 0 plays his own `REGIONTOOWEAK_01_GLAD`, where the original's merged tails play his first too strong line (`0x67C9F6`). | His remark told a land too strong for him where it was too weak; the line is his, in the sound data, and the roll's odds stay (#1737's rule). | repair | #1247, #1739 |
| Hostile creatures' idle noises at rest | A creature hostile to the hero makes its idle noises only while it does not fight, always of the group 0, and the lists have no limit; the moving and striking states' groups, the name's path, the flags and the record before are left out. | The states come from counters that the creatures' updates and the sound events feed in ways not read, and the limits, the name's path and the flags concern few creatures. | deviation | #714, #1140 |
| MP3 samples from their first frame | `opensacred-convert sound` decodes the 73 MP3 records of `sound.pak` that start with a frame's worth of zeros from their first frame into the FLAC masters. | The original's decoder searches for the sync word, so the zeros cost at most a frame of silence; the sound does not change. | repair | #354 |
| Music files from their first frame | `opensacred-convert sound` decodes the 154 music files of `mp3/` that hold zeros, or a jingle's name, before their first frame from that frame into the FLAC masters. | The Miles decoder searches for the sync word, so they play in the original too; the sound does not change. | repair | #367 |
| Music kept under a fanfare | The music keeps its level under a side quest's fanfare, where the original's dip to half ends after one frame, about 1.7 %, and rises back over 5 s. | The original's dip is a step of one frame that no one hears, and its rise brings back what it took. | deviation | #1243 |
| Pacified land told by its region | The hero's `REGIONPACIFIED` at an area's border looks up the area's region (`0x95CBFC`'s table), the number that the scripts' `RegionFrei` pacifies, where the original's pass looks the table up by the area's own number (`0x6268B5`). | The original's remark told another land's peace, or kept silent on entering a pacified one; when it may play stays (#1737's rule). | repair | #1247, #1739 |
| Peace stream's ambient and spot loops | Within its phase the ambient loop changes as soon as the place calls for another (the region's music, the sector's nature and the hour, as `0x696A90` chooses), and the spot loops of the cemetery and the arenas (`ATMOSPOT_*`) take the music's channel while the hero stands in their sectors, the turns held. | When the original chooses the loop again within its phase, and where it plays the spot loops, is not read (**hypotheses**: it chooses the ambient loop again at the region's events, which a sector of music posts, plays the arenas' on a stream of their own and asks for the ambient loop at the cemetery). | deviation | #56, #1177, #1093 |
| Scripts' misnamed sounds named right | `opensacred-convert netscript` makes the `PlaySound`s and `PlayMusic`s that name a sound wrongly name the sound they mean (`script_repairs::SOUNDS`): the locations' lines written without their number's leading zero (62), the Wood Elf's written `SCelf` for the samples' `SCHELF` (50), the quests' voice-overs cut short (13) and two music cues written without their underscores, 2,423 records of the 20 trees; the original finds none of these names among its sounds and never plays the 125 samples and the two cues. | The maintainer's decision (#2652). The fight's end jingles 9 to 16 stay unplayed, as the original's choice of their profile, not a name, leaves them. | repair | #2652 |
| Sound in Ogg Opus | `build` encodes every sound master into Ogg Opus at 48 kHz, a sample at 96 kbit/s mono and 128 stereo, a music file at 256 stereo and 128 mono, so that the original's MP3s are encoded a second time. | Opus is held transparent at these bitrates (**estimate**) and keeps its quality at the voices' low ones; the maintainer's choice (#694, #701). | deviation | #694, #697, #701 |
| Sound's switch silences the tracks | The options' sound tick off silences every track of the effects, the speech and the music, the sounds going on unheard and the streams on where they are, and on brings them back as they stand (`volumes`), where the original stops every sample, pauses every stream and refuses every new sound, the streams resuming where they paused and the stopped samples lost (`0x6902F0`). | The channels' modules keep their loops by their own state, which a stop behind their back would break; the difference is only where a stream or a long sample stands as the sound comes back. | deviation | #2341 |
| Volumes' tracks scale what plays | A volume's change scales the sounds of its kind already playing, as each kind's channels' tracks carry its level (`volumes`), where the original shifts each playing sample's level by the change, `level + (new − old)` (`0x677A60`), and sets the streams anew. | The tracks' volume is the engine's one level for every sound on a channel; the two differ only for samples playing as a slider moves. | limitation | #2341 |
| Volumes' wheel heard at once | A volume's slider moved by the wheel sets its level at once, as a press does, where the original's wheel moves the slider and what OK writes but sets no level until the slider is next pressed or the game starts again (`0x716E00`, `0x7172F0`). | A slider that moves without being heard looks broken to the player; the level is the press's, the position over 126. | deviation | #2341 |
| Water loops panned by the water | The water's loops pan by the middle of the clear-water cells on the screen, where the original's place mixes world units with cells and depends on the camera's sector. | The original's place reads as a slip of units that has nothing to do with the water, and only the loops' pan changes. | deviation | #1169 |
| Wilbur's first click line named right | A click on Wilbur plays his first click line, `SFX_CLICKMYSHWILBUR_1`, as the sample is named, where the original's code names it `SFX_CLICKMYSH_WILBUR_1`, which no sample has, and never plays it (`0x67E645`). | The maintainer's decision (#2684); his other three lines and the other companions' are named as their samples. | repair | #2684 |
