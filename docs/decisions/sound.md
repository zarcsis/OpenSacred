# Sound

The converter's sound and the game's music, ambience and noises (docs/re/sound.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Armour and weapon remarks weigh their own kind | The hero's `NOARMOR` and `NOWEAPON` remarks name the magic or the poison where three times its own share of the damage let through passes the physical's, where the original's strike weighs the fire's share for both (`0x56DCE0`, `0x56DE47`). | The remark tells which kind hurt or failed; the fire's share names a magic or a poison that hardly hurt, and never one that came alone (#1737's rule: a text tells what the code does). | repair | #1673, #1739 |
| Gladiator's first too weak line his own | The Gladiator's too weak region's roll 0 plays his own `REGIONTOOWEAK_01_GLAD`, where the original's merged tails play his first too strong line (`0x67C9F6`). | His remark told a land too strong for him where it was too weak; the line is his, in the sound data, and the roll's odds stay (#1737's rule). | repair | #1247, #1739 |
| Hostile creatures' idle noises at rest | A creature hostile to the hero makes its idle noises only while it does not fight, always of the group 0, and the lists have no limit; the moving and striking states' groups, the name's path, the flags and the record before are left out. | The states come from counters that the creatures' updates and the sound events feed in ways not read, and the limits, the name's path and the flags concern few creatures. | deviation | #714, #1140 |
| MP3 samples from their first frame | `opensacred-convert sound` decodes the 73 MP3 records of `sound.pak` that start with a frame's worth of zeros from their first frame into the FLAC masters. | The original's decoder searches for the sync word, so the zeros cost at most a frame of silence; the sound does not change. | repair | #354 |
| Music files from their first frame | `opensacred-convert sound` decodes the 154 music files of `mp3/` that hold zeros, or a jingle's name, before their first frame from that frame into the FLAC masters. | The Miles decoder searches for the sync word, so they play in the original too; the sound does not change. | repair | #367 |
| Music kept under a fanfare | The music keeps its level under a side quest's fanfare, where the original's dip to half ends after one frame, about 1.7 %, and rises back over 5 s. | The original's dip is a step of one frame that no one hears, and its rise brings back what it took. | deviation | #1243 |
| Pacified land told by its region | The hero's `REGIONPACIFIED` at an area's border looks up the area's region (`0x95CBFC`'s table), the number that the scripts' `RegionFrei` pacifies, where the original's pass looks the table up by the area's own number (`0x6268B5`). | The original's remark told another land's peace, or kept silent on entering a pacified one; when it may play stays (#1737's rule). | repair | #1247, #1739 |
| Peace stream's ambient and spot loops | The ambient loop changes with the nature under the hero after 2 s on the other ground within its phase, and the spot loops of the cemetery and the arenas (`ATMOSPOT_*`) take the music's channel while the hero stands in their sectors, the turns held. | Where the original chooses those loops is not read (**hypotheses**: it chooses the ambient loop again at the region's events, plays the arenas' on a stream of their own and asks for the ambient loop at the cemetery). | deviation | #56, #1177 |
| Sound in Ogg Opus | `build` encodes every sound master into Ogg Opus at 48 kHz, a sample at 96 kbit/s mono and 128 stereo, a music file at 256 stereo and 128 mono, so that the original's MP3s are encoded a second time. | Opus is held transparent at these bitrates (**estimate**) and keeps its quality at the voices' low ones; the maintainer's choice (#694, #701). | deviation | #694, #697, #701 |
| Water loops panned by the water | The water's loops pan by the middle of the clear-water cells on the screen, where the original's place mixes world units with cells and depends on the camera's sector. | The original's place reads as a slip of units that has nothing to do with the water, and only the loops' pan changes. | deviation | #1169 |
