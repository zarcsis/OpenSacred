# The guards without torches or attacks

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#239).

## The original

An awake guard (docs/re/ai.md, "The peaceful creatures' behaviours") takes a torch by night when its id is odd and puts it away by day, attacks a foe within 500, and otherwise strolls about its post in one frame of 101 or walks back to it.
A guard with a dialogue (`+0x245`) stands at its post instead of strolling (docs/re/ai.md, "The townsfolk", #241).

## Ours

A guard strolls about its post at the animals' rate, 30 in 101 a second (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-animals.md), and walks back to it as the original's does; one with a dialogue does not stroll (#247).
It carries no torch and attacks no one.

## Why

The torches need the items that light the night in a hand (#61), and the attacks the fight's rules (M3); until then a guard keeps its post as the original's does by day and with no foe near.
