# The guards without torches or attacks

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#239); changed 2026-10-23 (#608, #639, #630).

## The original

An awake guard (docs/re/ai.md, "The peaceful creatures' behaviours") takes a torch by night when its id is odd and puts it away by day, attacks a foe within 500, and otherwise strolls about its post in one tick of 101 or walks back to it, deciding at every tick as it walks too.
A guard with a dialogue (`+0x245`) stands at its post instead of strolling (docs/re/ai.md, "The townsfolk", #241).

## Ours

A guard strolls about its post at the animals' chance, one tick of 101 (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-animals.md, #639), and walks back to it as the original's does, deciding as it walks too (#608); one with a dialogue does not stroll (#247).
It carries no torch.
Since #630 it attacks the nearest creature hostile to it within 500 and fights it in melee (docs/decisions/2026-10-23-creatures-fight.md), and since #670 one with a ranged weapon fights it at range.

## Why

The torches need the items that light the night in a hand (#61); until then a guard keeps its post as the original's does by day.
