# The ambient loops

Sound (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17.

## The original

The ambient loop follows the nature underfoot by day and by night, and some regions and sieged villages have their own (docs/re/sound.md).

## Ours

The day's loops by the nature, always, on a channel of their own beside the music; no night loops and no regions' loops.

## Why

The game has no day and night and no regions yet; the client plays the loops and the music through one function, `cMSS::playMusic`, and whether both sound at once is not read.
