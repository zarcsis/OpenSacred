# The hostile creatures before the fight

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#237); replaced in part by docs/decisions/2026-10-20-pursuit.md (#279), by which the hostile creatures that would fight come at the hero.

## The original

An awake hostile creature (docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake") weighs its level against the hero's and, when the test passes or the hero is within its reach, attacks it: it takes the hero as its target and comes at it from up to 800 (the AI's states 4 and 8); else it goes dormant again and in one case of three takes the idle action.
The idle action fidgets in one case of ten (`*_FUN_IDLE_*` or its attack's clip).

## Ours

The game has no fight yet: an awake hostile creature goes dormant again and in one case of three takes the idle action, as one that the test of the levels holds back, whatever its level; the hostile creatures within 800 of the hero (400 while it moves) step about as those beyond.
The fidgets are left out: the creature stands in its idle or its stance instead.

## Why

Coming at the hero without striking would show a chase that ends in nothing; the steps about show the creatures alive as the original shows them while no fight comes, and the test of the levels and the attack come with the fight's rules (M3).
The fidgets need clips that the model's slots 180 to 192 and the attack's slot give, which the NPCs' graphs do not hold yet.
