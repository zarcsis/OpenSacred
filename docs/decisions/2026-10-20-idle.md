# The hostile creatures before the fight

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#237); changed 2026-10-20 (#279).
Replaced in part by [2026-10-20-pursuit](2026-10-20-pursuit.md), by which the hostile creatures that would fight come at the hero.

## The original

An awake hostile creature (docs/re/ai.md, "Dormant and awake") weighs its level against the hero's and, when the test passes or the hero is within its reach, attacks it: it takes the hero as its target and comes at it from up to 800 (the AI's states 4 and 8); else it goes dormant again and in one case of three takes the idle action.
The idle action fidgets in one case of ten (`*_FUN_IDLE_*` or its attack's clip).

## Ours

An awake hostile creature that the test of the levels holds back, with the hero out of its reach, goes dormant again and in one case of three takes the idle action, as the original's; the others come at the hero (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-pursuit.md).
Until #279 every awake hostile creature did so, whatever its level, and those within 800 of the hero (400 while it moves) stepped about as those beyond.
The fidgets are left out: the creature stands in its idle or its stance instead.

## Why

Before the fight's rules, coming at the hero without striking would have shown a chase that ends in nothing; the steps about showed the creatures alive as the original shows them while no fight comes.
The fidgets need the model's slots 180 to 192 and the attack's clip; the NPCs' graphs hold the attack's clips (#280) and the slots 180 to 183 of the instruments (#253), not the rest.
