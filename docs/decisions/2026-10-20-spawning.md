# The sectors' creatures

Spawning (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#221, #222).

## The original

As a sector comes into the 3 × 3 sectors about the view's centre, the engine runs its script function `Sector<x><yyy>Init` once and its `Sector<x><yyy>Enter` at each coming (docs/re/spawning.md).
`Init` places the sector's monsters, which persist; `Enter` places its villagers and animals again, and its `FillSector` records make creatures at random with the C library's `rand()`: a count by the sector's levels and the hero's level, the types by their weights, the cells at random, packs of thieves about a leader, a quarter of the rabbits of other variants, and items from the `MakeGroup` rules.
As the sector leaves, the creatures of its `Enter` go, the living ones kept and put back at the next coming while the region is the same.

## Ours

The game places the records of both functions as their sectors come in, and again whenever they come back (`npcs`, #221).
The fills roll with a generator of the game's own seeded by the sector and the record (`spawning`), so a sector that comes back has the same creatures on the same cells; the hero's level is taken as 1, the game having no levels.
The packs and the rabbits' and crows' variants follow the client's steps (#229), the first leader of a run alone as its distance in cells has it.
Their `MakeGroup` weapons and shields are rolled by the same generator (#228).
Not followed: the worn items and the crossbows' replacement, the `DETAILLEVEL` 0 that leaves the animals out, the crabs and the fish of a sector entered without kept creatures, the creatures' own levels, the champions and the bosses, the black riders, and the type 344's variant made by the creature maker; the creatures stand idle.

## Why

Nothing in the game changes the creatures yet (no fight, no AI, no time), so placing them again as their sector comes back shows what the original's kept creatures show; a seeded generator keeps that true of the fills too, where a new roll at each coming would move them about.
The rest waits for what it needs: the worn items for the worn models, the levels, the champions and the alarm for the rules of M3.
