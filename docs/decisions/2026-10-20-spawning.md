# The sectors' creatures

Spawning (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#221, #222); changed 2026-10-20 (#237, #282, #300, #318).

## The original

As a sector comes into the 3 × 3 sectors about the view's centre, the engine runs its script function `Sector<x><yyy>Init` once and its `Sector<x><yyy>Enter` at each coming (docs/re/spawning.md).
`Init` places the sector's monsters, which persist; `Enter` places its villagers and animals again, and its `FillSector` records make creatures at random with the C library's `rand()`: a count by the sector's levels and the hero's level, the types by their weights, the cells at random, packs of thieves about a leader, a quarter of the rabbits of other variants, and items from the `MakeGroup` rules.
As the sector leaves, the creatures of its `Enter` go, the living ones kept and put back at the next coming while the region is the same.

## Ours

The game places the records of both functions as their sectors come in, and again whenever they come back (`npcs`, #221): a creature killed or moved since comes back whole at its record's place, where the original's `Init` creatures persist and its `Enter` creatures come back alive where they were (#329).
The fills roll with a generator of the game's own seeded by the sector and the record (`spawning`), so a sector that comes back has the same creatures on the same cells while the hero's level stays; their count takes the hero's level as the sector comes in (#300).
The packs and the rabbits' and crows' variants follow the client's steps (#229), the first leader of a run alone as its distance in cells has it.
Their `MakeGroup` weapons and shields are rolled by the same generator (#228).
The creatures' levels and the fills' bosses and champions follow the original (docs/re/fight.md, "In `opensacred-game`"; #278, #318).
Not followed: the worn items and the crossbows' replacement, the `DETAILLEVEL` 0 that leaves the animals out, the crabs and the fish of a sector entered without kept creatures, the scripts' bosses, the black riders, and the type 344's variant made by the creature maker.

## Why

Placing the creatures again as their sector comes back showed what the original's kept creatures show while nothing in the game changed them; since the AI (#237) and the fight (#282) it departs further, a creature killed or moved coming back whole (#329).
A seeded generator keeps the fills' creatures on the same cells, where a new roll at each coming would move them about.
The worn items wait for the worn models.
