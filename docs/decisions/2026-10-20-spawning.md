# The sectors' creatures

Spawning (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#221, #222); changed 2026-10-20 (#237, #282, #300, #318, #329), 2026-10-22 (#530), 2026-10-26 (#1107) and 2026-10-27 (#1217); brought up to date 2026-10-23 (#692).

## The original

As a sector comes into the 3 × 3 sectors about the view's centre, the engine runs its script function `Sector<x><yyy>Init` once and its `Sector<x><yyy>Enter` at each coming (docs/re/spawning.md).
`Init` places the sector's monsters, which persist; `Enter` places its villagers and animals again, and its `FillSector` records make creatures at random with the C library's `rand()`: a count by the sector's levels and the hero's level, the types by their weights, the cells at random, packs of thieves about a leader, a quarter of the rabbits of other variants, and items from the `MakeGroup` rules.
As the sector leaves, the creatures of its `Enter` go, its `CreateNPC`s' made again at the next coming, its fills' living ones kept and put back while the region is the same; a sector whose fills were all killed after one had been kept is not filled again until a sector of another region is filled, which empties the other regions' kept creatures (#1103).

## Ours

The game places the records of both functions as their sectors come in (`npcs`, #221), and keeps what becomes of them as the original does (#329): a creature of the start script or of an `Init` is placed once, and as its sector goes it is kept where it stands, on its floor, with its health and its level, coming back so, and a killed one never; a fill's living creatures are kept with their sector and put back so at its next coming; the villagers and animals of an `Enter` are made again at every coming.
The fills follow the original's regions (#1107): a sector that kept a fill's creature fills nothing while its region is the one that filled last, and stays without its fills once they were killed, and a sector of another region filling drops what the other regions' sectors kept, which fill anew at their next coming.
The waters' crabs and fish are not kept, as the original's are not.
A creature that walked into a sector that stays loaded goes with that one rather than with its own, and comes back with its own sector where it stood.
The game's sectors go two sectors from the hero's (5 × 5), not as they leave the 3 × 3 about it.
The fills roll with a generator of the game's own seeded by the sector and the record (`spawning`), so a sector that comes back has the same creatures on the same cells while the hero's level stays; their count takes the hero's level as the sector comes in (#300).
The packs and the rabbits' and crows' variants follow the client's steps (#229), the first leader of a run alone as its distance in cells has it.
Their `MakeGroup` weapons and shields are rolled by the same generator (#228), and a crossbow or a musket that a creature's model cannot shoot becomes another weapon as the original's does, a fill's and a `CreateNPC`'s, chosen by the drops' choice of a type with that generator, a `CreateNPC`'s seeded by its cell and record (#1217).
The creatures' levels and the fills' bosses and champions follow the original (docs/re/fight.md, "In `opensacred-game`"; #278, #318).
A `CreateNPC`'s creature rolls whether it takes one level more than the hero by a generator seeded by its cell (#530), so it has the same level at each coming while the hero's level stays, where the original rolls anew; a creature of the start script, which the game makes as its sector first comes in, takes the level the original gives it as the game starts.
Not followed: the worn items, the `DETAILLEVEL` 0 that leaves the animals out, the scripts' other bosses (of `CreateNPC`'s tag `0x72` and of the script command), the black riders, and the type 344's variant made by the creature maker.
The crabs and the fish of a sector entered without kept creatures are made since #575 and #664 (docs/decisions/2026-10-23-waters-edges.md), and the bosses of `CreateNPC`'s level 1000 since #540 (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-bosses.md).

## Why

Keeping the creatures as the original's sectors' functions do keeps a killed monster dead and a fled rabbit where it fled (#329), now that the AI (#237) and the fight (#282) change them; the wider ring of sectors serves the streaming's needs.
The sectors come in as the original's do, the 3 × 3 about the hero's, so the region that filled last changes where the original's does; since they go later, a sector that the hero leaves and comes back to within two sectors keeps its creatures in the world rather than coming in again.
A seeded generator keeps the fills' creatures on the same cells, where a new roll at each coming would move them about, and a village's creatures at the same levels.
The worn items wait for the worn models.
