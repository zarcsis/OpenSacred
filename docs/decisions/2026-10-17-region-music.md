# The regions' music

Sound (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17.

## The original

Each region has its music; how the original picks it is not recovered (docs/02 §10.3).

## Ours

The tracks of the `music` profile's first group (`MUSIC_WOOD01`–`03`) play one after another everywhere, a random one first, fading in.

## Why

The starting region's woods fit them; the regions' music replaces them once recovered.
