# The hero's pick-up fills a pack

Items (`opensacred-game`'s `pickup`), 2026-10-26 (#911).

## The original

A hero that reaches an item it was sent to plays its pick-up clip (`MOTION_PICKUP`, with the setting `PICKUPANIM`, on by default) and takes the item at the clip's half (`cCreature::pickupItem`, `0x558080`).
The item goes, in this order: a quest's item into the list of the main quest's items, the element stones there too, gold into the purse, a weapon into an empty hand or another item into an empty slot that it fits, onto the cursor while the inventory is open, the category 14 into its list, else into the first place of the inventory's grid where its size fits; without room it jumps where it lies (docs/re/items.md, "Picking up").
With Alt held the items come before the hostile creatures under the cursor.

## Ours

The hero takes the item at once, without a clip.
Gold goes into its purse; every other item goes into its pack, in the order taken, with no grid, so that the hero always has room.
The hands and the slots are not filled, the quests' items are not counted, and Alt does not change the order.

## Why

The game has no inventory yet: no window, no grid, and no change of the hero's look and values as it puts an item on.
The grid needs the items' sizes in cells (`0x428D60`, a table and a switch over the types), and the quests' items need the quests' collecting lists, which the scripts do not keep yet.
The pack keeps every item with its values, so that the inventory, when it comes, can place them as the original does.
