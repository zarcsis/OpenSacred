# Spawning

The sectors' creatures as they come and go (docs/re/spawning.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Black riders carried by their horses | A fill's black rider and its horse go together, the rider's place carried by its horse's as a mounted hero's is; the original's fill sets neither the rider's `+0x200` bit 4 nor the horse's bit 8, so that its rider's place stays where it last stood idle while its horse moves (**seen**), and the rider's distances are measured from there; so the original's axe rider waits for its horse to stand before it strikes (`0x4E47C7`–`0x4E4812`) and the charge's legs read the rider's own heading, which the game's rider, its horse under it, needs neither. | The game moves a rider by its own walk and carries its horse under it, as it does the hero; a rider apart from the horse it is drawn on would strike and be struck from where nothing stands, and the pair's fight hardly changes. | simpler | #2288, #2338 |
| Crabs and fish by the loaded sectors | A sector's crabs and fish test only the cells of the sectors loaded as it comes in, so that the edge rows next to neighbours not loaded get none, up to one in 16 of its cells. | Reading a sector's cells for the rings would need a second reader of the sector files beside the streamer's, for a few fish made by a roll of one in 11 anyway. | limitation | #575 |
| Respawn's gate as meant | A sector whose `Enter` has fills fills anew no sooner than the balance's `Respawn` (300 s) after its gate last passed, `DungeonRespawn` (1,000 s) for a dungeon's sector, counted on the world's time since the game began, which a pause stands and a save keeps (`npcs::Kept`'s clock): a coming within the bound makes no fill and leaves the sector's kept creatures, its time, the region that filled last and the other regions' lists as they are, its kept creatures coming back and, with none, the crabs and fish of its waters; a coming after it passes, the time kept as the sector's last pass, whether it then fills anew or puts back what the sector kept; a sector that never passed passes, the gate is one a coming for all of its `Enter`'s fills, and a fill of another region, which drops what the sectors of this one kept, drops their gates' passes too, so that they fill anew at their next coming. The original's gate (`FillSector`, `0x47CC1E`) chooses its bound by the sum of the time it keeps and the dungeon's test and compares the machine's real time since its boot with that bound alone, so that it passes at every `Enter` but in the machine's first 300 s, and a sector that kept none of its fills fills anew at its very next coming, however soon (docs/re/spawning.md, "The respawn's gate"). | The bound's expression reads as a slip: the time kept beside it and the two constants' names say that a sector was meant to wait out `Respawn` before it fills again, so that a sector cleared stays clear for a while, as the maintainer chose on 2026-11-25; the gate is one a coming, as the literal gate, record by record, would hold every fill of an `Enter` but its first; the world's time, which a save keeps and a pause stands, counts in place of the machine's; and the passes go with the lists that a region's change drops, else a walk back and forth across a region's border within the bound would leave the sectors behind with neither their kept creatures nor new ones. | repair | #3786 |
| Sectors' creatures kept and seeded | Sectors go two from the hero (5 × 5) rather than as they leave the 3 × 3, and the fills roll from a generator seeded by the sector and the record, so that a sector comes back with the same creatures on the same cells. A `CreateNPC`'s extra level is rolled by a generator seeded by its cell and the game's seed, so that a game and the loads of its saves keep it and each new game rolls it anew, and the start script's creatures are made as their sector first comes in. | The wider ring serves the streaming, and the seeded rolls keep the fills' creatures in place and a village's at the same levels, where a new roll at each coming would move them about. | simpler | #221, #329, #530, #3875 |
| Sectors' fills kept through a save | A save and its load change nothing of the sectors' fills: every sector keeps through them the living creatures of its fills that went with it and the time it kept them, however long ago it left, the region that filled last, and its respawn's gate's last pass with the clock it counts on (`npcs::Kept`). The original's save writes a sector's list only where it holds a creature and the sector left less than `Respawn` (300 s) ago, the same bound for a dungeon's, and else empties the list and clears its time in the running game too (`0x623240`), so that the sector fills anew at its next coming with new rolls; no part of its save holds the region that filled last (`0x94E080`), so that a load in a new run empties, at its first fill, the lists of every sector of another region; and its load reads the sectors' information again, clearing the gate's times (docs/re/saves.md, "A creature killed far away"; docs/re/spawning.md, "The respawn's gate"). | A save is the play's record and should not change the play: in the original a save, made or not, decides whether a sector left more than five minutes before comes back with the creatures it kept or filled anew, which a player could farm; the game's sectors come back as the play left them, saved or not (the maintainer's choice of 2026-11-25). | better | #3750, #3786 |
