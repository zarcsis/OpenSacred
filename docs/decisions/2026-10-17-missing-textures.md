# Textures the game does not find

Models (textures), 2026-10-17.

## The original

A texture whose name is not in `texture.pak` is `IGNORED` by the game's binding (community-reported); what it then shows is not known.

## Ours

The file's embedded image (26 textures: 18 VTex, 8 one-colour raw RGBA placeholders of weapons).

## Why

Without it citizens, farmers and zombies would have no texture; hypothesis: Granny keeps its own decoded image.
