# The creatures' health, before items and skills

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#278); changed 2026-10-20 (#318).
Changed in part by [2026-10-20-bosses](2026-10-20-bosses.md), by which the bosses' and the champions' factors apply.

## The original

A creature's most health (`0x5658F0`, docs/re/fight.md, "Health") comes from its `Creature.pak` base and its level, with the values of the items it wears, raised by its skill 10, by the bosses' and champions' factors, by the level's factor of a creature not a hero and by the difficulty's; its level is the hero's held within its sector's levels, jittered for a fill's creature, a boss's or champion's taken when it first fights.

## Ours

The most health is the base's and the level's by the same formula, with the level's factor, the difficulty's `ProzHP` of bronze and the bosses' and champions' factors (#318); the items and the skill 10's part (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-skills.md) are left out, and the hero's level is 1 until it gains experience.
A creature of a script takes its sector's level without the fills' jitter (**hypothesis**: `CreateNPC` reads the sector's levels the same way, docs/re/spawning.md).
Without the balance's names of its rows (`opensacred-convert tables` before #277), the difficulty's factor is 1, bronze's own.

## Why

The worn items are not in the game, and the skill 10's part of the health is partly read (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-skills.md); the health is what the strikes needed first (#280, #282), and the original's own bronze is the difficulty the game plays.
