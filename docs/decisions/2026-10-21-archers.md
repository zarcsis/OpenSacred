# The archers shoot only the hero

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-21 (#391, #396); brought up to date 2026-10-23 (#692).
Replaces in part [2026-10-20-pursuit](2026-10-20-pursuit.md), where an archer came as close as the others.

## The original

A creature with a ranged weapon fights in the AI's state 8 (docs/re/fight.md, "At range: the AI's state 8", "The archers"): it keeps 300 to 800 from its target and shoots arrows, which hit the target or the first object near their way that is alive and hostile to the shooter, by a test of hostility between any two creatures (`0x548F60`).
An archer whose target stands within 1.5 times the reach and targets it puts its bow away in one case of two and fights in melee with a sword, a dagger, a bastard sword or a mace made at its level (`0x4D4B70`), most of them taking the bit `+0x2B8` `0x10` that later gives them a bow again (`0x4D4A80`); a creature of class 5 within 150 of its target goes to melee in one case of four, its bow put down at its place.
The shot's details follow its kind: several arrows for a multi-shot, special projectiles for the combat arts, arrows that a ghost lets through and that hit it again every 0.5 s, arrows that an effect turns aside, a sound by the weapon's type at a hit, and a point on the target's neck moved by up to 5 along each axis.

## Ours

The archers keep their band and shoot as the original's, and their arrows fly, end and hit as its arrows do; they go to melee when the hero attacks them from near, and take a bow again, by the original's rolls (docs/re/fight.md, "In `opensacred-game`").
But:

- An arrow hits only the hero and flies on through any other creature near its way, which the original's hits when it is hostile to the shooter: the creatures of hostile sides fight one another in melee since #630 (docs/decisions/2026-10-23-creatures-fight.md), and at range they wait for #670.
- The switch to melee and the bow-taking follow the original's (#396), with these differences.
  The hero targets an archer when the archer is its attack's target, the original's other conditions holding for every creature of the game (no companions, no `+0x1F0` of 12).
  A creature of class 5 is taken as of a `+0x25F` other than 10, a field the game does not keep.
  The bow that a creature of class 5 puts away is gone, where the original puts it at the creature's place and hands it on by the event `0x14` (**hypothesis**: dropped on the ground).
  The walk a creature has when it changes its weapons ends, where the original's goes on until its next decision.
  The two chances of an archer that decides not to shoot, as it decides and as it would back off, come in one frame, where the original's come in two.
  The new weapon's values are made by the game's dice, seeded by the creature's cell and the weapon's type.
  A creature that goes with its sector and comes back with it holds again what it was made with, without the bit, which the original keeps with its record (`0x4939F0`).
- A shot is one plain arrow, aimed at the hero's neck itself; the multi-shot, the special projectiles, the ghosts, the deflection and the hit's sound are left out, and a back-off at a walk walks facing its way, the meaning of `+0x200` bit `0x800` not being read.
- The chain of an archer's shots never breaks: the original resets it at an action other than standing, moving and shooting, which the game's archers do not take.
- A boss or a champion, which the game levels as it is made, backs off as the others do, where the original's mark of the levelling (`+0x1F4` bit `0x200`) keeps it from backing off.
- An archer whose model has no attack clip for the class of what it holds does not shoot (the original's fallback for the attack's slot is not read); the bandits, soldiers and others of the bows have theirs (slot 114).
- An arrow leaves from 80 over the shooter's feet when its model has no left hand (**hypothesis**: about a hand's height).

## Why

Which creatures are hostile to one another and what the other kinds of shots do are not read; the game keeps neither the items a creature drops nor the companions, nor a creature's state beyond its place and health when its sector goes; the plain shot at the hero is what the archers of the first sectors do, and follows the original's.
