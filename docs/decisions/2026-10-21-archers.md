# The archers shoot only the hero, and keep their bows

Fight (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-21 (#391).
Replaces in part [2026-10-20-pursuit](2026-10-20-pursuit.md), where an archer came as close as the others.

## The original

A creature with a ranged weapon fights in the AI's state 8 (docs/re/fight.md, "At range: the AI's state 8", "The archers"): it keeps 300 to 800 from its target and shoots arrows, which hit the target or the first object near their way that is alive and hostile to the shooter, by a test of hostility between any two creatures (`0x548F60`).
An archer whose target stands within 1.5 times the target's reach and targets it puts its bow away in one case of two and fights in melee with a sword, a dagger, a bastard sword or a mace made at its level (`0x4D4B70`), most of them taking the bit `+0x2B8` `0x10` that later gives them a bow again (`0x4D4A80`); a creature of class 5 within 150 of its target goes to melee in one case of four.
The shot's details follow its kind: several arrows for a multi-shot, special projectiles for the combat arts, arrows that a ghost lets through and that hit it again every 0.5 s, arrows that an effect turns aside, a sound by the weapon's type at a hit, and a point on the target's neck moved by up to 5 along each axis.

## Ours

The archers keep their band and shoot as the original's, and their arrows fly, end and hit as its arrows do (docs/re/fight.md, "In `opensacred-game`").
But:

- An arrow hits only the hero: the game's creatures are hostile to the hero alone, as the hero's look takes them (`+0x1F4` bits 0 and 1, docs/re/motions.md), so an arrow flies on through any other creature near its way, which in the original might be hostile to the shooter.
- An archer keeps its bow: it neither goes to melee when the hero attacks it from near (`0x4D4B70`) nor, of class 5, within 150, and no creature takes a bow by `+0x2B8` bit `0x10`, whose first setter is not found.
- A shot is one plain arrow, aimed at the hero's neck itself; the multi-shot, the special projectiles, the ghosts, the deflection and the hit's sound are left out, and a back-off at a walk walks facing its way, the meaning of `+0x200` bit `0x800` not being read.
- The chain of an archer's shots never breaks: the original resets it at an action other than standing, moving and shooting, which the game's archers do not take.
- A boss or a champion, which the game levels as it is made, backs off as the others do, where the original's mark of the levelling (`+0x1F4` bit `0x200`) keeps it from backing off.
- An archer whose model has no attack clip for the class of what it holds does not shoot (the original's fallback for the attack's slot is not read); the bandits, soldiers and others of the bows have theirs (slot 114).
- An arrow leaves from 80 over the shooter's feet when its model has no left hand (**hypothesis**: about a hand's height).

## Why

Which creatures are hostile to one another, which creatures take a bow and what the other kinds of shots do are not read, and the switch to melee needs the creatures' weapons changed in their hands, with their values and their clips; the plain shot at the hero is what the archers of the first sectors do, and follows the original's.
