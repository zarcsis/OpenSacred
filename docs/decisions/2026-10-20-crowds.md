# The crowded cells, simpler

AI (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-20 (#240).

## The original

An idle creature that shares its cell with another parts in one frame of six (docs/re/ai.md, "The peaceful creatures' behaviours"); a flyer or a big model counts the eight cells about it too; a creature with a partner, of some flags, or that a script holds does not part.
A creature that walks into the hero's way steps aside only while the hero's own AI state is 0.

## Ours

The chance to part is a rate in time, 5 a second, as one frame of six gives at 30 frames a second (docs/decisions/2026-10-20-animals.md).
Every creature counts its own cell only; only the tag `0x74` keeps one from parting; the hero's state is not asked.

## Why

The models' sizes, the partners, the scripts' hold and the hero's AI states are not read into the game yet, and without them a few creatures part or step aside where the original's would stand: the play hardly changes.
