# The HUD

Interface (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17; changed 2026-10-19 (#168).

## The original

The original lays its interface out in code from the `gui_main_*` atlases (docs/02 §11).

## Ours

No HUD of the hero: only the original's cursor, and the interface's scale with the window's height.
The upper left holds the debug overlay on F3 (docs/10 §5).

From 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-19 a minimal HUD of whole textures stood there: the portrait, a health bar and the gait.

## Why

The atlases' layout is not recovered; the interface is redesigned in M5 with the original's textures as skins.
The minimal HUD said nothing yet: the health bar was always full until the rules of M3, and the gait is the overlay's now.
