# The HUD

Interface (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-17; changed 2026-10-19 (#168) and 2026-10-21 (#442).

## The original

The original lays its interface out in code from the `gui_main_*` atlases (docs/02 §11).
It draws its 32 × 32 cursor at the screen's resolution, unscaled, and runs full screen at 800 × 600 to 1280 × 1024, where the monitor stretches the whole picture.

## Ours

No HUD of the hero: only the original's cursor, and the interface's scale with the window's height.
The upper left holds the debug overlay on F3 (docs/10 §5).

The cursor grows with the system's display scale in whole steps, rounded down: the original's 32 × 32 at 100 % to 199 %, 64 × 64 at 200 %, whatever the window's size and mode.

From 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-19 a minimal HUD of whole textures stood there: the portrait, a health bar and the gait.
Until 2026-10-21 the cursor grew with the window's height, in whole steps rounded to the nearest, computed once at the start: started borderless on a 2160-pixel-high display it was 96 × 96, and stayed so in a window.

## Why

The atlases' layout is not recovered; the interface is redesigned in M5 with the original's textures as skins.
The minimal HUD said nothing yet: the health bar was always full until the rules of M3, and the gait is the overlay's now.
A cursor that grows with the window's height looks coarse, its texels in blocks of three, and much larger than the system's own cursor; one that follows the display scale is as large as the system's, and whole steps keep its texels square.
