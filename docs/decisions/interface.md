# Interface

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Art damage's survival share as the fight takes it | The statistics window's selected art's line and its panel both raise its damage by the share that the fight takes, half the survival curve and set 28's `10·D` (`fight::survival_raise`), where the original's line halves the set's part with the curve and its panel takes it whole (`0x6A6550`, `0x6A82F0`). | The two told different numbers for one damage; the fight's is the one the hero deals (#1737's rule). | repair | #1545, #1761 |
| Art tooltip's 1011 value whole | The tooltip of the weapon art 1011 shows its value as a whole number, where the original passes a double to `%d` and prints the low 32 bits of its pattern, 0 for most values (`0x5893B0`). | The original's number is a defect of its format, which tells the player nothing; the line and its label are the original's. | repair | #1694 |
| Autosave off from the first test | `[gameplay] autosave` of `settings.toml` is off by default and every test of it reads the file (`saves`), where the original's first test in a `Settings.cfg` that lacks `AUTOSAVE` writes it 0 and answers on that once (`0x81B6F0`), so that a fresh installation's first quest exit autosaves and no later one does. | The one autosave is a quirk of writing the default, which no later play repeats, and off is the default that the original keeps. | deviation | #1989 |
| Bar, portrait and cursor scaled | The bar and the hero's portrait scale with the window's height, the bar centred along the bottom and the portrait at the right edge of a window wider than 4:3, and the cursor grows with the system's display scale in whole steps, where the original's screen is a fixed 4:3 and its cursor unscaled. | In a wide window the bar's centre and the portrait's corner are where the eye looks for them, and a cursor by the display scale is as large as the system's, its texels square. | deviation | #168, #442, #1087 |
| Bonus lines of what the code applies | An item's tooltip shows no line of the open wounds (857), which the code reads on the struck creature's own items, nor of the alchemy (868) where neither the item nor what the hero wears carries an effect that marks it for the getter (`fight::marks`), and shows the life leech (841) times the game's difficulty's factor (`Difficulty::leech`, 0.8 to 0.5 from silver), where the original's lines show each at its face (`0x5C4450`; docs/re/items.md, "The bonus effects that the code reads"). | The lines tell what the hero's items do (#1737's rule); the leech's share of the distance, which changes with each strike, no line can tell. | repair | #1746, #1759 |
| Class refusal as the slots refuse | An item's tooltip shows `UI_ITEM_CANTUSE` where the equipment's slots refuse the item to the hero's class (its classes' bits, the bit 6 and the weapons' kinds, `equipment::may_use`), where the original's names an item of one other class without refusing it and refuses one of no class's bit, which every class may use, a socket's upgrade among them (`0x5C5360`). | The line tells whether the hero may wear the item, as the grid's red cells and the slots do (#1737's rule). | repair | #1740 |
| Dialogue lines by Bevy's glyphs | The dialogue's window lays its text out with Bevy's unhinted glyphs of AntiquaSSK at the size whose long lines are the original's width, so that a line of narrow or wide glyphs may take a word more or less than the original's, where the original's GDI rounds each glyph to whole pixels. | The window's frame, measures and colours are the original's; matching GDI's hinted widths would take a rasteriser of its own, and the words and the window's height hardly change. | deviation | #1458 |
| Dialogue window holds back world clicks | While a dialogue's window shows, all of the hero's clicks on the world are held back, where the original's window takes only the clicks inside its rectangle. | A button's press must not walk the hero away and end its own talk. | deviation | #1028 |
| Experience lines of a frame together | The experience that the hero gains in one frame shows one line of the sum, under the lines over it. | The original's `addExperience` shows each gain's line; two gains of one frame are rare, and the game follows the points rather than each of their causes. | deviation | #1386 |
| Horse window of its own state | The horse window shows each stable's horse with its own trader's name and portrait, and its leave's text by its state, where the original links a trader with its last horse alone, so that its other horses show the title and the portrait of the window's last opening, and keeps the leave's text 1028 for the rest of the run once a state without a sheet showed it (`0x6BE6E0`, `0x6BE80C`; **seen**: the first horse of the stable at 3180,2788 under another stable's dealer). | Defects of the window's state, kept over from an opening to the next, which name a dealer of another stable to the player. | deviation | #2300 |
| Keys by their place on any layout | Every key of `controls.toml` is a key by its place on the keyboard, named as on a US layout, and keeps that place on any layout (`controls`), where the original takes the letters of its game's keys from `WM_CHAR` and so from the layout in force (docs/re/window.md, "The input"). | A key's place does not move with the layout, so that the defaults work as one on every keyboard, a Russian one's too, and a key the player binds is the key pressed; the maintainer's choice (#1980). | deviation | #1553, #1980 |
| Keys 1 to 5 choose no art slot | The keys 1 to 5 choose the weapon sets alone, every press, and the game has no `COMBINE_SLOTS`; the original's first press of one in an installation whose `Settings.cfg` lacks the setting chooses the art slot of its number too, then writes the setting 0, which every later press reads. | The one combined press is a quirk of the setting's default that no player keeps, and a setting that chooses both was of no use to the maintainer's play (the maintainer's choice, 2026-10-30). | deviation | #1529, #1597 |
| Menus' scale, keys, text and figures | The menus keep the original's picture of 1024 × 768 scaled to the window's height, black at its sides, and the keyboard chooses with the arrows, Enter and Esc. The choice of the hero draws its words with Bevy's unhinted text, thinner than the original's hinted GDI, its unlit figures dark, and the menus' water at the installation's detail. | The keyboard serves play without a mouse and the scripts, the original's light of the figures is not read, and the game has no setting of the water's detail. | deviation | #172, #178, #1118, #1119, #1127, #1130, #1221 |
| Options' 3D sound never offered | The options window hides its third sound quality, the 3D sound, and its line says that no 3D sound card is found, on every computer, as the original's on a computer whose sound finds no 3D provider (`0x692B60`); `[sound] quality` 2 is kept and ticks neither choice shown. | The game's sound has no DirectSound3D provider to place the samples in 3D (docs/re/sound.md), so the choice could not do what it says. | limitation | #2340 |
| Options' FSAA filter and data flow change nothing | The options window keeps and writes the FSAA filter (`[graphics] fsaa_filter`) and the multiplayer's data flow (`[network] speed`), but neither changes anything in the game, where the original's filter moves its client's ground quads' texture coordinates in by 1/256 at each edge (`0x81C870`) and its data flow sets the network's speed mode (`setNetSpeedMode`). | The game's ground is drawn by its own shaders, with no seams of the original's anti-aliasing to hide (**estimate**), and it has no multiplayer before M9; the window keeps the original's controls. | deviation | #2340 |
| Options' held sliders at 60 presses a second | A slider of the options window held under the left button is pressed again every sixtieth of a second of the real time, where the original's window presses it at every frame it draws (`0x716E00`). | The original's pace at its 60 frames a second, whatever the game's frame rate, so that a held "+" runs over the volumes in the same time on any display. | deviation | #2340 |
| Poison's source not saved | A save keeps an effect without the creature that gave it (`effects::Timed`'s `source`), so that a poison loaded ticks on with no creature credited for its strikes and takes a new poison of any creature as another's (`saves`, the version 6), where the original's save keeps its objects and the effect names its source by the object's id (`+0x08` of the entry's list), the same creature after a load. | The game's creatures are entities, whose ids name slots of the running world alone and are made anew at a load, the creatures of the fills among them without a kept index to find them by. | limitation | #2308 |
