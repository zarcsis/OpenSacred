# Interface

The interface over the world, the menus and the dialogues' window (docs/re/interface.md, docs/re/menus.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Art tooltip's 1011 value whole | The tooltip of the weapon art 1011 shows its value as a whole number, where the original passes a double to `%d` and prints the low 32 bits of its pattern, 0 for most values (`0x5893B0`). | The original's number is a defect of its format, which tells the player nothing; the line and its label are the original's. | repair | #1694 |
| Bar, portrait and cursor scaled | The bar and the hero's portrait scale with the window's height, the bar centred along the bottom and the portrait at the right edge of a window wider than 4:3, and the cursor grows with the system's display scale in whole steps, where the original's screen is a fixed 4:3 and its cursor unscaled. | In a wide window the bar's centre and the portrait's corner are where the eye looks for them, and a cursor by the display scale is as large as the system's, its texels square. | deviation | #168, #442, #1087 |
| Class refusal as the slots refuse | An item's tooltip shows `UI_ITEM_CANTUSE` where the equipment's slots refuse the item to the hero's class (its classes' bits, the bit 6 and the weapons' kinds, `equipment::may_use`), where the original's names an item of one other class without refusing it and refuses one of no class's bit, which every class may use, a socket's upgrade among them (`0x5C5360`). | The line tells whether the hero may wear the item, as the grid's red cells and the slots do (#1737's rule). | repair | #1740 |
| Dialogue window holds back world clicks | While a dialogue's window shows, all of the hero's clicks on the world are held back, where the original's window takes only the clicks inside its rectangle. | A button's press must not walk the hero away and end its own talk. | deviation | #1028 |
| Dialogue lines by Bevy's glyphs | The dialogue's window lays its text out with Bevy's unhinted glyphs of AntiquaSSK at the size whose long lines are the original's width, so that a line of narrow or wide glyphs may take a word more or less than the original's, where the original's GDI rounds each glyph to whole pixels. | The window's frame, measures and colours are the original's; matching GDI's hinted widths would take a rasteriser of its own, and the words and the window's height hardly change. | deviation | #1458 |
| Experience lines of a frame together | The experience that the hero gains in one frame shows one line of the sum, under the lines over it. | The original's `addExperience` shows each gain's line; two gains of one frame are rare, and the game follows the points rather than each of their causes. | deviation | #1386 |
| Keys 1 to 5 choose no art slot | The keys 1 to 5 choose the weapon sets alone, every press, and the game has no `COMBINE_SLOTS`; the original's first press of one in an installation whose `Settings.cfg` lacks the setting chooses the art slot of its number too, then writes the setting 0, which every later press reads. | The one combined press is a quirk of the setting's default that no player keeps, and a setting that chooses both was of no use to the maintainer's play (the maintainer's choice, 2026-10-30). | deviation | #1529, #1597 |
| Menus' scale, keys, text and figures | The menus keep the original's picture of 1024 × 768 scaled to the window's height, black at its sides, and the keyboard chooses with the arrows, Enter and Esc. The choice of the hero draws its words with Bevy's unhinted text, thinner than the original's hinted GDI, its unlit figures dark, and the menus' water at the installation's detail. | The keyboard serves play without a mouse and the scripts, the original's light of the figures is not read, and the game has no setting of the water's detail. | deviation | #172, #178, #1118, #1119, #1127, #1130, #1221 |
| Quest book shows every kept line | A quest's item in the book shows every line its entry keeps, ten at most (`quests::LINES`), where the original's shows nine of them, so that a tenth line was kept and never shown (`0x6B07E0`). | The book tells what the log keeps (#1737's rule). | repair | #1560, #1745 |
| Quest book tracks what the pin follows | A click on a side quest of 9000 to 9499 in the book does not track it, and such a quest, though newest, is not shown as tracked (`quests::pinned`), where the original's click tracks it and its icon says so while the compass's inner pin, which the markers' resolver gives no such quest, shows nothing (`0x49DAB0`, `0x4A5980`). | The tracked icon tells that the pin follows the quest (#1737's rule). | repair | #1560, #1745 |
| Resistances' rows after their factors | The statistics window's resistances' panel shows each kind's resistance after its factor (`+0xD6`), as its sum and the strike take them, where the original's rows show them before it (`0x6A8A00`), so that they do not add up to the sum above them. | The rows tell what the hero resists (#1737's rule). | repair | #1525, #1740 |
| Smithing's cost as a share of the price | The panel of the skill 33 shows its cost as the per cent of the smith's price that the Dwarf asks, `100·f` of the price's own factor `f = 100/(s + 79)` (`smith::dwarf_price_factor`), 125 % at the level 1, where the original prints `f` itself as a per cent, 1.25 % (`0x566070`). | The original's line printed a factor as a share; the price is the one the window charges (#1737's rule). | repair | #1715, #1740 |
| Underworld menu tile made opaque | `opensacred-convert textures` scales the alpha of `GUI_UW_OPTION12.TGA`, 195 to 255 over its body, by 255/195, held at 255, so that the menu's water does not show through its rocks. | A tile of a painted screen is not meant to be seen through (**community-reported**, with a community fix), and the play does not change. | repair | #352 |
| Yellow potions' hint by their fill | A yellow potion's hint tells what its cocktail fills (`combos::refilled`): `POTION_L_YELLOW_USE`, every combo, where the cocktail fills a combo's time, else `POTION_M_YELLOW_USE`, where the original names its size's, a quarter for the small one and a part for the medium one, though every cocktail of the balance fills every combo (docs/re/arts.md, `0x562470`). | The hint tells what drinking it does (#1737's rule). | repair | #1740 |
