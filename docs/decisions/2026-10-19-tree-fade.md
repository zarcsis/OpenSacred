# The trees' fade, per point

Rendering (`opensacred-game`), 2026-10-19.

## The original

A sprite of a type of bit `0x10` (the trees and walls of type 25, the floors of type 29) drawn after the hero fades about it: each corner of each of the sprite's pieces, blocks of up to 64 × 64 pixels, takes an alpha by its distance to the hero's point, from 50 out of 255 at the point to 255 at 190 pixels (`TransSize`, `TransValu`), and the rasterizer blends that alpha between the corners; the texels' alpha times it then goes through the sprites' alpha test (docs/re/drawing.md, "The trees about the heroes").
The point is the centre of the hero's bounds on the screen.

## Ours

The sprites' shader computes the same alpha at every point of the sprite from that point's distance, rather than at the pieces' corners: a sprite is one quad here (docs/05 §3, the sprites assembled from their pieces).
The hero's point is its feet's on the screen, 60 pixels higher, half its height (`statics::HERO_MIDDLE`): the bounds of its model are not taken.

## Why

The pieces' corners lie 64 pixels apart, a third of the fade's radius, so the original's alpha is the same function, drawn with straight lines between its values at the corners: per point it is smooth where the original's shows the pieces' edges, and needs no vertices beyond a sprite's four.
The model's bounds change with every pose; the middle of the figure stands where the bounds' centre stands for a hero upright.
