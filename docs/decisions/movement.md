# Movement

Walking and the way (docs/re/movement.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Riding hero steers its horse | The riding hero is the body that a click sends and that walks, and its horse is carried under it, where the original hands the engine's control (`+0x13C`) to the horse, whose moving action takes the clicks and carries the rider (`0x4F4770`, `0x5941A0`). The hero goes at its horse's speeds by its class's pace, by none of the cells a ridden horse may not step onto, and every creature stops it but while the horse tramples, as the horse's own walk would. | The play is the same: the speeds, the cells, what stops the walk and the camera on the pair follow the horse's; the hero keeps one body for its orders, its way and its saves, which the mount and the dismount would otherwise hand from one to the other. | deviation | #2299, #2401 |
| Walking the way by straight lines | The hero and the creatures head for the farthest cell of their way that they reach in a straight line, keep their point while the line to it stays clear, and run straight toward a target they cannot reach; their way is searched again as they step into the next cell of it, where the original's walker searches again as it passes that cell's centre, and their course is kept where it is still open (a straight one's line from the walker on clear, what is left of a way no longer than the way found again); the hero's step that it may not take searches its way again at once, where the original's hero in its straight mode steps onto a cell beside its line (`0x4FB7E0`); the goals (`+0x259`) are not followed. | The original walks its way from cell centre to cell centre, so that a way of as many steps found again changes nothing it shows; ours, heading for the farthest cell it sees, would turn between such ways. How the client's walk meets a step it may not take on a way searched and treats a target out of reach is not read. The ways searched again at all, for every walker, are the maintainer's choice (#2175). | deviation | #212, #216, #981, #989, #996, #2175 |
| Way never cuts a corner | The way never steps between two cells that touch at a corner, so that the hero goes round into a house where the original's sometimes stops at its door. | The original's stop leaves the hero outside a house it can walk into, by where it was clicked from; the maintainer's choice (#476). | deviation | #476 |
| The search's goal on the ground | The search over floor numbers ends at the floor of the level that the target's cell is at on the target's floor number, the ground's where no building there has that floor, where the original's ends at the target's floor byte (`0x4F8AF0`): a walk from a building's floor toward a target on the ground takes the building's exits. | With the floor byte, the hero of `annehmen2003` stepping onto the chapel's west exit on its way at the floor number 1 stands there, every way back to the floor number 1 closed by the exits, until the queue drops its walk (docs/re/movement.md, "The chapel's west exit"); the maintainer kept the floors' rules but not the stranding (#2013). | deviation | #1344, #2013 |
