# Movement

Walking and the way (docs/re/movement.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero opens the doors on its way | The hero's way passes the cells of a closed door that no barred trigger holds, and as its next step would enter one the hero stops, opens the door and goes on along its way once the cells are free, where the original's way never passes a closed door's cell (`0x4EBCA0`): a click behind a closed door finds no way, the hero stops at the wall at the end of its line of at most 14 cells (`0x4FB44A`), and nothing on its walk opens a door, which only a click on the door does (docs/re/movement.md, "A closed door on the way"). | The hero goes into the room it is sent to rather than against the wall before it; the maintainer's choice (#3832). | better | #3832 |
| Riding hero steers its horse | The riding hero is the body that a click sends and that walks, and its horse is carried under it, where the original hands the engine's control (`+0x13C`) to the horse, whose moving action takes the clicks and carries the rider (`0x4F4770`, `0x5941A0`). The hero goes at its horse's speeds by its class's pace, by none of the cells a ridden horse may not step onto, and every creature stops it but while the horse tramples, as the horse's own walk would. | The play is the same: the speeds, the cells, what stops the walk and the camera on the pair follow the horse's; the hero keeps one body for its orders, its way and its saves, which the mount and the dismount would otherwise hand from one to the other. | simpler | #2299, #2401 |
| The search's goal on the ground | The search over floor numbers ends at the floor of the level that the target's cell is at on the target's floor number, the ground's where no building there has that floor, where the original's ends at the target's floor byte (`0x4F8AF0`): a walk from a building's floor toward a target on the ground takes the building's exits. | With the floor byte, the hero of `annehmen2003` stepping onto the chapel's west exit on its way at the floor number 1 stands there, every way back to the floor number 1 closed by the exits, until the queue drops its walk (docs/re/movement.md, "The chapel's west exit"); the maintainer kept the floors' rules but not the stranding (#2013). | repair | #1344, #2013 |
| Walk ends at any step into a closed cell | An NPC's step into a cell it may not enter ends its walk whatever the step's direction (`npcs::walk`), where the original's ends it at a step along one axis and at a diagonal step toward the lower x and y, and at another diagonal step or a step of more than one cell puts the walker back at the corner of the last cell it could enter, truncated into the cell before it along both axes, holding it there with its walk going on until its way is searched again (docs/re/movement.md, "The walk's step into a closed cell"). | The original's difference comes of truncating the corner's place to a cell, which picks the cell below it along both axes whatever the step's direction: a slip, not a design; a creature held at a corner and one that stops there look alike. | repair | #3648 |
| Walking the way by straight lines | The hero and the creatures head for the farthest cell of their way that they reach in a straight line, keep their point while the line to it stays clear, and run straight toward a target they cannot reach; their way is searched again as they step into the next cell of it, where the original's walker searches again as it passes that cell's centre, and their course is kept where it is still open (a straight one's line from the walker on clear, what is left of a way no longer than the way found again); the hero's step that it may not take searches its way again at once, where the original's hero in its straight mode steps onto a cell beside its line (`0x4FB7E0`); a straight line counts as clear only where it is clear whole, where the original's walker sets out straight on a line longer than 5 cells once two thirds of it are clear (docs/re/movement.md, "The way"); the goals (`+0x259`) are not followed. | A walk by straight lines turns less and takes a shorter way round what stands in it than the original's walk from cell centre to cell centre, the maintainer's choice (#2863). The original's walk shows nothing of a way of as many steps found again; ours, heading for the farthest cell it sees, would turn between such ways, so it keeps its course. How the client's walk meets a step it may not take on a way searched and treats a target out of reach is not read. The ways searched again at all, for every walker, are the maintainer's choice (#2175). A line whose last third is closed would only be searched again on the way. | better | #212, #216, #981, #989, #996, #2175, #2863, #2950 |
| Way found anew at a load | A loaded game's hero walks on to the target and the level it was going to at the save, its way searched anew from where it stands as it lands, at the pace that Shift gives (`saves::Walk`), where the original's save keeps its way's points, the segment walked and its gait, and its load walks on along them (`+0x2ED` to `+0x399`, `+0x1F8`; docs/re/saves.md, "A creature's record"). | The search for the player's way runs again every half a second while it walks (docs/re/movement.md, "Searching again (`0x4F4770`)"), so a way found anew from the same place to the same target is the same; the gait is Shift's at every step in both. | simpler | #3742 |
| Way never cuts a corner | The way never steps between two cells that touch at a corner, so that the hero goes round into a house where the original's sometimes stops at its door. | The original's stop leaves the hero outside a house it can walk into, by where it was clicked from; the maintainer's choice (#476). | repair | #476 |
| Way searched by the fewest steps | The way is the one of the fewest steps, searched breadth first over the eight neighbours of a cell in a window of 64 × 64 cells, with no costs and no limit of entries (`opensacred_rules::ways::search`), where the original's search goes over the four side neighbours in a field of 55 × 55 cells, each delayed by a cost of 1 toward the target, 3 across and 6 away, turns an L of the way back into a diagonal, keeps 13 points and fails past the 1,024 entries of its lists (docs/re/movement.md, "The search (`0x4F8AF0`)"). | One search of the fewest steps serves: the walk heads by straight lines for the farthest cell of the way that it sees, so that the way's cells hardly show, the two ways were seen alike round an obstacle (#2014), and the game's search has no limit at which it gives up; the maintainer's choice (#2845). | simpler | #2014, #2845 |
