# Items

The drops and the items on the ground (docs/re/items.md).

| Decision | Ours, against the original | Why | Kind | Issues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A class's bonus for its own type alone | A bonus slot whose condition's bits 8 to 11 name a hero's type counts for that type alone (`opensacred_rules::fight::Bonus::admits`), the magic bonus's slots of the Dwarf (8) and the Daemon (9) among them, where the original's `0x568B30`, whose switch has cases for the types 1 to 6 alone, admits every holder for the values 8 to 15, so that those slots count for every hero (docs/re/items.md, "The bonus slots in the values (`0x5796A0`)"). | A slot written for the Dwarf or the Daemon counting for every class reads as a slip of the switch, which the types 7 to 9 outgrew; the maintainer's choice (#3486). | repair | #3417, #3486 |
| A drop's flight out of the loaded sectors | A drop still in flight whose place leaves the loaded sectors flies on over the height 0, where it then lies. | The game has no ground there; whether the original's objects of a sector out of its 3 × 3 fly on is not read. The drops lie until they are taken, as the original's (docs/re/items.md, "On the ground", #2871). | limitation | #415, #2871 |
| Containers emptied by their placements | A container that the hero emptied is placed again emptied and open by its placement (`npcs::Npcs::emptied`), its opening clip ended within a frame (`containers::clips`), where the original's sector's `Enter` makes each container it kept again by its trigger's state: shut where the state word is 0, else emptied and open, its clip of the slot 170 moved to its last tenth of a second (`0x494919`, `0x450B70`; docs/re/fight.md, "Doors in the way"). | The two agree for every container that the hero opens, the lid's last tenth of a second aside. They would differ for a container whose trigger a script alone opened or barred before its sector comes in, which the original's test would show emptied and open: a case that no tree was searched for, that was not seen, and that reads as the test's side effect rather than a rule. | `simpler` | #1380, #3077 |
| Drops rolled by the game's generator | The drops' rolls come from the game's own generator rather than the C library's `rand()`, so that a death drops what the original's might, not what it would. | The C library's `rand()` is one sequence that every draw of the client shares, an order that the game does not reproduce. | simpler | #414 |
| Drops' class choice drawn evenly | With a class asked, a drop's type is drawn evenly among the candidates of the class's bit or of none (`opensacred_rules::drops::Catalogue::choose`), where the original keeps their indices as bytes, so that past 256 candidates an index wraps and some types come oftener than others, as the weapons', the armours' and the rings' lists pass 256 (docs/re/items.md, "A pass: what it makes"). | The wrap is an overflow of the original's list, whose meaning is the even draw; the maintainer's choice (#2950). | repair | #2950 |
| Emptied sockets priced anew | An item whose sockets the smith empties is priced by its own values again, as an item that never had them. | The original takes every socket's item's values off the item (`0x5C0430`) but leaves its price (`+0x08`) at the worth that the last filling gave it with them (`0x5C0810`), so that the empty item sells as dear as the filled one; the game prices an item by its values whenever it asks, and keeps no stale price. | repair | #1661 |
